## Format notes
- Voice is governed by `VOICE.md` (hard rules: no em dashes, no "and"-starts, short and punchy).
- Substack: title + subtitle pair (subtitle is the hook and doubles as the social-preview line). Newsletter essay with light scannable subheads. No LinkedIn scaffolding (no hashtags, no "link in comments", no separate launch post). Close with a reply/subscribe CTA, not a hard sell. Reuse a stable post-tag set so tag archives accumulate; starter set: AI Adoption, Engineering Leadership, AI. When repurposing an essay out to LinkedIn, re-hook the first two lines for the feed.
- Substack SEO fields (record in note frontmatter as `seo_title`, `seo_description`, `slug`): SEO title keyword-forward and under ~60 chars (the on-page headline can be longer/punchier); SEO description ~150-160 chars, lead with the searchable setup then deliver the payoff so the snippet earns the click; slug 3-5 hyphenated keyword words, hand-set. Lock the slug before publishing (Substack does not redirect a changed slug and it breaks the sent-email link).
- LinkedIn: strong first two lines, short lines, no hard sell.
- Blog: scannable subheads, practical, CTA at the end.
- Webinar: teach first, pitch last.

## Hard nos
- No hype-cycle over-promising.
- No AI slop / generic buzzwords.
