### Read
- Standing-start volume: 17 visitors, 1 subscriber, $0 for the week.
- Direct is 76% of traffic (13 of 17). On a young newsletter that is a discovery problem, not a strength: it means existing contacts and the Substack app, not new readers found through channels.
- LinkedIn is the only converting source: 2 visitors, 1 subscriber (the 8/5 sub). Lowest volume, highest quality.
- Substack's own network sent 0. Recommendations / cross-Substack discovery is not running yet.
- Search and email are negligible, expected this early.

### The leak (cross-reference with LinkedIn Analytics)
LinkedIn served ~2,816 impressions last week but sent only 2 visitors to Substack. Largest audience and only converting channel are the same channel, and almost none of that reach is routed to the newsletter. The bottleneck is the LinkedIn-to-Substack handoff, not the writing or the Substack setup.

### Takeaways carried into next week's plan
- Route LinkedIn reach to Substack deliberately: a soft, specific CTA or first-comment link on the best-performing posts (the 95%-pilots type), pointed at the matching Substack essay.
- Turn on Substack Recommendations to open the discovery engine that is currently at 0.
- Do not read into subs/revenue yet. One sub on 17 visitors is noise. Watch the trend as volume grows.
- Caveat: single-digit counts, directional only.
