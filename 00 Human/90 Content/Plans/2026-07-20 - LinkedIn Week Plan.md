**Angle detail per day:**
- **Tue (Authority / Saves):** Break down the real line-item cost of adopting AI badly (rework, trust erosion, shadow tooling) vs. done right. Ties to "AI slop vs. quality" pillar. Odd-precision numbers make it save-worthy.
- **Wed (Narrative / Likes):** A vulnerability post. A specific pilot that underdelivered, the moment you saw it, what you changed. Ties to "adopting AI well: where the hype fails."
- **Thu (Authority / Comments):** Strongest opinion, "Agile needs updating for the gen-AI age." Contrarian by design to pull comments. Land a soft offer at the end (week's CTA-driving post): "This is most of what we untangle for teams at Dual Logic."
- **Fri (Community / Reposts):** Name and tag 3 real engineering leaders whose thinking shaped yours. Generous, no ask. Tagged people often repost.

## Daily comment targets (10-20 substantive comments/day)

**Tue** — data-first pattern (add a number or a counter-datapoint):
- Peer CTOs/VPs Eng at 5-20k followers
- 1-2 AI-infra founders posting adoption metrics
- 1 BigCo eng leader whose post you can extend with a receipt

**Wed** — first-commenter + answer-their-question pattern:
- Peer founders/operators sharing pilot post-mortems

**Thu** — contrarian-but-kind pattern (budget time to reply to inbound on your own post):

- 1-2 prospects (heads of eng at target-size cos)

**Fri** — genuine-praise pattern:
- The 3 people you tagged (reply to their reposts fast, 6-24h warm window)
- Their commenters (expand reach into adjacent networks)

**Comment split target:** ~70% peers · 20% aspirational · 10% prospects.
