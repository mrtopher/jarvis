## Angle detail per day
- **Mon (Leadership / Comments):** Chris's belief that you can't blindly swap workers for agents. Concrete: what actually falls apart (judgment, context, accountability) when you cut the humans. Written to the CEO weighing headcount vs "just use AI."
- **Tue (Authority / Saves):** The real cost of AI slop shipped fast: rework, trust erosion, the cleanup bill. Experiential, no invented dollar figures. Save-worthy because it names the hidden line items a CEO doesn't see coming.
- **Wed (Narrative / Likes):** Vulnerability. A rollout Chris led, the moment he saw it wasn't landing, what he changed. Ties adoption-done-well to leadership.
- **Thu (Authority / Comments):** Strongest opinion, "Agile needs updating for the gen-AI age." Contrarian by design. Land the week's one soft offer at the end: "This is most of what we untangle for teams at Dual Logic."
- **Fri (Community / Reposts):** Address the CEO directly on the "AI makes engineers stronger, not optional" pillar. Generous to the engineers, useful to the exec. No ask beyond a reflection prompt.

## Daily comment targets (10-20 substantive comments/day)
Aim reach up: spend more time in CXO/VP/founder threads to correct last week's IC-skewed distribution.
- **Split target:** ~60% peers (CTOs/VPs Eng) · 25% aspirational (well-known eng/AI leaders) · 15% prospects (CEOs/heads of eng at target-size cos).
- **Mon:** warm the network before the week's posts. First-commenter on peer + prospect posts.
- **Tue:** data/receipt pattern. Add a counter-datapoint on posts about AI ROI or failed rollouts.
- **Wed:** answer-their-question pattern on founder/operator post-mortems.
- **Thu:** contrarian-but-kind on agile/delivery threads. Budget time to reply to inbound on your own post.
- **Fri:** genuine-praise pattern. Reply fast to reposts (6-24h warm window).
