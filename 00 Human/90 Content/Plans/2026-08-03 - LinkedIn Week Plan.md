## Angle detail per day
- **Mon (Adopting AI well / Saves):** Citi reports 10,000+ engineers on AI daily and ~100k eng hours/week freed, but the real driver is a 4,000-person internal enablement network. Adoption is a people/training problem, not a license purchase. Teases Tue.
- **Tue (Hype fails / Saves+comments):** MIT's 95%-zero-return finding (stated with its own methodological caveat), corroborated by PwC 2026 (56% of CEOs see no gain) and Gartner (30% of GenAI scrapped by end 2026). The failure is choices, not the model: use case, success criteria, gradeability.
- **Wed (Engineers stronger / Comments):** METR's randomized trial. Devs predicted +24%, felt +20%, measured -19%, mostly from verifying unreliable output. Both honest caveats stated. Lesson: measure the outcome, not the vibe.
- **Thu (Leadership / Comments+DMs):** Chris's own read of AI-era engineering-leadership JDs: they still screen for building software, the part AI made cheap. Hire for judgment. Soft, dual-purpose CTA (consulting + job search), reaches the CXO/hiring layer the reach is missing.
- **Fri (Agents x leadership / Comments):** Deloitte says ~74% plan agentic AI within two years, few have governance. Citi's Arc launch shows the serious version: controls and a named owner before autonomy. Accountability angle, not "agents misbehave."
