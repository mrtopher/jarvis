## Body
I rolled out an AI tool to a team and told them to go faster.

Three weeks in, half of them had quietly stopped using it, and I understood why.

I had led with the tool. Buy it, learn it, hit the number. I skipped the part where people decide whether a new thing is going to make their day better or just make them more measurable.

The engineers who dropped it had no problem with AI. They had a problem with a stopwatch wearing a friendly logo.

So I backed up. We started over with the boring questions. What is actually slow for you right now? Where do you lose an afternoon to something dumb? Then we pointed the tool at that.

Adoption came once the tool earned its place, no mandate required.

The lesson stuck. A rollout is a change in how people work. People opt into that, or they wait you out.

## Takeaway / CTA
If you are rolling out AI this quarter, resist leading with the tool. Start with the afternoon your best person keeps losing. What is it? That is where adoption actually begins.
