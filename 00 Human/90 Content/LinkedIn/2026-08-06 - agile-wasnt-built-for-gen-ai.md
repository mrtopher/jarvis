## Body
Agile was written for a world where the slow part was typing the code.

That world is gone, and most teams are still running its rituals.

Story points estimate effort. When an agent can draft a feature in an hour, what exactly are you pointing? The typing is cheap now. The thinking is not.

The bottleneck moved. It used to be building the thing. Now it is deciding the thing is right, safe, and worth shipping. Review, judgment, and taste became the constraint.

So the ceremonies drift out of sync. Standups track output that arrives faster than the meeting. Sprints measure velocity on the one part that got easy. Meanwhile the hard part, verifying that fast output is good output, has no column on the board.

Keep agile. Update what it optimizes for. Point it at judgment instead of typing. Make review a first-class stage, not a rushed afterthought. Reward the person who caught the confident mistake more than the one who generated the most.

## Takeaway / CTA
Look at your board this week. How much of it measures output, and how much measures whether the output was any good? If it is all the first thing, your process is optimizing the part that is already solved.

Untangling this for teams is most of what we do at Dual Logic. If your delivery process still assumes typing is the bottleneck, that is worth a conversation.
