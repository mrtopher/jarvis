## Body
Not every task is worth handing to an AI agent.

Here is the 30-second test I run before I automate anything in a browser.

Three questions. A task needs a yes to all three.

1. Is it repetitive and high-volume?
If your team does it once a quarter, automate your calendar instead. The payoff lives in the task someone does forty times a week.

2. Do the rules fit on an index card?
"Pull the invoice total and paste it into row two" is a rule. "Use your best judgment on which vendor to trust" is not. Clear rules in, reliable agent out. Fuzzy rules in, confident nonsense out.

3. Is a wrong move recoverable?
Copying data into a draft is recoverable. Wiring money or emailing a customer is not. Start where a mistake costs you a redo, not a refund.

Three yeses, you have a strong candidate. One no, keep a human on it for now.

That is it. No committee. No six-month platform evaluation.

I'm running this test live next week against real tasks people bring. You watch an agent take a real one end to end, stumble once, and recover.
