## Body

Nearly half of Google searches now answer the question before you click anything.

TechCrunch says AI Overviews show up in 43% of them, and the number is climbing fast. The headline this week called it the new default.

For twenty years the game was simple. Rank on page one, earn the click, win attention on your own site. You spent real money to climb that page.

The click is going away. Someone searches, Google writes the answer at the top, the person gets what they came for, no website visit required. Your page still ranks. Fewer people ever see it.

This is bigger than an SEO tweak. It changes where your customers meet your brand. They meet it inside Google's answer now, or they do not meet it at all.

So the question shifts. Ranking still matters. What matters more is whether Google's AI cites you when it answers the questions your customers ask.

That rewards clear, factual, well-structured content an AI can quote with confidence. It punishes thin pages built to game a ranking.

Three things worth doing this quarter if you run a company.

Check what share of your traffic comes from search, and watch whether it is sliding.

Search the questions your customers actually ask, and see whether the AI answer names you or a competitor.

Write for the answer, not just the ranking. Facts, specifics, language a machine can trust.

Ranking on page one used to win you the customer. Now the win is getting quoted in the answer they read instead.

## Takeaway / CTA
Pull up Google and search one question your best customer would ask. Does the AI answer mention you? Tell me what you find.
