# LinkedIn - What hiring managers get wrong about AI-era eng leaders

## Angle

## Body
I have read a lot of engineering leadership job descriptions lately.

Most are hiring for 2019.

They lead with years in a language, team size managed, systems scaled. Real signals, all aimed at a world where the hard part was building the software.

That part moved. When a team can generate a week of code in a day, a leader's value is in judgment: knowing what not to build, catching the confident wrong output before a customer sees it, deciding which of ten plausible AI answers you would bet the quarter on.

None of that fits a keyword filter. So the filters miss it.

Here is what I would screen for instead. How does this person decide? Show me a call they made with bad options and how they reasoned through it. How do they keep a team sharp when the tool does the easy part? What do they refuse to ship, and why?

The best engineering leaders I know right now ask the sharpest questions and hold the line on quality while everyone around them chases speed.

## Takeaway / CTA
If you are hiring an engineering leader this year, look past the stack and the headcount. Hire the judgment. If you are working on that hire, I am always up for the conversation.
