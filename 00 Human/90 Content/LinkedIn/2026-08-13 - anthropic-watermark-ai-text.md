# LinkedIn - Anthropic's Claude watermark won't grade the work

## Angle
> Anthropic is watermarking Claude's text. Leaders will treat it as an AI-slop detector. It marks origin, not quality, it strips easily, and even a true hit says nothing about whether the work is good.

## Hook (first 2 lines)

## Body
Anthropic is about to put an invisible watermark on everything Claude writes. A lot of leaders will treat this as the tool that finally catches AI writing at work. It will not.

New Claude models will carry a hidden mark inside the text, applied worldwide to meet the EU's new transparency rules. A detection tool is coming with it.

The mark is easy to lose. Anthropic itself lists what defeats it: paraphrasing, translation, heavy editing, or blending the text into your own words. Run the output through one more tool and the signal is gone. The people cutting corners are the first ones who will strip it.

Say it survives and the detector lights up anyway. All that proves is the words passed through Claude. It does not prove the work is wrong, lazy, or even AI at all. Proofread your own memo with Claude and it gets marked too.

A watermark can tell you a machine touched the text. It cannot tell you whether the work is any good. Those are two different problems, and only one of them pays your bills.

The one that pays is quality, and no detector will grade it for you. Someone has to read the work and decide if it holds up.

## Takeaway / CTA
Provenance is worth having. Do not ask it to do a human's job. If your whole answer to AI slop is a detector, you do not have an answer. Who on your team actually reads the output and owns whether it is good?
