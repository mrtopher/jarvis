# LinkedIn - Can't fire the team and hand the work to agents

## Angle
> Replacing people with agents doesn't remove the work. It removes the judgment that made the work safe to ship.

## Hook (first 2 lines)
A CEO told me last month he wanted to run his engineering org on agents and keep three people.
I asked him who was going to catch it when the agents were confidently wrong.

## Body
A CEO told me last month he wanted to run his engineering org on agents and keep three people.

I asked him who was going to catch it when the agents were confidently wrong.

He didn't have an answer, because the answer is the team he wanted to cut.

An agent misses what makes the work safe. It does not know why you built the thing the weird way three years ago. It never sees the customer's face when the feature breaks. It carries no accountability either, because you cannot fire software.

Agents are fast. Speed was never the bottleneck. Judgment was.

When you pull the people out, the work does not leave. It moves to the three you kept, plus a new job nobody scoped: reviewing everything a tireless machine produces at machine speed. That is a harder job than the one you cut.

The teams getting real value from agents did not shrink to three. They kept their people and pointed them at better problems.

## Takeaway / CTA
Before you count the seats you can cut, ask a smaller question. When your agents are confidently wrong, who notices? If the answer is nobody, you are removing the brakes and calling it automation.
