# LinkedIn - AI doesn't make your engineers optional

## Angle
> The CEO who thinks AI makes engineers optional is about to lose the exact people who make AI pay off.

## Hook (first 2 lines)
To the CEO quietly wondering if AI means you need fewer engineers:
you are about to lose the people who were going to make AI worth it.

## Body
To the CEO quietly wondering if AI means you need fewer engineers:

you are about to lose the people who were going to make AI worth it.

I get why the math looks tempting. The tools are strong. The demos are fast. The pitch decks promise a smaller team.

The deck leaves out the part that matters. A good engineer with AI knows which of the ten answers will not page you at 2am. That judgment is the whole game now, and it lives in your people, not your tools.

Cut them, and you keep the tool and lose the taste. You end up shipping more, understanding less, and trusting none of it.

Your strongest engineers already feel the ground moving. The good ones are excited to become far more capable. The signal you send this year decides whether they do that with you or somewhere else.

Tell them the truth. AI makes them stronger. You are handing them a bigger lever and expecting more of what only they can do.

## Takeaway / CTA
If you lead a team, ask yourself one thing this weekend. Do your best engineers believe AI is a lever you are handing them, or a countdown you started? Their answer is already shaping who stays.
