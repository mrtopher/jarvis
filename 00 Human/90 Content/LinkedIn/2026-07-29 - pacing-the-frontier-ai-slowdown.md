# LinkedIn - Pacing the Frontier

## Angle
> The people building the most advanced AI are asking for brakes. For a CEO, that is a signal about tempo, not a reason to panic: adopt fast where you can check the work, slow down where you cannot.

## Body
1,273 people who build the world's most advanced AI just asked the U.S. government to help them slow it down.

These are engineers and executives at OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, and Meta. Dario Amodei signed it. So did OpenAI's chief scientist.

Their statement is called "Pacing the Frontier." It says these companies may be close to automating AI research itself, and the world has no reliable way to pace that progress safely.

The people closest to the work are the ones raising a hand.

For a business owner, this is about tempo. No cause for alarm, but the pace is real enough that the builders themselves want guardrails. That same pace is why adopting AI cannot mean handing an agent the keys and walking away.

Move fast where you understand the output. Go slower where you cannot yet check the work, and keep a person on anything that touches customers, money, or trust.

The builders are asking for time to get it right. Give your own business the same.

## Takeaway / CTA
What would you need to see before you trusted an AI system to run without you watching?
