## Body
The demo shipped in a weekend. Everyone clapped.

The bill for that weekend showed up two months later, and it was ugly.

AI can produce a working-looking feature faster than any team you have ever run. That is real. What it does not do is tell you the part that quietly costs you.

Here are the line items nobody reads out at the launch.

Rework. Code that looks right and is subtly wrong takes longer to fix than code that was never written, because first you have to figure out what it was trying to do.

Trust. The first time a customer hits the broken seam, they stop believing the polished parts too.

Onboarding. Nobody can explain the system, because nobody wrote it. They prompted it.

Review debt. Every fast thing you shipped is now a slow thing someone has to understand before they can change it.

Speed at the front is a loan. You pay it back with interest in the quarter you were not looking.

## Takeaway / CTA
Save this before your next "we shipped it in a weekend" moment. Then ask: can someone on your team explain how it works? If not, you shipped a future outage with a launch party.
