### Takeaways carried into next week's plan
- The 8/4 "95% of AI pilots return nothing" post is the resonance winner (4.7%, buyer framing) and shares its thesis with the 8/11 Substack essay. Follow up with a real client before/after.
- Do not chase the 8/7 spike. Big reach, 0.5% rate, pulled IC engineers at 10k+ companies. Use engineer-bait tags (aiengineering, continuouslearning) sparingly; they reach the wrong audience for the job search and consulting.
- Opinionated news-reactions (Citi, METR, AI-layoff market) reliably carry engagement and reach leaders. Keep them central.
- Match hashtags to intent: leadership · aiadoption · aistrategy pull buyers; aiengineering · continuouslearning pull ICs.
- Post Mon-Fri. Weekend reach is near zero again (Sun 8/9 = 21 / 0).
- Caveat: single- and double-digit engagement counts. One noisy week, read as directional.
