EstateSpace is looking for a CTO for their next phase of growth.

About EstateSpace, Inc.
EstateSpace is private asset management software for family offices, estate management firms, and financial advisory practices. It gives operations teams one system of record to replace scattered spreadsheets and tools. Our AI assistant, Lily, handles setup, organization, and retrieval so teams can manage by exception instead of chasing updates.

Description

About the Opportunity

EstateSpace gives family offices, estate management firms, and financial advisory practices one shared, verified record for managing real estate, art, vehicles, vessels, aircraft, and the vendors and documents tied to them, instead of scattered spreadsheets. We're entering our next phase of growth, and we're looking for a CTO to lead our technology and engineering organization - scaling the product, evolving the platform, and building the team and systems to move fast as we grow.

The Role

You'll own technology end to end: architecture, infrastructure, security, and AI strategy. You report to the CEO and work closely with the CRO, CSSO, and VP of AI & Data Science. You'll set the technical direction, stay hands-on writing and reviewing code as needed and build the senior team around you.

Key Responsibilities

• Own the technology strategy and architecture, from design through delivery.

• Evolve the platform and deliver the product roadmap on an ambitious timeline.

• Design an AI-native architecture that lets a lean team ship continuously.

• Establish modern engineering practices - CI/CD, automated testing, monitoring, and incident response.

• Partner with the CSSO to keep the platform secure and compliant.

• Set technical strategy and turn it into a roadmap the team can execute.

• Travel periodically to our HQ and other locations for offsites and leadership meetings.

Required Qualifications

• 15+ years in platform and product engineering, including hands-on ownership of architecture, services, and delivery for a multi-tenant SaaS platform at meaningful scale.

• 5+ years in a technical leadership role, managing distributed engineering teams.

• Led a comparable platform modernization end to end, including CI/CD buildout, automated testing and deployment.

• Expertise in cloud infrastructure (AWS or comparable), modern frontend/backend frameworks, and mobile delivery.

• Security-first approach, with experience keeping a platform compliant through major technical change.

• History of establishing AI-forward development and AI-native workflows across teams.

Preferred Experience

• Wealth management, real estate, or another high-trust, high-touch enterprise domain.

• Degree in computer science, engineering, or a related field, or equivalent hands-on experience.

Benefits

• Base salary: $300,000–$350,000, based on experience.

• Meaningful equity.

• Performance-based bonus tied to individual and company goals.

• Medical, dental, and vision; 401(k); disability and life insurance; unlimited PTO.

• We are not able to sponsor employment visas at this time
