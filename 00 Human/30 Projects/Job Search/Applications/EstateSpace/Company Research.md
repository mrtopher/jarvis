# Company Research - EstateSpace, Inc.

## Snapshot
- **What they do:** Private asset management SaaS. One verified system of record for family offices, estate management firms, and financial advisory practices to manage real estate, art, vehicles, vessels, aircraft, plus the vendors and documents tied to them. Replaces scattered spreadsheets and tools. Ships an AI assistant named "Lily" for setup, organization, and retrieval so teams "manage by exception."
- **Size / stage:** Seed stage. ~19 employees (2026). Patented platform, launched 2019.
- **Funding / ownership:** ~$4.3M raised across 2 rounds. Last was a ~$3.3M Seed (2022) at a ~$17M valuation. Founder-led, not a large institutional cap table.
- **HQ / locations:** US-based, exact HQ city unconfirmed in public sources. The role requires periodic travel to HQ and other locations, so there is a physical HQ to verify (confirm city + travel cadence).
- **Remote policy:** Reads as remote or remote-friendly with periodic HQ/offsite travel. No visa sponsorship available.

## Products & business model
> B2B SaaS sold to family offices, estate management firms, and financial advisory practices serving ultra-high-net-worth (UHNW) clients. Vertical system of record for high-value physical assets and the operations around them. Business model is subscription SaaS. ~65 customers reported. Revenue growth is steep: ~$960K (2024) to ~$3.9M ARR (2025). Small base, fast trajectory, classic early-growth SaaS.

## Tech stack
> Not fully public. The JD signals a multi-tenant SaaS platform on AWS (or comparable), modern frontend/backend frameworks, and mobile delivery. AI-native direction with an existing AI assistant (Lily) and a VP of AI & Data Science on the leadership team. Marketing site is WordPress/Elementor (marketing only, not the product).

## Culture & values
> Founder-driven, mission framed around bringing order to high-trust, high-touch UHNW estate operations. Jonathan Fishbeck came from luxury design-build construction and owner's-rep work for UHNW families, so the culture is grounded in that white-glove service world. Lean team, hands-on, fast-shipping expectation. Security and discretion matter because the customer data is wealthy families' private asset records.

## Reputation
> Too small for meaningful Glassdoor signal. Founder has a visible, positive public profile (podcasts, founder interviews, philanthropy as chair of Fisher House Foundation). No obvious public red flags surfaced.

## Recent news
- Steep revenue ramp 2024 to 2025 (~4x) - the trigger for this "next phase of growth" CTO hire.
- Presence at Domestic Estate Management Association (DEMA) events; active LinkedIn brand ("oneplatformforabuiltworld").

## Green flags
- Comp is strong and disclosed: $300K-$350K base + meaningful equity + performance bonus. That sits ABOVE Chris's ~$250-300K target.
- Title and mandate are a clean match: own tech end to end, report to CEO, hands-on player-coach, build the senior team.
- AI-native mandate is Chris's core identity and thesis (AI makes lean teams ship more).
- Real, growing revenue and paying customers (not pre-revenue).

## Red flags
- Seed stage, ~19 people, ~$3.9M ARR. A $300K-$350K base is generous for a company this size, so probe runway, burn, and next-round plans. Cash risk is the headline diligence item.
- An existing "Chief Technology & Product Officer" title has appeared in prior public data. The JD hires a net-new CTO reporting to the CEO. Clarify what happened to the prior tech leader and why the seat is open (departure, elevation, reorg).
- Travel-to-HQ requirement with unconfirmed HQ location. Confirm the city and cadence against Chris's remote preference.
- UHNW / wealth-management / real-estate domain is new for Chris (preferred, not required).
