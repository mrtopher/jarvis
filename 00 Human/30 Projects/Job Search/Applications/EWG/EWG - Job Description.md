# Vice President, Engineering - EWG

> Source: https://ewg.applytojob.com/apply/ZUGzfeLFKn/Vice-President-Engineering

**Organization:** Environmental Working Group (EWG)

**Role Title:** Vice President, Engineering

**Location:** Washington, DC (preferred, hybrid); remote considered for exceptional candidates

**Employment Type:** Full Time

**Seniority Level:** Executive / VP-level

**Salary Range:** $180,000–$300,000 annually (commensurate with experience)

## About the Company

The Environmental Working Group (EWG) is a nonprofit, nonpartisan organization dedicated to empowering people to live healthier lives in a healthier environment. The organization drives impact through scientific research, consumer tools, and advocacy. Core digital platforms, including Skin Deep®, Food Scores®, and the Guide to Healthy Cleaning, are powered by large-scale datasets and play a critical role in the mission.

## Key Responsibilities

- Lead modernization of the core technology stack, platforms, and data systems; assess architecture and define a phased roadmap
- Reduce dependence on manual, spreadsheet-based workflows by implementing scalable systems and automation
- Define and own the technical roadmap, translating organizational goals into prioritized, measurable milestones
- Design and improve data ingestion, validation, transformation, and processing pipelines
- Establish engineering standards: testing, QA/QC, validation, release processes, and documentation
- Improve planning, estimation, prioritization, and execution discipline across teams
- Evaluate and integrate AI-powered tools for consumer-facing products (search, recommendations, personalized guidance)
- Identify practical opportunities to apply AI to data processing, classification, workflows, and product capabilities
- Oversee integrity, governance, reliability, and scalability of data infrastructure
- Evaluate build-vs-buy decisions and platform/tool investments
- Partner with the Chief Experience Officer to align engineering with product and organizational goals
- Foster a culture of accountability, quality, and continuous improvement
- Remain hands-on as a technical contributor: write, review, and debug code alongside the team
- Directly manage the development team: hiring, mentoring, performance management, and team leadership
- Communicate strategy, priorities, risks, and progress to leadership, board, funders, and external stakeholders

## Requirements

- 15+ years in engineering, platform, or technology leadership
- Experience leading organizations through systems modernization or transformation
- Experience applying AI, machine learning, or intelligent automation to products or operations
- Strong background in data platforms, APIs, cloud architecture, and scalable systems design
- Demonstrated ability as a hands-on technical contributor (writing, reviewing production code) while managing teams
- Experience implementing automation and workflow systems for operational efficiency
- Track record building and leading high-performing technical teams
- Strong cross-functional leadership with technical and non-technical stakeholders
- Excellent judgment, communication, and decision-making skills
- Ability to translate strategy into execution in complex, evolving environments

## Success Metrics (First 12–18 Months)

- Modernization roadmap in place with measurable progress
- Engineering team operating with stronger delivery practices and transparency
- Data quality and governance processes matured
- AI and automation initiatives delivering efficiency gains
- Platforms and products more scalable, accurate, and mission-aligned

## Total Rewards

- Fully paid health care
- Paid parental leave
- Tax-deferred 403(b) plan with matched contributions
- Casual, collaborative, mission-driven environment
