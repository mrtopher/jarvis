# Vice President, Engineering - EWG

> Source: https://ewg.applytojob.com/apply/ZUGzfeLFKn/Vice-President-Engineering
> Fetched: 2026-07-07
> Archived verbatim. Do not edit.

**Organization:** Environmental Working Group (EWG)

**Role Title:** Vice President, Engineering

**Location:** Washington, DC (hybrid preferred; remote considered for exceptional candidates)

**Employment Type:** Full Time

**Seniority Level:** Executive

**Salary Range:** $180,000–$300,000 annually (commensurate with experience)

## About the Company

"The Environmental Working Group (EWG) is a nonprofit, nonpartisan organization dedicated to empowering people to live healthier lives in a healthier environment." The organization operates major digital platforms including Skin Deep®, Food Scores®, and the Guide to Healthy Cleaning, which serve millions annually.

## Key Responsibilities

- Lead modernization of technology stack, platforms, and data systems with a phased roadmap
- Reduce reliance on manual and spreadsheet-based workflows through scalable systems
- Define technical roadmap aligned with organizational goals
- Improve data ingestion, validation, and processing pipelines
- Establish engineering standards for testing, QA/QC, and release processes
- Evaluate and integrate AI-powered tools for consumer products
- Oversee data infrastructure integrity and governance
- Direct the engineering team through hiring, mentoring, and performance management
- Remain hands-on writing and reviewing production code
- Communicate technology strategy to leadership, board, and funders

## Requirements

- 15+ years in engineering, platform, or technology leadership
- Experience leading systems modernization
- Demonstrated AI/ML application to products or operations
- Strong background in data platforms, APIs, cloud architecture
- Ability to work as hands-on technical contributor alongside management
- Track record building high-performing teams
- Cross-functional leadership skills
- Excellent communication and strategic execution ability

## Total Rewards

- Comprehensive medical, dental, and vision coverage
- Generous paid leave (annual and sick)
- Employer-paid disability and life insurance
- Paid family leave
- 403(b) retirement plan with matching
- Telework flexibility
