# Phone Screen Prep - EWG VP, Engineering

Screen with Farah Siddiq. Applied 2026-07-07, so this is a warm callback roughly seven weeks later.

## Who you are talking to

Farah is almost certainly a recruiter or People screen, not the hiring manager. The role partners with EWG's Chief Experience Officer, so the CXO is the likely hiring manager you meet later. Farah's job on this call is one decision: pass you to the next round or not. She cannot hire you, but she can stop you. So the goal is simple. Remove every reason to drop you, and hand her two or three lines she can repeat upward.

## Your 90-second background pitch

Use this for "walk me through your background," then stop and let her drive.

> I am a technology executive who still writes code. Right now I run the implementation side of an AI consulting practice, so I own the architecture, build the data pipelines, and carry them to production for clients. Before that I was SVP of Technology at Pipeline 360, where I shipped a revenue-critical data platform from zero to production in under 90 days during a post-divestiture reset, protecting an 8-figure line. Before that I spent seven years at Industry Dive growing one engineering team of eight into five teams of forty-plus, serving 100-plus properties used by millions.
>
> The thread through all of it is taking aging, manual-heavy operations and turning them into modern, automated platforms without breaking the people who depend on them. That is what your posting describes for EWG, and it is why I applied.

## Three gaps to get ahead of

1. Nonprofit is new for you. Own it the way your cover letter already does. A nonprofit is a new setting, and you want to be straight about it. What carries across is how you lead. You translate strategy for the people who fund it, and you steward budget like it is the mission's money, because it is.
2. Location and remote. The posting prefers DC hybrid and considers remote for exceptional candidates. You run remote now, and Pipeline 360 was fully remote. Decide your answer before the call. Hybrid, relocation, or remote-only. If remote-only, position yourself as the exception and lean on your remote leadership track record.
3. Consumer-scale data. You have "100+ properties used by millions" and a shipped AI qualification product, which is honest and enough. If pressed on database scale, reframe Skin Deep and Food Scores as data-integrity products, millions of validated records, which plays to your Pipeline 360 pipeline strength.

## Likely questions and your ammo

- Why EWG and why this role. Mission plus the modernization moment you like best, legacy and manual to modern and automated. You reach millions through Skin Deep, Food Scores, and the Healthy Living app, and you are ready to move off spreadsheet-bound workflows.
- Why looking and why now. Frame Dual Logic honestly. EWG is a chance to own one mission-driven platform end to end and stay hands-on. Do not badmouth anything.
- Tell me about a modernization you led. Pipeline 360, 90 days, zero to production, 8-figure line protected, security held. Your strongest story.
- How do you use AI responsibly. The AI qualification system with a human review loop. Your line: you add AI where it earns trust, with a human in the loop where the stakes are high. Tie it to EWG's brand being built on honest science.
- Salary expectations. Range is $180K to $300K. Know your number before the call. Safe answer: your posted range works for me, and I would want to understand remote flexibility, scope, and team size before landing on a specific figure.

## Questions to ask Farah

1. Who does this role report to, and is the engineering team in place today or is this a build?
2. What is driving the modernization now, a specific platform pain, a funding milestone, or a board mandate?
3. How real is the remote flexibility for this role in practice?
4. The posting lists first 12-18 month success metrics. Which one matters most to leadership?
5. What is the interview process and timeline after this call?

## Fifteen-minute homework before the call

- Open Skin Deep, Food Scores, and the Healthy Living app. Have one real observation about each as a product and a data platform.
- Skim EWG's recent homepage and news so you sound current, not like you applied and forgot.
