# Role Summary – EWG Vice President, Engineering

## At a glance
- **Company:** Environmental Working Group (EWG), nonprofit consumer-health research/advocacy
- **Role:** Vice President, Engineering (net-new executive seat, no incumbent CTO)
- **Location / remote:** Washington, DC hybrid preferred; remote considered for exceptional candidates
- **Seniority:** Executive
- **Compensation:** $180,000–$300,000

## What the role is
> A hands-on, player-coach engineering executive brought in to modernize EWG's aging technology stack and data systems. The org runs consumer platforms (Skin Deep, Food Scores, Healthy Living app) reaching ~30M people, but leans on manual and spreadsheet-based workflows and a legacy CMS stack. The VP owns the modernization roadmap, stands up real data ingestion/validation/processing pipelines, sets engineering standards (testing, QA/QC, release), integrates AI responsibly into consumer products, directs a small engineering team, stays in the codebase, and translates technology strategy to a non-technical President, board, and funders.

## Key requirements
- 15+ years in engineering, platform, or technology leadership
- Experience leading systems modernization
- Demonstrated AI/ML application to products or operations
- Strong background in data platforms, APIs, cloud architecture
- Hands-on technical contributor alongside management (player-coach)
- Track record building high-performing teams
- Cross-functional leadership + excellent communication and strategic execution
