About You

You have clear and proven attribution of commercial success and business outcomes in self-serve SMB products  (i.e. products similiar to Patch's promoted events and featured business listings: self-serve, low price point, recurring).
You are fluent in Clay Christensen's JTBD framework.
You have 5+ years of leading leaders in technical and product roles in entrepreneurial environments.
You have technical credibility: 10+ years of experience as an engineer and/or managing engineering teams, and the ability to reason directly about architecture, data, feasibility, and trade-offs.
You are hands-on: You prototype, inspect the data, talk with customers and work through details with the team; you do not delegate the substance of product leadership.
You lead with curiosity, candor, self-awareness, low ego, and a sense of humor.


What We Offer / Why Patch

Patch -- the largest, and one of the few thriving local media businesses -- has been on the cutting edge of AI since 2022. We have approximately 15,000 communities with AI newsletters that have a 94+ NPS. Our moderation for 160M active users (and many other parts of the business) are AI-native.
Our work connecting people with their communities matters. We are Patchy: we have fun and take our mission seriously but not ourselves.
The annual base salary range for this position is $225,000 - $275,000 USD. The actual salary will be determined based on skills, experience and other job-related factors, consistent with applicable law. This position is also eligible for a performance-based discretionary bonus and equity. Reasonably good benefits.
Even if you are a world-class leader, you will become a better leader at Patch through our leadership standards. All applicants for the role will go through them.


Product and Technology Environment

Patch's products span consumer web and mobile, email, publishing, user-generated content, payments, data, search and advertising integrations.
The technology environment includes JavaScript/TypeScript, React, Next.js, Node.js, Drupal/PHP, AWS, Heroku, Pantheon, GCP/BigQuery/GA4, Stripe, Auth0/Okta, Typesense/OpenSearch, GitHub/Jira and modern AI tools.
You need not master the stack, but you must be technically fluent and able to earn the trust of engineers.


What You Will Lead

Commercial success for platform products, leading with with force, courage, and velocity. If you are doing something common in media, it is not bold enough.
Helping sales make more money, especially through applying our AI work with large brands (separate from platform products)
Finding ways to make our journalists' careers more rewarding financially and operationally.
