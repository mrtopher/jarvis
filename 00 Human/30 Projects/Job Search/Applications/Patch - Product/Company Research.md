# Company Research – Patch

> Company facts carried over from the 2026-07-30 Patch (VP of Engineering) research — same company, so not re-fetched. Only the role context differs.

## Snapshot
- **What they do:** Hyperlocal news and information platform. Community news, events, classifieds, business listings, and local advertising. The JD cites ~15,000 communities with AI newsletters (94+ NPS) and AI-native moderation for 160M active users. (Prior research: 25M monthly users, ~3M active email subscribers, 85-person newsroom.)
- **Size / stage:** Small but profitable operating company, private. "The largest, and one of the few thriving local media businesses" (JD). Mature turnaround, not a startup.
- **Funding / ownership:** Owned by investment firm Hale Global. Originally an AOL property (2009); spun out of AOL in 2014 as a joint venture with Hale Global, which turned it around to profitability. Not VC-backed round-by-round.
- **HQ / locations:** New York roots; operates remote-first with employees and contractors across time zones.
- **Remote policy:** Remote-first, distributed. (Note: the Ashby board at jobs.ashbyhq.com/patch.io is a DIFFERENT company — a climate/carbon API firm in SF. Do not confuse the two.)

## Products & business model
> Advertising-driven hyperlocal media, plus self-serve monetization products. Revenue from local advertising, enterprise/large-brand advertising (sales-led), and self-serve SMB products: promoted events and featured business listings (self-serve, low price point, recurring). The moat is scale of local coverage at low cost. Since 2022 the growth story is AI: AI-assisted newsletters expanded coverage dramatically and AI-native moderation runs at 160M-active-user scale. Products span consumer web + mobile, email, publishing, user-generated content, payments (Stripe), data, search, and advertising integrations.

## Tech stack
> JavaScript/TypeScript, React, Next.js, Node.js on the modern side; Drupal/PHP as meaningful legacy. Infra across AWS, Heroku, Pantheon (Drupal hosting). Data on GCP/BigQuery/GA4. Payments via Stripe. Identity via Auth0/Okta. Search via Typesense/OpenSearch. GitHub/Jira for SDLC. Modern AI tooling. Classic modern-plus-legacy media platform.

## Culture & values
> Mission-driven around local news surviving. Scrappy, lean, builder-first. "We are Patchy: we have fun and take our mission seriously but not ourselves." Explicit leadership standards that all applicants go through. For this seat: wants a hands-on product leader with real technical credibility who does not delegate the substance of product leadership, and who leads "with force, courage, and velocity" ("if you are doing something common in media, it is not bold enough").

## Reputation
> Glassdoor ~3.2/5 across 254 reviews; ~42% would recommend. Work-life balance 2.9, culture/values 3.3, career opportunities 2.8. Positives: remote/flexible, good colleagues, funding to succeed. Negatives: management changes approach and priorities too frequently ("big push then change it a month or two in"); some historical favoritism / DEI-exclusion complaints. Priority thrash is the recurring theme — directly relevant to a product leader asked to set commercial direction "with force and velocity."

## Recent news
- AI newsletter expansion scaled coverage and subscribers dramatically — covered by Columbia Journalism Review, The Media Copilot, AdExchanger.
- CEO Warren St. John on the media/conference circuit (SXSW, DMWF) positioning Patch as a profitable AI-driven local-news model.

## Green flags
- Profitable and independent — rare runway safety.
- Genuinely AI-forward with shipped product (not slideware) — matches Chris's AI-native identity.
- Senior product seat with real commercial ownership and exec exposure.
- Remote-first (clean for a VA-based candidate).
- Comp $225-275K base + bonus + equity — disclosed, at/near target band.
- Digital-media domain — Chris's Industry Dive lane.

## Red flags
- **Role type is Product, not Engineering** — a pivot out of Chris's technology/engineering-leadership lane (see Role Summary).
- Priority thrash / frequent direction changes on Glassdoor — setting durable commercial product direction may be an organizational fight.
- Lean, DIY seat — hands-on, not a big-org command role.
- **Second seat at the same company** — Chris already has a VP of Engineering application in at Patch (stronger fit); applying for a second exec seat with the same CEO needs a deliberate reason.
- Historical DEI/exclusion complaints in older reviews.
- Hyperlocal ad-driven media is structurally tough; margins lean.
