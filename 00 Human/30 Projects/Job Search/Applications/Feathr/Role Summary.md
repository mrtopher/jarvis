# Role Summary - Feathr VP of Engineering

## At a glance
- **Company:** Feathr - nonprofit marketing/adtech SaaS (Gainesville FL, founded 2012, ~141 staff, ~$22M ARR, Series B / Fulcrum Equity-backed, 1,300+ nonprofit customers)
- **Role:** VP of Engineering - own the whole engineering org, report to and partner with the CEO
- **Location / remote:** Fully remote US, EST/CST preferred (Chris/VA fits cleanly)
- **Seniority:** Executive / VP
- **Compensation:** $200-250K base + annual bonus + equity (base is below the $250-300K target; bonus + equity may close it - confirm the full package early)

## What the role is
> A **maturity-and-craftsmanship** engineering leadership seat, not a growth/scaling one. The explicit mandate is to refine, harden, and improve an existing 14-year-old product: pay down technical debt, lift reliability/performance/usability, and run engineering by quality and customer-experience metrics (defect rate, uptime, support resolution, CSAT). Two things make it distinctive: (1) build the engineering **management layer from scratch** by coaching tech leads into managers, and (2) **lead Feathr's AI transformation** with organization-wide Claude access, driving agentic-AI adoption with measured impact. The VP partners daily with Product, Customer Success, and Managed Services, joins customer calls, and works as the CEO's technical thought partner on where limited engineering capacity earns the most customer value.

## Key requirements
- 6+ years engineering leadership (steady-state/mature phase valued, not just hypergrowth)
- 10+ years formal software development
- Built management layers / developed tech leads into engineering managers
- Drove AI-coding-tool adoption, rollout, governance, and impact metrics; excited to lead with agentic AI
- Deep modern architecture + cloud infrastructure knowledge
- Track record improving quality/performance/usability in **established codebases** (not just net-new features)
- B2B SaaS strongly preferred; MarTech or adjacent a plus
