## What they likely care about
- A VP who can **take engineering off his plate** credibly: build the management layer (coach tech leads into managers) so he can step back from day-to-day engineering leadership.
- **Product quality, reliability, and customer experience over raw feature velocity** - the JD is explicit that this is a maturity/craftsmanship phase, not growth.
- **Doing more with a flat or lean team via AI** - he has committed org-wide Claude access and wants a leader who will make agentic AI a real force multiplier, with metrics to prove impact.
- **Business-legible engineering** - as a physics-to-CEO founder he will want technical decisions tied to customer value and defensible with data (defect rates, uptime, CSAT, support resolution).
- Pragmatism and judgment ("when is 'good enough' right, and when isn't it").
