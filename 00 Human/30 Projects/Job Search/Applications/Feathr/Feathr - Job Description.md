# Feathr - VP of Engineering (archived job posting)

**Company:** Feathr, a nonprofit marketing platform trusted by over 1,300 nonprofits.

**Role Title:** VP of Engineering

**Location:** Remote, United States (preference for EST/CST time zones)

**Employment Type:** Full-time

**Seniority Level:** Executive/VP-level

## Compensation
- Base salary: $200,000-$250,000 annually
- Annual performance bonus
- Equity

## About the Company
Feathr is purpose-built to help nonprofits "unlock more results, time, and confidence." The software marketing platform equips nonprofits with tools to build community connections and grow impact. The company emphasizes culture built on five values: Practicality (Progress Over Perfection), Ambition (When Feathr wins, We all win), Candor (Better communication, Better results), Trust (Assume the best, Share openly), and Service (We exist to fix).

## The Role
Lead engineering teams focused on "refining, hardening, and improving our existing product - not scaling headcount or chasing rapid growth." This is described as a "maturity and craftsmanship role" centered on usability, reliability, and customer experience. You'll build management structures and technical rigor to sustain a healthy, high-quality product long-term, working as a thought partner to the CEO on where engineering investment drives customer value.

## Key Responsibilities
- Partner with CEO to define technology vision and operating plan focused on product quality, usability, and customer experience
- Identify and prioritize technical debt, performance, and reliability improvements alongside customer-facing enhancements
- Build engineering management capability by coaching tech leads and determining where formal management roles are needed
- Oversee planning and execution of product refinements and upgrades, ensuring quality and service readiness
- Own budget, performance management, and career development for tech leads and broader team
- Define, track, and report on metrics for a maturing product: defect rates, performance, uptime/reliability, support resolution time, and customer satisfaction
- Balance individual customer needs and support escalations with broader product priorities
- Collaborate daily with Product, Customer Success, Managed Services, and cross-functional stakeholders
- Define and continuously improve technical support triage and stakeholder touchpoints
- Make judgment calls on where to invest limited engineering capacity for highest customer impact
- Participate in customer calls to represent technical perspective
- Maintain clear documentation of engineering processes, decisions, and standards
- Lead Feathr's AI transformation using organization-wide Claude access to shape practices and standards

## Required Experience
- 6+ years of engineering leadership experience, ideally including mature/steady-state product phase (not just hypergrowth)
- 10+ years of formal software development experience
- Experience developing tech leads into engineering managers or building management layers from scratch
- Experience driving adoption, rollout, and governance of AI coding tools across engineering teams, managing impact metrics, and excitement about leading the team with agentic AI
- Deep knowledge of modern software architecture, cloud infrastructure, and development practices
- Track record improving product quality, performance, or usability in established codebases, not just shipping net-new features
- B2B SaaS experience strongly preferred; MarTech or adjacent verticals a plus

## Desired Qualities
- Motivated by refinement and craftsmanship, not growth for its own sake; takes real satisfaction in making existing products better
- Comfortable operating without a management layer already in place; can coach and develop tech leads
- Balances strategic thinking with hands-on involvement; comfortable in architecture and code-quality discussions and roadmap planning
- Instinctively connects engineering decisions back to customer experience and usability outcomes
- Uses quality, reliability, and customer-experience metrics to guide priorities, not just velocity
- Pragmatic, organized, and detail-oriented, with judgment to know when "good enough" is right and when it isn't
- Strong communicator who moves fluidly between engineering, executive, and customer-facing audiences
