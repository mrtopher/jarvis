# Company Research - Feathr

## Snapshot
- **What they do:** Digital marketing / marketing-automation platform purpose-built for nonprofits. Combines display + social advertising, email marketing, landing pages/forms, website analytics, nonprofit templates/workflows, conversion tracking and reporting in one platform. Trusted by 1,300+ nonprofits (marketing puts the customer count at 800-1,300 depending on source).
- **Size / stage:** ~141 employees as of 2026, down from ~164 in 2023 (shrinking headcount). ~$22M ARR/revenue. Mature company, founded 2012 (14 years in market).
- **Funding / ownership:** ~$14-20M total venture funding. Most recent major round was a Series B (~$11M) led by Fulcrum Equity Partners (Atlanta growth-equity firm). Growth-equity backed, not a fresh-VC hypergrowth story.
- **HQ / locations:** Gainesville, FL (UF spinout roots).
- **Remote policy:** Fully remote, US. Preference for EST/CST time zones (Chris is VA/EST - clean fit).

## Products & business model
> B2B SaaS. Subscription marketing platform sold to nonprofits (associations, foundations, advocacy, mission-driven orgs). Recent product expansion in late 2025 / 2026: **Feathr Intelligence** (AI-powered marketing intelligence, live now) and **Feathr Fundraising** (integrated fundraising, launched Q4 2025), with peer-to-peer fundraising, SMS, and auction tools on the 2026 roadmap. The company is layering AI and fundraising onto a mature advertising/email core.

## Tech stack
> Not disclosed in the JD. The role names "modern software architecture, cloud infrastructure, and development practices" and an **organization-wide Claude access** mandate for AI-assisted / agentic development. Expect a mature multi-year SaaS codebase (adtech + email + analytics) with meaningful technical debt to pay down (the JD's central theme). Confirm cloud (AWS vs GCP) and languages in early conversations.

## Culture & values
> Five stated values: Practicality (Progress Over Perfection), Ambition (When Feathr wins, We all win), Candor (Better communication, Better results), Trust (Assume the best, Share openly), Service (We exist to fix). Historically known for a 4-day work week and strong benefits (~99% covered health/dental/vision, extended winter break, parental leave, wellness + home-office + pro-dev budgets).

## Reputation
> Glassdoor ~3.5/5 across ~90 reviews; 53% would recommend (85% among US reviewers). Positives: benefits, work-life balance, low office politics, investment in people. **Recent-review concerns are real diligence flags:** some 2026 reviews describe the culture as "dying," leadership as "listless," and speculate the company is "likely to be sold or dissolved." The **4-day work week ended in 2026**. Headcount is down ~14% from its 2023 peak.

## Recent news
- 2025-2026: Launched Feathr Intelligence (AI) and Feathr Fundraising; announced 2026 roadmap (P2P fundraising, SMS, auctions).
- Headcount contraction 164 (2023) -> ~141 (2026).

## Green flags
- **AI mandate is the headline of the role** (org-wide Claude access, lead the AI transformation, agentic AI) - a near-exact match to Chris's Dual Logic identity.
- Mature, revenue-generating B2B SaaS (~$22M ARR) - real scale, clears the small-team pass bar that killed Sensei (~7 eng).
- Fully remote, EST/CST preference - Chris (VA) fits perfectly.
- Role is explicitly about product quality, reliability, and paying down technical debt - maps to the Pipeline 360 reliability reset and platform-hardening work.
- Technical founder-CEO who has led engineering himself and is handing it off - the hiring manager will speak Chris's language.
- Strong benefits, values-forward culture.

## Red flags
- **Comp base ($200-250K) sits below the $250-300K target** (bonus + equity may close the gap - confirm the full package early).
- **Shrinking headcount + Glassdoor "sold or dissolved" / "culture dying" / "listless leadership" signals** - this reads as a mature company under cost discipline, possibly positioning for a sale. A new VP's mandate (harden, mature, do more with a flat/lean team using AI) fits that posture, but the runway/exit question is real.
- **4-day work week ended in 2026** - a culture change that shows up in reviews.
- **Nonprofit + adtech/martech domain is new** for Chris (martech is a "plus," not required; carried as adjacent - Pipeline 360 is a B2B demand-gen/marketing platform and Industry Dive is digital media/advertising, so this is adjacent-new, not cold).
- The role is deliberately **not a growth/scaling seat** - a slight tension with Chris's build-and-scale track record (framable via the quality/reliability/reset work, but worth an honest note).
