# Company Research – Hyperproof

## Snapshot
- **What they do:** AI-powered GRC (Governance, Risk & Compliance) platform. Centralizes compliance operations, IT risk, audit readiness, and third-party risk management (TPRM) in one system of record. Pioneered the "compliance operations" category.
- **Size / stage:** Growth-stage private B2B SaaS. Headcount not publicly confirmed for 2026 (~150-250 range implied by hiring + funding); Glassdoor shows 72 reviews.
- **Funding / ownership:** ~$62M+ total raised. $40M growth round Aug 2023 led by Riverwood Capital, joined by Toba Capital; ~$22M+ raised prior. Venture-backed, founder-led.
- **HQ / locations:** Bellevue, Washington. Globally distributed / remote-friendly.
- **Remote policy:** This role is Remote (US). Company runs quarterly in-person connections.

## Products & business model
> B2B SaaS subscription. Core GRC platform plus an AI-native Third-Party Risk Management product (launched Apr 2026) that replaces questionnaire-driven vendor reviews with evidence-based risk intelligence. Continuously monitors legal/financial/security/reputational signals; maps to ISO 27001, NIST, HIPAA, PCI DSS, SOC 2, CAIQ, VSAQ. Serves compliance/security/GRC teams. Named a Category Leader in three 2026 Chartis RiskTech Quadrants (eGRC, TPRM, IT Risk).

## Tech stack
> B2B SaaS platform, cloud-native (multi-cloud convergence is an explicit mandate; AWS and/or Azure named in preferred quals). Microservices + APIs. AI/GenAI + agentic workflows in production. Core platform capabilities called out: Authentication, Roles/Permissions, Tasks, Workflows, Logging. FedRAMP Moderate authorized (Mar 2026), so a hardened, audited cloud posture.

## Culture & values
> Glassdoor 4.1/5 overall; work-life balance 4.4, culture & values 4.4, career opportunities 3.9, comp & benefits 3.9; 78% would recommend. Reviews describe a genuinely good day-to-day culture. Strong benefits (unlimited PTO, 12 wks parental leave, 4% 401k match, two company-wide rest weeks/yr).

## Reputation
> Positive employee sentiment on culture, but a specific leadership/stability concern: a ~25% layoff hit "the Monday after Thanksgiving" (late 2025) and surprised the company. At least one review calls out executive churn / "pulling the rug on projects that were near completion" and "incompetent executives." Read against this SVP seat, that matters (see red flags).

## Recent news
- **Apr 28, 2026** — Launched AI-native, evidence-based Third-Party Risk Management (the app this role is asked to architect for multi-cloud).
- **Mar 12, 2026** — Achieved FedRAMP Moderate authorization (a compliance obligation this role must uphold).
- **Feb 12, 2026** — Released 2026 IT Risk & Compliance Benchmark Report (1,000+ GRC professionals).
- **2026** — Named Category Leader in three Chartis RiskTech Quadrants (eGRC, TPRM, IT Risk).

## Green flags
- Remote (US), comp top end ($291K) reaches the top of Chris's target band.
- Clean title/shape match: SVP owning unified Product + Engineering, reports to CEO.
- AI-native product direction + agentic workflows = Chris's Dual Logic thesis in a product company.
- Category leader with real market recognition; FedRAMP + enterprise credibility.
- Strong culture + benefits ratings.

## Red flags
- **25% layoff in late 2025 + executive-instability reviews.** A brand-new SVP Platform seat that *consolidates* Product and Engineering under the CEO, right after a big cut, reads as a reorg/turnaround. Likely sits above or absorbs the existing VP of Product (Bob Heddle) and VP of Development (Dave Brennan) — political exposure and an org in flux.
- **Comp band $222-291K.** Bottom of band is below target; only the top reaches it. Equity terms unknown at a growth-stage private co that just cut 25%.
- **GRC/compliance-product domain is new** for Chris (mitigable; security/compliance ownership is real, but the vertical is not).
- Azure and FedRAMP specifics are gaps (both preferred/operational, not hard requirements).
