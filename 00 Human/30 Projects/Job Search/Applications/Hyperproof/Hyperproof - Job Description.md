## Position Overview

Hyperproof seeks a Senior Vice President of Platform to lead unified Product and Engineering organizations reporting directly to the CEO. This role oversees platform architecture, data infrastructure, and AI technology integration across the B2B SaaS platform. The role focuses on building scalable B2B SaaS infrastructure with AI integration.

## Key Responsibilities

- Provide unified leadership to globally distributed platform architects, product managers, and engineers.
- Define the technical roadmap for the enterprise platform and AI capabilities, aligned with corporate strategy and security standards.
- Drive platform-led growth supporting ARR, NRR, and GRR expansion.
- Standardize core capabilities: Authentication, Roles/Permissions, Tasks, Workflows, Logging.
- Architect multi-cloud deployment / convergence strategy for the Third-Party Risk Management application.
- Champion developer experience through reusable components and frameworks.
- Establish production patterns for AI/GenAI capabilities.
- Implement quality control frameworks and performance SLAs ensuring 99.9% uptime.
- Deliver predictable platform roadmaps within six months.
- Ensure compliance with FedRAMP Moderate obligations.

## Required Qualifications

- 10+ years leading software product/engineering organizations in B2B SaaS; 5+ years in executive roles (SVP/VP level).
- Proven simultaneous management of both Product and Engineering teams.
- 12+ years progressive technology experience spanning software engineering, cloud infrastructure, or data platforms.
- Track record delivering enterprise platforms and developer productivity solutions.
- Strong cloud-native design, microservices, and APIs expertise; familiarity with foundational AI/GenAI patterns.
- Multi-tiered organization management across distributed locations.
- Metric-driven leadership (DORA metrics, system availability).
- Current U.S. work authorization.

## Preferred Qualifications

- Experience in regulated environments (GRC, cybersecurity, or fintech).
- AWS and/or Azure scaling expertise.
- Advanced AI/agentic workflow production experience.
- B.S./M.S. in Computer Science or related discipline.
