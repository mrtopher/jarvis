## Q: How would you describe your hands-on experience with AI/GenAI engineering patterns such as RAG, model orchestration, or embeddings?

My experience here is hands-on, at the code level. I recently earned my AI Engineering Certification from AI Makerspace and built the capstone myself: an agentic RAG application that classifies products into US HTC codes for customs brokers - https://github.com/mrtopher/clearclass.

The problem is a good test of these patterns. HTC classification is a large, hierarchical rulebook where the answer turns on precise wording, so a general model guesses and hallucinates. I embedded the tariff schedule and reference material into a vector store, retrieved the closest candidate headings for a given product description, then had the model reason over only that retrieved context and return a classification with citations back to the source. Retrieval keeps it grounded, and the citations let a broker verify the call rather than trust it blindly.

I work in the same patterns day to day at Dual Logic for enterprise clients: RAG over private knowledge, embeddings and vector search for retrieval, and model orchestration that chains retrieval, tool calls, and evaluation behind guardrails so output quality and cost stay in check. I build these systems myself.
