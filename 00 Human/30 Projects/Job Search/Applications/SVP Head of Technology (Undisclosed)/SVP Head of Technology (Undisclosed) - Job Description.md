# SVP, Head of Technology - Job Description (verbatim)

> Source: LinkedIn recruiter posting, pasted by candidate 2026-07-23. Company intentionally not disclosed in the listing (confidential search). Recruiter name not captured in the paste.

SVP, Head of Technology

Remote (U.S.) | East Coast Hours Preferred | Total Compensation ~$500K (Base + Bonus + Equity)

I'm partnering exclusively with a private equity-backed technology company on one of the most compelling executive opportunities I've seen this year.

This isn't a traditional CTO role.

This isn't a mature organization looking for incremental optimization.

This is an opportunity to architect the future of an entire company as it transforms into an AI-native business, where intelligence, not headcount, becomes the primary driver of product innovation, operational leverage, and enterprise value.

Over the past 18 months, the company has invested heavily in its AI foundation. The core platform already exists. Your mandate is to determine what gets built, and just as importantly, what doesn't, while leading Product, Engineering, AI, Data, and Platform through their next phase of growth.

You'll report directly to the CEO and own the technology vision behind a modern, customer-facing software platform used by many of the world's leading brands. This is a highly visible role where you'll shape architecture, drive AI commercialization, oversee a lean team of exceptional engineers, and translate emerging AI capabilities into measurable business outcomes.

We're looking for an executive who still enjoys being close to the technology: someone equally comfortable discussing system architecture, challenging engineering decisions, evaluating build-versus-buy tradeoffs, and partnering with customers and the Board on long-term strategy.

Experience building AI-native products, leading Product and Engineering together, and scaling technology organizations in startup or growth-stage environments will resonate particularly well.

Backgrounds in AI platforms, AdTech, MarTech, SaaS, developer infrastructure, commerce technology, or adjacent industries are all highly relevant.

Highlights

Executive ownership of Technology, Product, AI, Data & Platform
AI-native operating model already underway
Significant autonomy and direct partnership with the CEO
Opportunity to build proprietary AI-powered products and intellectual property
Lean, high-caliber engineering organization
Private equity-backed with meaningful growth trajectory
Remote (East Coast hours preferred)
Total compensation approaching $500K (base, bonus, and equity)

If you've been looking for an opportunity to build rather than maintain, and want to help define what an AI-native technology organization looks like in practice, I would welcome a confidential conversation. Please apply and/or send me a direct message with your CV and upcoming availability.
