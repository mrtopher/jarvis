# Role Summary – Rebuy VP, Engineering

## At a glance
- **Company:** Rebuy (Shopify DTC personalization platform, ~130 people, Series A)
- **Role:** VP, Engineering (owns the engineering org and technical delivery)
- **Location / remote:** Fully remote, US or Canada
- **Seniority:** VP / top engineering leader, reports to the founders
- **Compensation:** $225K–$300K base, plus equity and full benefits

## What the role is
> The single owner of Rebuy's engineering organization. Set strategy, own architecture and code standards, and run the pod operating model (4–6 engineers per pod with team captains). Build the org: structure, career paths, hiring quality. Ship the headline technical bet, a V2 API-first surface (REST + OpenAPI + an agent-readiness layer so AI agents can call Rebuy inside the Shopify stack). Own reliability and performance of the engine (latency, errors, large datasets), lead platform security, and get the org and codebase diligence-ready for a strategic transaction, handled with discretion.

## Key requirements
- 15+ years engineering leadership; has led 30–50 person organizations
- Scaling high-growth SaaS (ecommerce, martech, or dev tools); reliability and operational excellence
- Deep architecture, distributed systems, APIs, cloud, platform engineering
- API-first product experience: REST, OpenAPI, developer experience, agent-tool patterns
- Modern practices: CI/CD, DevOps, observability, incident management
- Application and database performance at scale; large datasets
- Modern stack fluency (PHP / Node / React / Shopify preferred)
- Comfort in an exit-process posture with discretion
- Executive presence; translate technical work into business strategy
