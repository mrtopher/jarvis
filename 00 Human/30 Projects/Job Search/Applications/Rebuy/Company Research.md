# Company Research – Rebuy

## Snapshot
- **What they do:** AI/ML personalization and merchandising platform for Shopify DTC brands (upsells, cross-sells, smart carts, post-purchase, automated follow-ups). Named "Rebuy Engine."
- **Size / stage:** ~130 employees (as of mid-2026). Series A stage, high-growth SaaS.
- **Funding / ownership:** ~$17M Series A (Feb 2023), led by M13 (Latif Peracha on the board); ~$21.4M total across 2 rounds / 7 investors. Founder-controlled board (John and James Erck) alongside investor seats. Estimated ~$12M ARR.
- **HQ / locations:** Fully distributed. Team across US and Canada (some Europe presence).
- **Remote policy:** Fully remote (US and Canada eligible for this role).

## Products & business model
> Shopify app that increases merchant revenue through data-driven personalization: intelligent upsells and cross-sells, smart carts, checkout and post-purchase offers, and automated order follow-ups. SaaS subscription plus usage tiers, sold into DTC brands. Marquee customers named in the posting: Tecovas, Momofuku, Dime Beauty, Liquid Death, Magic Spoon, OLLY. 800+ reviews on the Shopify app store.

## Tech stack
> Posting signals PHP / Node / React in the Shopify ecosystem. API-first push toward a V2 REST surface with an OpenAPI spec and an "agent-readiness layer" (tools that AI agents can call). Cloud infrastructure, large datasets, database performance at scale. Exact cloud provider not stated in the posting.

## Culture & values
> Stated core values: ownership, drive, empathy. Fully distributed team that leans on written communication and autonomy. Pod operating model (4–6 engineers per pod with team captains). Founder-led, brothers as CEO and COO.

## Reputation
> Well regarded inside the Shopify ecosystem; strong app-store rating and recognizable DTC logos. Public employee-review data is thin at this size, so read culture live in the interviews rather than from Glassdoor.

## Recent news
- Posting explicitly flags "diligence-readiness for a strategic transaction" and asks for "comfort operating in exit-process posture with discretion." Strong signal the founders are preparing for an acquisition or growth-equity event in the next 12–24 months.
- V2 API-first / agent-tooling roadmap is the headline technical bet: making Rebuy callable by AI agents inside the Shopify commerce stack.

## Green flags
- VP Engineering owns the whole eng org and technical delivery. Real scope, direct line to the founders.
- Comp band is honest and solid for the stage: $225K–$300K base.
- 100% employer-paid health/dental/vision for the whole family, flexible PTO, 401(k) match. Strong benefits for a Series A company.
- The core mandate (install pod model, build career paths, harden reliability, ship an API-first V2, get diligence-ready) is exactly the org-plus-platform work Chris has done before.
- Fully remote, recognizable brand customers, clear AI-forward direction.

## Red flags
- "Exit-process posture" cuts both ways: an acquisition can mean a short runway for a new VP, a redundant eng leader post-deal, or heavy diligence pressure early. Ask directly about timeline, the founders' intent, and what happens to the role through a transaction.
- Series A at ~130 people with ~$12M ARR means comp upside is mostly equity in a company heading toward an exit; understand the equity grant and its treatment in a deal.
- Founder brothers as CEO and COO: strong alignment, but the VP Eng must earn trust fast and carve out real authority under two founders who have run engineering themselves.
- Domain (Shopify DTC commerce, agent-tool API design) is adjacent to, not the same as, Chris's B2B media / martech background.
