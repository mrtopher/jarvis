**POSITION**
- Title: VP, Engineering
- Location: Remote - US
- Employment Type: Full Time
- Workplace Type: Remote (US and Canada eligible)

**COMPENSATION**
Base pay range: $225,000–$300,000 USD annually (varies by geographic market and experience)

**COMPANY OVERVIEW**
Rebuy powers DTC brands including Tecovas, Momofuku, Dime Beauty, Liquid Death, Magic Spoon, and OLLY with intelligent, personalized experiences. The company operates fully remote across the US and Canada. Core values emphasize ownership, drive, and empathy.

**ROLE SUMMARY**
The VP of Engineering owns the engineering organization and technical delivery of rebuyengine.com, leading engineering pods (4–6 engineers each with team captains). Primary objective: deliver the V2 API-first surface, a standardized REST toolset for agents calling Rebuy within the Shopify ecosystem.

**KEY RESPONSIBILITIES**
- Set engineering strategy, own technical architecture and code standards
- Lead and grow the organization; install pod operating model, design structure, establish career paths
- Ship V2 REST API, OpenAPI spec, agent-readiness layer
- Own engineering KPIs: velocity, reliability, defect density, hiring quality
- Partner on diligence-readiness for strategic transaction
- Partner with Product on roadmap and technical vision
- Own Rebuy engine performance metrics (latency, errors)
- Lead platform security
- Review and optimize tech stack; assess failure points and redundancies
- Ensure high-quality code delivery

**REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS — Hard Skills**
- 15+ years engineering leadership; led 30–50 person organizations
- Experience scaling SaaS in ecommerce, martech, or developer tools
- Proven scaling of high-growth SaaS platforms emphasizing reliability and operational excellence
- Deep expertise: software architecture, distributed systems, APIs, cloud infrastructure, platform engineering
- Experience building API-first products using REST and OpenAPI standards
- Modern development practices: CI/CD, DevOps, observability, incident management, engineering operations
- Application and database performance optimization; large-scale distributed systems
- Strong opinions on API design, developer experience, OpenAPI, REST conventions, agent-tool patterns
- Modern stack fluency (PHP/Node/React/Shopify ecosystem preferred)
- Comfort operating in exit-process posture with discretion
- Vast experience with large datasets; database expertise required

**REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS — Soft Skills**
- Strong communication; ability to break down complex problems
- Executive presence; ability to inspire confidence and build trust
- Translate technical concepts into business strategy and recommendations
- Collaborative leader across Product, Customer Success, Sales, Operations, Executive Leadership
- Positive attitude, problem-solver, self-motivated, passionate about ecommerce

**INTERVIEW PROCESS**
1. Recruiter Screen (30 min)
2. Hiring Manager Interview (30–45 min)
3. COO Interview (30 min)
4. Panel Interview with engineering team (45–60 min)
5. Project-based Presentation (3–5 business days)
6. Final Interviews with CEO and COO (15 min each)

**BENEFITS**
- Fully remote work (US/Canada)
- Flexible PTO, generous holidays, parental leave, birthday holiday
- 100% employer-paid health, dental, vision for employee and family
- 401(k) (US) or TFSA/RRSP (Canada) with 3% gross salary contribution
