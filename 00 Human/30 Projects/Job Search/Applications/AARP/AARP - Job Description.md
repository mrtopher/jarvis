## Job Description

This role leads enterprise-wide technology initiatives in partnership with digital and business stakeholders to deliver innovative, consumer-centric experiences. The position drives strategic platform integration to amplify organizational capabilities and accelerate digital transformation, championing rapid delivery to achieve measurable business outcomes.

## Key Responsibilities

- Shape the strategic direction of consumer-facing technology, guiding architecture and capability development
- Lead strategy and governance for consumer-facing Secure Software Development Lifecycle practices
- Oversee full lifecycle from conceptualization through roadmap development, implementation, and optimization across business-critical platforms
- Manage enterprise digital budget planning and financial stewardship
- Establish strategy for high-volume, low-latency cloud-native applications supporting mission-critical platforms
- Champion innovation in scalable design, performance engineering, and high-availability infrastructure
- Ensure seamless collaboration across technical functions

- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, Engineering, Business Administration, or related field
- 10+ years leading enterprise-wide technology strategies and large-scale modernization programs
- Experience with Generative AI technologies including LLMs, embeddings, RAG, and vector databases

- DevOps and SSDLC experience with Git-based repositories and CI/CD pipelines
- Mobile technology strategy experience across iOS and Android
- Large-scale technology budget management experience
- Regular attendance and strong communication skills
