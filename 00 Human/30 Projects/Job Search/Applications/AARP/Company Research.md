# Company Research – AARP

## Snapshot
- **What they do:** Nonprofit membership and advocacy organization for Americans 50+. Advocacy, media/content, member benefits, and a large consumer digital footprint (aarp.org, mobile apps, member services).
- **Size / stage:** ~6,600 employees; ~$1.7B annual revenue (2026). Mature, established nonprofit (501c4) founded 1958. ~35-38M members.
- **Funding / ownership:** Nonprofit. Revenue from membership dues, royalties on branded products/services (insurance, etc.), and grants. Not VC-backed; no runway risk.
- **HQ / locations:** Washington, DC.
- **Remote policy:** Posting lists Washington, DC. Remote/hybrid not stated in the JD; AARP HQ is DC-based and many roles are hybrid. Confirm early (DC-adjacent VA helps Chris).

## Products & business model
> Consumer-facing digital properties (aarp.org, member portal, mobile iOS/Android apps, content/media) plus benefits marketplace. Business model is dues + royalty/affiliate revenue, so the digital experience is a direct driver of membership acquisition and retention. This VP seat owns the technology behind those consumer experiences.

## Tech stack
> JD signals: AWS (Lambda, EKS, S3), Git-based CI/CD, GenAI stack (LLMs, embeddings, RAG, vector databases), mobile (iOS/Android). Preferred: Palantir Foundry (data/AI platform), Adobe Experience Manager (CMS/DXP), ML/predictive analytics. This is a cloud-native, AI-forward, content-platform-heavy environment.

## Culture & values
> Mission-driven (empowering people to choose how they live as they age). Employees consistently praise the mission, work/life balance, and pay. Overall Glassdoor ~4.2/5.

## Reputation
> Glassdoor overall ~4.2/5 (878 reviews) — strong. BUT a recurring, specific red flag: the IT / Digital Transformation functions are repeatedly called out as toxic — burnout, bullying/retaliation, wasted spend, and constant priority/signal switching on projects. Leadership criticism centers on poor managers kept in place too long. The good-org-average masks a rough tech-org experience, which matters directly for this seat.

## Recent news
- 2026-07-14: AARP named Michael Caruso SVP & Chief Content Officer (media/editorial leadership refresh).
- Ongoing multi-year digital transformation toward a unified, omni-channel consumer experience for ~38M members (led by CIO Naj Uddin).

## Green flags
- Massive consumer scale (35-38M members) and a real digital-transformation + AI mandate — squarely in Chris's lane.
- Financially stable nonprofit ($1.7B rev), no runway risk, mission with genuine weight.
- JD requirements map almost one-to-one to Chris's profile (AWS cloud-native, GenAI/RAG, SSDLC/CI-CD, mobile strategy, budget ownership, platform modernization).
- DC location is commutable/hybrid-friendly for a VA-based candidate.

## Red flags
- Documented toxicity specifically in IT / Digital Transformation (burnout, bullying, priority thrash) — the exact org this role sits in. This is the headline diligence item.
- VP comp at AARP appears to top out modestly by market standards (Glassdoor VP avg ~$200K; nonprofit bands), likely at or below the $250-300K target. Confirm the band early.
- Large, mature nonprofit = bureaucracy and slower change velocity than the JD's "rapid delivery" language implies.
- Remote/hybrid unstated; may require meaningful DC on-site presence.
