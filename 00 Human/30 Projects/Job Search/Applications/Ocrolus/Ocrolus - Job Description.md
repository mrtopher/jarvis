## About the Company

Ocrolus is an AI-powered platform for financial institutions, automating underwriting workflows across small business, mortgage, and consumer lending. The platform processes nearly one million credit applications monthly, with over 99% accuracy in capturing financial document data. Trusted by 400+ customers including PayPal, Brex, SoFi, Plaid, Better Mortgage, Enova, and Square.

---

## Role Overview

"This is a player-coach role: a hands-on technical leader who progressed through the IC route - Architect or Principal Engineer - gets into the weeds when it matters, and leads at the executive level."

---

## Key Responsibilities

**Engineering Delivery & Velocity:**
- Own end-to-end execution: roadmap planning, sprint cadence, release quality, dependency management
- Partner with Product teams on capacity alignment and tradeoff communication
- "Drive a culture of high velocity and high trust - ship fast, learn fast, fix fast"

**AI-Native Platform:**
- Lead engineering strategy for integrating LLMs and agentic workflows at scale
- Drive adoption of AI-augmented development tools across teams
- Define standards for AI output quality, hallucination control, and cost governance
- Compress prototype-to-production cycles for GenAI features

**Architecture & Reliability:**
- Lead architecture reviews and critical system design decisions
- Own technical roadmap for distributed systems, platform architecture, scalability
- "Set and enforce engineering standards: code quality, observability, incident response and security"

**Organization Leadership:**
- Lead distributed engineering org across US and India as one of three VP Engineering leaders
- Manage directors and senior managers; develop leadership capabilities
- Set hiring standards and recruit senior talent
- Own DEI and retention outcomes

**Executive Leadership:**
- Represent engineering at executive level on technology execution and risks
- Manage domain engineering budget: headcount, tooling, cloud costs
- Partner with Sales and Customer Success on SLAs and escalations

## Required Qualifications (Non-Negotiables)

- "14+ years in software engineering with at least 5 years in engineering leadership; has scaled through Series C or equivalent growth"
- Strong IC background (Architect or Principal Engineer) with architecture and system design ownership
- "Hands-on experience shipping AI/ML systems to production: LLMs, RAG, agentic workflows or document intelligence"
- Build and scale large-scale distributed platforms with high availability
- Technical strategy partnership experience with CTO/CEO
- "Deep expertise in distributed systems, cloud-native microservices architecture at scale (AWS preferred)"

## Strong Signal Qualifications
