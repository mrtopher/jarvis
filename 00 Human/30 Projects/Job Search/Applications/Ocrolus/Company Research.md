# Company Research - Ocrolus

## Snapshot
- **What they do:** AI-powered document and analytics platform for lenders. Automates underwriting across small business, mortgage, and consumer lending. Reads 1,150+ financial document types (bank statements, pay stubs, tax forms) at 99%+ accuracy.
- **Size / stage:** ~656 employees. Series C. Founded 2014.
- **Funding / ownership:** ~$142M total raised. $80M Series C led by Fin Capital / Oak HC/FT at a $500M+ valuation. Backed by Stage 2 Capital (whose careers board hosts this posting).
- **HQ / locations:** New York, NY. Remote-first. Distributed engineering across US and India.
- **Remote policy:** Remote-first. This role is listed Washington, DC (Remote).

## Products & business model
> B2B fintech infrastructure. Sells document AI + analytics to financial institutions on a usage/subscription model. Processes nearly one million credit applications monthly and $100B+ in lending decisions annually. Customer roster includes PayPal, Brex, SoFi, Plaid, Better Mortgage, Enova, and Square. 400+ enterprise customers.

## Tech stack
> Cloud-native microservices at scale, AWS preferred. Distributed systems with high availability. Proprietary human-in-the-loop (HITL) verification engine paired with 10+ years of labeled financial document data. Actively integrating open- and closed-source LLMs, RAG, and agentic workflows into the core platform.

## Culture & values
> Remote-first, values Empathy, Curiosity, Humility, Ownership. Self-describes as "builders, thinkers, and problem solvers." Emphasis on velocity and trust ("ship fast, learn fast, fix fast"). DEI and retention are called out as explicit VP-level ownership.

## Reputation
> Category leader in document AI / intelligent document processing (IDP) for lending. Well-regarded, name-brand fintech logos. Series C fintech in a sector that saw a funding pullback, so watch for pressure on margins and headcount discipline (budget ownership is explicit in the JD). CTO Pieter Nel is a credible AI-strategy hire (ex-McKinsey, MIT Sloan).

## Recent news
- Data-centric AI push: platform is being re-architected as "AI-native," integrating state-of-the-art LLMs alongside the HITL engine.
- Scaling senior technical leadership (multiple VP Engineering leaders, CTO Pieter Nel joined ~2024).

## Green flags
- Genuinely AI-native product, not AI-washing. Agentic workflows and LLMs are core to the roadmap, which matches Chris's hands-on AI work exactly.
- Player-coach VP role: wants a hands-on architect who also leads at the exec level. That is Chris's actual shape.
- HITL verification engine maps to Chris's belief that you can't blindly replace workers with AI agents. Strong values fit.
- Washington, DC posting: Chris is DC-based (Industry Dive was DC).
- Distributed US/India org: Chris has run engineering across time zones before.

## Red flags
- Compensation not listed. Biggest unknown. VP of Engineering at a Series C NYC fintech should be strong ($250K+ base plus equity), but confirm early.
- Fintech / document-AI / IDP domain depth is a genuine gap. The JD lists it as a "strong signal," not a non-negotiable, but two other VP Eng peers may already carry that domain.
- "Scaled through Series C or equivalent growth" is a required qualification. Chris has scaled growth-stage orgs (Series A at Phone2Action, post-acquisition scale at Industry Dive) but not a named Series C. Frame as "equivalent growth."
- Series C fintech budget pressure: explicit cloud-cost and headcount ownership signals cost discipline expectations.
