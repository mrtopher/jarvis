# Company Research – Tiny Health

## Snapshot
- **What they do:** At-home gut and vaginal microbiome testing built on shotgun metagenomics + proprietary AI + one of the largest longitudinal microbiome datasets. Consumer health from the first 1,000 days through healthy aging.
- **Size / stage:** ~41 employees. Series A (2024). No. 208 on the 2026 Inc. 5000 (fast-growth). 150,000+ people tested; the company cites ~25% month-over-month growth since 2022 launch.
- **Funding / ownership:** $8.5M Series A (2024) led by Spero Ventures (Overwater, Next Coast, Peterson, Modi, Tamarisc, Cartan, Taylor Sittler; existing TheVentureCity, Conscience VC, North South). ~$13M total raised. Founder-led, VC-backed. Notable: ran a customer-as-investor round (Forbes, 2024).
- **HQ / locations:** Austin, TX roots; operates remote-first.
- **Remote policy:** Remote-first, US and Canada, 9 AM - 6 PM CST core hours.

## Products & business model
> Direct-to-consumer test kits (gut + vaginal microbiome), subscription/repeat-testing revenue, research-backed reports and actionable recommendations. CLIA-certified / CAP-accredited lab partners process samples. Data-and-AI product: the report/insights layer sits on a large proprietary longitudinal dataset. Expanding into HCP (healthcare provider) and enterprise channels (they are hiring a B2B Marketing Lead for HCP & Enterprise), so a B2B2C motion is emerging alongside DTC.

## Tech stack
> Not disclosed publicly. Inferable from the product: bioinformatics/metagenomics pipelines (likely Python), a consumer web/app experience, a data platform over the longitudinal dataset, and "proprietary AI" for microbiome trend detection. The open Engineering roles (Full Stack Engineer, AI Engineer, VP of Engineering) confirm a full-stack + applied-AI shop. Confirm specifics in interview.

## Culture & values
> Mission-driven consumer health, science-founder DNA (microbiome scientists + physicians), lean and fast-growing. Chief Medical Officer Dr. Elisa Song and a strong scientific advisory board (UCLA, Harvard, Mayo, Johns Hopkins) anchor clinical credibility. Small team means high ownership and hands-on expectations.

## Red flags
- Small (~41 people, ~12 engineers) and Series A: below the org scale Chris has recently targeted; thin support, heavy personal hands-on load.
- Comp not listed; a ~$13M-raised Series A may sit below the $250-300K target for a VP seat.
- Reports to a co-founder/Chief Growth Officer, not a CEO/CTO/CPTO — a growth-owner boss over an engineering exec is worth probing (priority pull toward growth over platform).
- Healthcare/HIPAA/SOC 2 experience is preferred; microbiome/health-data domain is new for Chris.
- No independent culture/reputation signal yet (too small for Glassdoor).
