# Role Summary – Tiny Health VP of Engineering

## At a glance
- **Company:** Tiny Health (consumer gut/vaginal microbiome testing + proprietary AI on a large longitudinal dataset)
- **Role:** VP of Engineering (the company's first)
- **Location / remote:** Remote-first, US & Canada, 9 AM - 6 PM CST core hours
- **Seniority:** VP / head of engineering; reports to a co-founder & Chief Growth Officer
- **Compensation:** Not listed (Series A, ~$13M raised — plan for possible below-target base)

## What the role is
> Own engineering end-to-end for ~12 engineers across two pods as the first dedicated engineering leader. Be the terminal owner of technical decisions, set architecture and scaling strategy, hire and develop tech leads, build real performance systems (impact, not ticket count), and represent engineering at the executive table. Explicitly a hands-on player-coach: as comfortable in the codebase as in a 1:1.

## Key requirements
- Direct management: 1:1s, performance reviews, growth planning
- Hands-on builder who still writes and reviews code
- Technical depth to debate architecture tradeoffs with senior engineers
- Comfortable as the sole engineering voice among business/product leaders
- Preferred: ran a 10-12 person team, scaled two-pod to leads/managers, developed engineers into leads, sourced A-players, healthcare/HIPAA/SOC 2, structuring ambiguous data into defensible conclusions
