## About Tiny Health

Tiny Health advances lifelong health from the first 1,000 days onward, addressing chronic disease through precision microbiome science. Founded in 2020 by microbiome scientists and physicians, the company uses shotgun metagenomics, proprietary AI, and one of the world's largest longitudinal datasets to reveal microbiome health trends. Over 150,000 people have used Tiny Health's research-backed gut and vaginal tests.

## The Opportunity

As the first VP of Engineering, you will own engineering end-to-end for a team of roughly a dozen engineers across two pods. Engineering currently lacks a dedicated leader and needs unified ownership over technical direction, architecture, hiring, and people management.

The role seeks a hands-on leader who is as comfortable in the codebase as in a 1:1, capable of earning senior engineer trust while representing engineering at the executive level. You will report directly to the co-founder and Chief Growth Officer.

## Key Responsibilities

- Become the terminal owner of engineering decisions.
- Build performance systems that measure individual impact beyond ticket volume.
- Set and evolve architecture and scaling strategy.
- Represent engineering at the executive table across departments.
- Build the team through hiring, growth paths, and developing tech leads.

## Required Qualifications

- Direct management experience leading engineers with 1:1s, performance reviews, and growth planning.
- Hands-on builder who writes and reviews code.
- Technical depth to engage senior engineers on architecture tradeoffs.
- Experience as the sole engineering voice among business and product leaders.

## Preferred Qualifications

- Ran a comparable-sized team (10-12 engineers).
- Scaled a team from a two-pod structure to a larger structure with leads/managers.
- Track record developing engineers into senior/lead roles.
- Personally sourced and onboarded A-player engineers.
- Healthcare, HIPAA, or SOC 2 compliance experience.
- Ability to structure ambiguous data into defensible conclusions.

## Work Environment

Remote-first with a 9 AM - 6 PM CST working-hours window. Open to candidates in the US and Canada.
