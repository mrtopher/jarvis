## Background
> Founder and CEO of Civis Analytics since 2013. Made his name as Chief Analytics Officer on Barack Obama's 2012 reelection campaign, where he led a ~54-person team of statisticians, engineers, data scientists, and organizers whose work was widely credited with reinventing data-driven national campaigns. Before that, Analytics Director at the DNC and a voter-file role in the 2008 Iowa caucuses. Eric Schmidt backed him to spin the campaign analytics team into Civis. Degree in Economics and Public Policy from the University of Chicago. Aspen Global Leadership Network fellow; Crain's Chicago "40 under 40."

## What they likely care about
- A CTO who **builds**, not just advises. He came up hands-on in a high-pressure, results-only environment (win the election). He will value someone who stays close to systems and delivery.
- Turning a strong data-science services brand into a real, standalone AI **product** company. He needs a technical partner who can own that translation.
- Decisiveness under ambiguity. The JD language ("imperfect information," "operating in ambiguity") reads like his own operating style.
- Trust and cohesion across a small, cross-functional team (product/eng/data/AI) after a period of contraction. He needs a steadying, credible technical leader.
- Mission and integrity. Civis's brand is built on "objective, data-driven truth."
