# Role Summary – Civis Analytics, Chief Technology Officer

- **Location / remote:** Remote, but must currently live in DC, IL, MD, NY, or VA

- **Compensation:** $225,000 - $300,000

## What the role is
> A hands-on, player-coach CTO hired to lead the technical vision, architecture, and delivery as Civis expands its core data platform and launches standalone AI products. The mandate is cohesion: keep the data platform strong and extensible while building a real AI product portfolio on top of it. Set strategy, stay close to systems, hire and develop the team, and be a public technical voice. This is a build-it role, not an advisory one.

## Key requirements
- Real-world, production experience with AI systems (building and scaling AI products in production)
- Hands-on leadership of software / platform / data engineering orgs
- Evolving technical roadmaps across multiple product lines
- Architecting complex, data-driven systems
- Hiring and developing technical teams
- Modern cloud platforms and distributed systems, hands-on
- Decisive under ambiguity; comfortable with imperfect information
