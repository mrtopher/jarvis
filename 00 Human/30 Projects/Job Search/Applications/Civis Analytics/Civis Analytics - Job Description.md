**Job Title:** Chief Technology Officer

**Location:** Chicago, IL (Remote) / Remote in Washington, DC

**Remote Policy:** Remote role with location restrictions. Candidates must currently live in one of the following states: DC, Illinois, Maryland, New York or Virginia.

**Seniority Level:** Executive (C-suite)

**Compensation:** $225,000 - $300,000

---

## Job Description

Civis Analytics seeks a CTO to lead the company's technical vision, architecture, and execution as we expand our platform and launch standalone AI products. The role reports directly to the CEO and involves a critical player–coach role: setting long-term technical direction while staying close to systems, decisions, and delivery.

The CTO owns the cohesion between Civis's core data platform and its growing AI product portfolio while ensuring infrastructure remains strong, reliable, and extensible.

---

## Key Responsibilities

- Be the tech voice within our senior leadership team and guide strategic decision-making
- Own and drive Civis's technical roadmap across platform and AI products
- Lead architecture decisions across platform, data, and AI systems
- Act as a player–coach: remain engaged with technical details while enabling team independence
- Lead and integrate a cross-functional team spanning product, engineering, data, and AI (approximately 15 people initially)
- Provide thought leadership and evaluate emerging technologies
- Be the public face of Civis technology and data science; network and evangelize

---

## Required Qualifications

- Practical, real-world experience working with AI systems and understanding their application in production environments
- Significant hands-on leadership of software, platform, or data engineering organizations
- Demonstrated ability to evolve technical roadmaps across multiple product lines
- Experience architecting complex, data-driven systems
- Comfort engaging directly with technical details when necessary
- Proven track record hiring and developing technical teams
- Clear communication and decisive decision-making capability
- Familiarity with building and scaling AI products in production
- Hands-on experience with modern cloud platforms and distributed systems
- U.S. work authorization

---

## Ideal Candidate Profile

Ideal candidates want to actively shape and build AI products, not just talk about them, enjoy owning strategy while remaining operationally engaged, and are comfortable operating in ambiguity and making decisions with imperfect information.

**Not a fit:** Those preferring purely advisory roles, avoiding hands-on AI work, expecting highly structured environments, or delegating all technical responsibilities.
