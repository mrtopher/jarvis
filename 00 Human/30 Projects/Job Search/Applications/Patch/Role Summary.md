# Role Summary – Patch, Vice President of Engineering

## At a glance
- **Company:** Patch — profitable hyperlocal news platform (~15,000 communities, 25M monthly users, ~3M email subscribers), owned by Hale Global.
- **Role:** Vice President of Engineering — senior-most technology executive, reports to the CEO.
- **Location / remote:** Remote-first, US, distributed team; availability for major production incidents.
- **Seniority:** VP / senior technology executive (executive-team + Board exposure).
- **Compensation:** $225-275K base + discretionary bonus + equity (at/near the $250-300K target band).

## What the role is
> The hands-on senior technology leader for a lean, profitable, AI-forward media company. Owns software engineering, ad technology, Data, and Site Quality. Sets technical direction and delivery, guides a modern-plus-legacy platform (Next.js/Node front, Drupal/PHP core, Fastly/AWS/GCP, Stripe, Auth0/Okta, Typesense/OpenSearch), leads production incidents with senior judgment, and drives practical AI in both customer products and internal newsroom workflows. Partners with a strong, separate Product function without running it. Explicitly a builder's seat, not a management layer — the CEO wants someone who inspects the code and the data.

## Key requirements
- Deep software engineering: JavaScript/TypeScript, React, Next.js, Node.js.
- Personally prototyping, shipping, and operating high-traffic digital products; leading teams that partner with a separate Product function.
- Hands-on AI-assisted engineering, rapid prototypes, agentic workflows, with judgment to productionize safely.
- Strong SQL/data; AWS; distributed or event-driven architectures; complex systems with meaningful legacy.
- People leadership (hiring, coaching, performance, org decisions) + executive communication with CEOs, Boards, cross-functional peers.
- Builder's mindset: curious, direct, low-ego.
