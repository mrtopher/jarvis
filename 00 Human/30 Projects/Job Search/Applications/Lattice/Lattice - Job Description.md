# Senior Director of Engineering - Lattice

**Company:** Lattice
**Department:** Engineering
**Location:** Remote - US

## About the Role

Lattice's Engineering team is building the future of People + AI. We're reimagining our talent management suite - including Reviews, Surveys, and Goals - with AI-powered experiences that unlock capabilities and insights that would otherwise be impossible to deliver. Our products help organizations surface meaningful signals, make better-informed decisions, and ultimately improve the performance outcomes their people and businesses rely on every day.

The Talent Engineering organization is at the forefront of this evolution. They build the best-in-class suite of products across Performance Management, Engagement Surveys, and Goal Setting, helping shape how organizations develop their people and achieve their goals at a time when AI is redefining what's possible in talent management.

## What You Will Do

- Lead the Talent Engineering organization, spanning 50+ engineers and managers across a portfolio of customer-facing products. Making thoughtful tradeoffs across customer needs, product priorities, and engineering investment.
- Coach and develop engineering managers and senior leaders across Lattice Engineering while continuing to raise the bar for leadership, execution, and organizational health.
- Ensure a delightful, reliable experience for Lattice customers around the globe while simultaneously enabling a rapid pace of product development.
- Partner closely with our Product and Design partners to define, plan, and prioritize our product strategy and roadmap that translates into outcomes for customers.
- Represent Lattice in conversations with key prospects and customers.
- Collaborate closely with the broader Engineering leadership team to shape department-wide strategy and goals.
- Scale the systems, processes, and organizational practices that enable multiple teams to plan effectively, deliver reliably, and learn quickly.

## What You Will Bring to the Table

- 10+ years of engineering management experience, including at least 5 years leading an engineering organization composed of multiple teams and multiple layers of leadership.
- Experience managing engineering investment across a portfolio of products and making thoughtful tradeoffs when resources are constrained.
- A history of shipping high-quality products where quality, trust, reliability, and scalability are essential to customer success.
- A high degree of autonomy, ownership, and sound judgment, with the ability to operate with a CEO mindset.
- Demonstrated ability to develop engineering leaders, including both individual contributors and managers.
- An eye for great products and the ability to work cross-functionally to effectively deliver them.
- Strong communication and executive presence, with the ability to influence decisions across Engineering, Product, Design, and company leadership.
- Curiosity about AI and its potential to improve how organizations develop their people.
