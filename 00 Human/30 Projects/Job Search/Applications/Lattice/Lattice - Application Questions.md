# Application Questions - Senior Director of Engineering, Lattice

## Q: Describe one difficult tradeoff you made across multiple customer-facing products or teams. What were the competing priorities, what decision did you make, and what was the outcome?
> 199 words. Industry Dive portfolio-vs-platform tradeoff. VOICE-clean.

At Industry Dive I grew one team of 8 into five cross-functional teams of more than 40, each owning properties inside a portfolio of more than 100 sites used by millions. Every team wanted more engineers on features. Product and customers wanted the same.

The competing priorities were real. Individual property teams had near-term feature asks that mattered to customers. At the same time, the whole portfolio was slowing under duplicated work, uneven reliability, and manual release steps that no single team owned or was funded to fix.

I pulled a slice of capacity off feature work and stood up a shared foundation every property could build on: paved-road CI/CD, observability, incident response, and reusable services. It meant asking each team to give up near-term velocity for a payoff with no customer-facing headline.

The outcome held up. We kept high availability across those 100-plus properties and cut time-to-ship 35 percent across the portfolio, because teams reused the foundation instead of rebuilding it. The teams that sacrificed early got it back with interest.

The lesson stayed with me. When a portfolio fights itself, the best tradeoff is often to slow everyone briefly so you can speed them all for good.
