# Role Summary - Senior Director of Engineering, Lattice

## At a glance
- **Company:** Lattice (People-management SaaS, "People + AI")
- **Role:** Senior Director of Engineering, Talent Engineering org
- **Location / remote:** Remote, US
- **Seniority:** Senior Director, leads 50+ engineers and managers, multiple layers of leadership
- **Compensation:** $283K to $354K base plus incentive stock options (above the $250-300K target)

## What the role is
> Own the Talent Engineering organization: 50+ engineers and managers building Lattice's flagship customer-facing products (Performance Management/Reviews, Engagement Surveys, Goal Setting). Make portfolio tradeoffs across customer needs, product priorities, and engineering investment. Coach and develop engineering managers and senior leaders, raise the bar on execution and org health, keep the products reliable at global scale while shipping fast, and partner tightly with Product and Design on strategy and roadmap. The distinctive through-line is AI: this org is reimagining talent management with AI-powered experiences, so the role is as much about landing AI in production as it is about running a large org. Reports up through Engineering leadership (most likely SVP Engineering Sophie Roberts, possibly via a VP).

## Key requirements
- 10+ years engineering management, including 5+ years leading a multi-team, multi-layer engineering organization
- Managing engineering investment across a portfolio of products, with disciplined tradeoffs under constraint
- History of shipping high-quality products where quality, trust, reliability, and scalability matter
- High autonomy, ownership, sound judgment, a CEO mindset
- Demonstrated development of engineering leaders (both ICs and managers)
- Cross-functional product delivery with Product and Design
- Strong communication and executive presence across Engineering, Product, Design, and company leadership
- Curiosity about AI and its potential to improve how organizations develop their people

### Why apply
- **AI is the strategic bet and this org owns the surface area.** Reimagining Reviews, Surveys, and Goals with AI maps directly to the Dual Logic agentic-AI-in-production work and the "ship AI that holds up, not slop" POV.
- **Real scale.** 50+ engineers with multiple leadership layers clears the recurring small-team pass line comfortably and matches the 8-to-40+ scaling story.
- **Comp above target.** $283K to $354K base plus equity sits above the $250-300K base target.
- **Values and hiring-manager fit.** Likely HM Sophie Roberts (SVP Eng) has written "AI doesn't make great companies, people do," and champions business-tied engineering. That is Chris's stance, not a stretch.
- **Remote US**, so it runs in parallel with the consulting business.

### Why not
- **Layoffs.** Two rounds in 13 months (Nov 2024 ~14%, Oct/Nov 2025 ~10-15%). Org stability and the mandate's durability are fair questions.
- **Equity risk.** $3B valuation is a stale Jan 2022 mark; ISO upside may be soft or carry down-round risk. Weigh the package as mostly cash.
- **Title framing.** Senior Director is nominally below his SVP/CTO titles; frame it as scope (50+ org, category leader) not a step down.
- **Domain.** No direct HR-tech experience (not required; frame the pattern as B2B SaaS at scale).

## If applying
- **Angle to lead with:** AI in governed production for a large product org, plus human-centered AI (make strong engineers and strong managers stronger, not replace them). Pair the org-scaling track record (8 to 40+, managers of managers) with landing AI features customers trust.
