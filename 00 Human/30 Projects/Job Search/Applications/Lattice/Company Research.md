# Company Research – Lattice

## Snapshot
- **What they do:** People management SaaS platform for HR and managers. Core products cover performance management and reviews, engagement surveys, goals/OKRs, compensation, career growth, analytics, and a newer HRIS. Positions itself around a "People + AI" vision where AI augments managers and HR teams.
- **Size / stage:** Roughly 570 to 770 employees depending on source and date (estimates conflict; 574 per getLatka 2026, 770 per Contrary). Private, late-stage VC-backed. Founded January 2015, HQ San Francisco.
- **Funding / ownership:** ~$328M to $332M total raised (sources vary). Last priced round Series F, January 2022, $175M at a $3B valuation. Investors include Dragoneer (Series F lead), Tiger Global, Founders Fund, Thrive Capital, Khosla Ventures, Shasta Ventures. Note: $3B is a 2022 mark and likely stale given the 2024 to 2025 downturn and layoffs (unverified current valuation).
- **HQ / locations:** San Francisco, CA. London office (EMEA) opened September 2021.
- **Remote policy:** Hires remote US roles (the target role is Remote US). Distributed with hub offices.

## Products & business model
> SaaS subscription, priced per person per month. Contrary reports Performance Management + OKRs & Goals bundled around $11/person/month, with Engagement and Grow each adding ~$4/person/month (2024 figures, may be outdated). Additional revenue from Lattice Advisory Services.
>
> Product suite: Performance Management (reviews, 1:1s, feedback, praise, updates), Engagement (pulse surveys, eNPS, onboarding/exit), Goals & OKRs, Compensation (benchmarking, cycle management), Grow (career development, competency matrices), Analytics dashboards, and HRIS (newer expansion into core HR system of record).
>
> Target customers: mid-market and enterprise, desk-based knowledge-worker companies. 5,000+ organizations. Named customers historically include Slack, Robinhood, Gusto, Allbirds, Monzo, HelloFresh, Webflow. Revenue estimated ~$127M ARR (getLatka, unverified).
>
> The Talent Engineering org (the hiring org for this role) owns Performance/Reviews, Engagement Surveys, and Goals, which are the flagship legacy products and the surface area where most AI features are landing.

## Tech stack
> From a Lattice "Senior Software Engineer, Compensation" job posting (Thrive Capital job board): React, Relay Modern, GraphQL, Node, and PostgreSQL, with modern languages including TypeScript, Ruby, and Python. So the stack is a React/Relay/GraphQL front end over a Node/Ruby/Python service layer on Postgres. Cloud provider not confirmed in public postings (commonly AWS for this type of company, unverified).
>
> AI features (2025 to 2026) build on LLMs and an agent framework, plus "Lattice MCP" exposing performance data to external AI tools (Claude, ChatGPT, Glean, Slack) via Model Context Protocol.

## Culture & values
> Mission language: "turn employees into high performers, managers into leaders, and companies into the best places to work," and to make "work meaningful." Runs a large HR practitioner community ("Resources for Humans," 24,000+ members). Offers paid "recharge days." Note: the site does not publish an explicit named-values list (open item).
>
> Under CEO Sarah Franklin (ex-Salesforce), the company leans hard into a "People + AI" narrative. Culture reviews are split (see Reputation): historically praised for smart, kind people, more recently strained by leadership changes and repeated layoffs.

## Reputation
> Glassdoor: ~3.x overall across 229 reviews. Work-life balance ~3.8, culture & values ~3.4, career opportunities ~3.2, comp & benefits ~3.9. 56% would recommend to a friend. Sentiment is divided. Positive reviews cite talented, humble colleagues and good benefits. Negative reviews from late 2025 cite "poor leadership decisions," a layoff affecting ~10% of the company in November 2025, and claims that senior leadership "destroyed the culture in recent years." (Glassdoor numbers approximate, pulled from search summaries, not directly verified on-page.)
>
> Reputational overhang: the July 2024 "digital workers" episode (below) drew national criticism and is still referenced in HR press.

## Recent news
- **June 10, 2026 (Lattiverse):** Launched "Workforce Intelligence for the AI Era" including Lattice MCP (performance data into external AI tools), AI Leverage Insights (links AI usage to performance quality, not token volume), an AI Agent in 1:1s for manager coaching, Evidence-Based Review Drafts grounded in real performance data, and a Voice modality for the AI Agent. CEO framing: "People + AI is the new way to work."
- **Fall/Winter 2025 release:** AI Meeting Agent (auto-joins 1:1s to take notes, summarize, capture action items), employee signatures, Workday integration.
- **March 2025:** Debuted the Lattice AI Agent for HR alongside reported HRIS customer growth. Talent Data insights rolled out for 1:1s, Updates, Feedback, with expansion to Reviews, Engagement, Grow, Goals planned into early 2026.
- **Layoffs:** ~14% cut (approx 125 people) in November 2024; a further ~10 to 15% cut in October/November 2025 (sources conflict on exact size and month). Repeated reductions over two years.
- **July 2024, "digital workers" controversy:** Announced giving AI agents employee records and org-chart placement as "digital workers." Public backlash within days led Lattice to suspend the plan. Predates 2025 but remains a live reputational reference (the prompt dated this to 2025; it was actually mid-2024).
- **Leadership:** Founder Jack Altman stepped down as CEO in December 2023 and is now Chairman/Co-founder; he launched his own VC firm, Alt Capital, in 2025. Sarah Franklin (ex-Salesforce President and CMO) became CEO in January 2024.

## Green flags
- AI is genuinely central to the product roadmap, not bolted on. The Talent Engineering org owns exactly the surfaces (Reviews, Engagement, Goals) where the "People + AI" features ship, so a Senior Director there sits on the strategic growth bet. Strong fit for an AI-forward engineering leader.
- Modern, coherent stack (React/Relay/GraphQL/Node/TypeScript/Ruby/Python/Postgres) with a real MCP/agent investment, so the AI work is architectural, not superficial.
- Comp band ($283K to $354K) is in range for a senior eng leadership role and roughly aligns with Chris's target band.
- Established category leader with 5,000+ customers and real ARR, not a pre-product-market-fit gamble.
- Human-centered "AI augments people" positioning aligns with Chris's own framing and Dual Logic's values.

## Red flags
- Two rounds of layoffs in 13 months (Nov 2024 and Oct/Nov 2025) signal financial or growth pressure and a strained org. Expect to lead through cost discipline, not pure expansion.
- $3B valuation is a January 2022 peak-market mark. A flat or down round is plausible; equity may be worth less than the headline suggests (verify current 409A/valuation directly).
- Split, declining culture sentiment on Glassdoor with specific complaints about senior leadership. A new senior director inherits morale repair.
- The 2024 "digital workers" misstep shows a pattern of getting ahead of the market on AI messaging. A candidate should be ready to ground AI ambition in what customers actually trust.
- Engineering leadership titles are unsettled in public sources: lattice.com/about lists Sophie Roberts as SVP of Engineering, while third-party profiles (ZoomInfo, Wiza) still show Stephen Poletto as CTO/VP Engineering. Reporting line for this role is unclear and should be confirmed (open item).
