# Company Research – Yourgi

## Snapshot
- **What they do:** Pet-care marketplace connecting pet parents with care. Two supply sides: a marketplace of independent pros (grooming, boarding, training, daycare) who run their business on Yourgi Pro, and Yourgi Connect, which links pet parents directly into brick-and-mortar centers (Destination Pet, Four Walls, and a growing national network) for live availability, reservation, and real-time booking.
- **Size / stage:** Early-stage 0-to-1 venture. ~30 engineers. Live in 5 markets (initial launches Dallas + Denver, per press). Founded 2022 as Yourgi, Inc.
- **Funding / ownership:** Subsidiary of **Destination Pet Holdings, LLC** (Irving, TX; ~533 employees; PE-backed — LetterOne acquired 2019, later backers include Opus Equity Partners and McLarty Capital Partners; ~$40M+ raised at the parent). Yourgi itself shows no standalone venture round — the "stable funding" in the JD comes from the parent, not an outside raise. This is a **corporate-backed internal venture**, not a classic VC startup.
- **HQ / locations:** Highlands Ranch, CO (Yourgi); parent Destination Pet HQ in Irving, TX.
- **Remote policy:** Remote, US only. No visa sponsorship. Must work US business hours; some travel for offsites. Runs on Microsoft Teams.

## Products & business model
> Two-sided marketplace plus a network-inventory product. The marketplace matches pet parents with independent pros (Yourgi Pro is the provider-side SaaS). **Yourgi Connect** is the strategic bet: it turns the parent company's national network of physical pet-care centers into online, bookable inventory (live availability, reserve, real-time book). Web + native iOS and Android on both the pet-parent and provider/center sides. The company is scaling from first markets to national coverage. Yourgi Connect gives Destination Pet's physical footprint an owned digital demand channel — the classic roll-up-plus-marketplace play.

## Tech stack
> Not publicly documented. From the JD: web + native iOS/Android apps, matching and booking systems, real-time availability/reservation against physical centers, CI/CD with feature flags, continuous deployment, DORA-metrics discipline, AI-assisted development wired into the dev workflow (spec-to-code-to-review, automated test generation, evals in CI). Platform-services / shared-backend-capability model. Microsoft Teams for collaboration (possible Azure lean, unconfirmed).

## Culture & values
> Mission-driven ("Earth's most trusted pet care connector"; "pets are family"). Trust, quality, convenience, background-checked caregivers. Startup mentality with stable (parent) funding. AI-first delivery as an explicit operating value. Engineering-owned quality and end-to-end team ownership are stated cultural goals, not just process asks.

## Reputation

Parent Destination Pet has a mixed-to-average reputation typical of a PE-backed, acquisition-driven pet-services roll-up (services workforce, not a software shop). No engineering-org reviews found. Treat culture as unverified — a genuine diligence gap.

## Green flags
- **Clean identity + role-shape match.** Consumer marketplace + provider tools + matching/booking + AI-first delivery + remote-US, reporting to a CPTO — this is Chris's exact VP-Eng lane (mirrors Guild, Patch, Ocrolus, ReWork).
- **AI-first delivery is the headline mandate.** Chris's Dual Logic identity (AI in the SDLC, evals + guardrails, throughput without slop) is a near-verbatim answer to the JD's central ask.
- **0-to-1 with real backing.** Startup autonomy without the runway cliff of an unfunded seed company — the parent bankrolls it and supplies the physical inventory.
- **Reasonable scope.** ~30 engineers / 6 leaders reporting in is squarely in Chris's proven manager-of-managers range (Industry Dive 8→40+), not a step beyond it.
- **Marketplace + booking dynamics** are a differentiator the JD calls out and Chris can speak to (consumer-scale at Industry Dive, revenue-critical multi-tenant at Pipeline 360).

## Red flags
- **Comp not stated.** No range in the JD and no public band. Biggest unknown — confirm early.
- **CPTO (the hiring manager and boss) is not publicly named.** Can't research the person Chris would report to. Fit with that leader is unknowable pre-screen.
- **Corporate-venture politics.** A 0-to-1 product inside a PE-backed physical-services roll-up can mean shifting priorities, parent-company dependencies (Yourgi Connect needs Destination Pet's operational buy-in), and pressure to show fast ROI. "Startup mentality with stable funding" can also mean lean budget + high expectations.
- **Mobile depth is a partial.** JD leans on native iOS/Android release management (release trains, staged rollout); Chris's hands-on depth is web/backend/AI, mobile is oversight-level. Honest partial, not a fabrication.
- **Real-time booking against physical inventory** is a specific domain (availability, reservation, capacity) Chris hasn't built directly — adjacent to multi-tenant/marketplace work but new.
- **Culture unverified.** No engineering-org signal; parent is a services company, not a software culture.
