# Role Summary – Yourgi VP of Engineering

## At a glance
- **Company:** Yourgi (pet-care marketplace; subsidiary of PE-backed Destination Pet Holdings)
- **Role:** VP of Engineering (reports to CPTO; 6 engineering leaders/managers direct; ~30 engineers)
- **Location / remote:** Remote, US only. No visa sponsorship. US business hours; some travel.
- **Seniority:** VP, manager-of-managers, full accountability for how engineering is structured, ships, and grows.
- **Compensation:** Not listed. Biggest open unknown.

## What the role is
> Lead ~30 engineers building a two-sided pet-care marketplace: consumer app, provider tools (Yourgi Pro), matching and booking systems, and marketplace surfaces, plus **Yourgi Connect**, a 0-to-1 product that turns a national network of physical pet-care centers into real-time bookable inventory. Own org design (teams that own a customer journey end to end), make AI-assisted development the default across the SDLC, build engineering-owned quality on automated testing + CI/CD, run the delivery cadence and DORA metrics, own the budget/headcount plan, and take Yourgi Connect from first markets to national scale on platform services that don't get rebuilt per market.

## Key requirements
- 12+ yrs software engineering, incl. 6+ yrs leading orgs through managers/leads (not just ICs)
- Track record of teams owning a product surface end to end and decomposing their own work
- Direct experience adopting AI-assisted development across a team, with a POV on throughput/review/quality
- Engineering-owned quality shifted upstream via automated testing + CI/CD
- Running remote engineering teams with a predictable operating rhythm
- Owning an engineering budget/headcount plan; tradeoffs with Product + Finance; DORA discipline
- US-based/authorized, US business hours
- Differentiators: scaled AI-assisted dev across a full team; marketplace/gig/consumer + two-sided matching/booking; mobile + web at scale incl. native release management; platform/core-services thinking; 0-to-1 to national scale
