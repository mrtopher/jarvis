# Company Research – Sensei Labs

## Snapshot
- **What they do:** Build Conductor, an enterprise strategy execution platform that governs program value across large transformation portfolios. Positioned as the AI-powered operating system for strategic programs, with an AI layer called Harmony woven through the product.
- **Size / stage:** Small, deliberately lean team. Engineering is roughly 7 people. Company headcount is small (tens, not hundreds). More than ten years in market, so this is an established product run by a small crew, not an early startup.
- **Funding / ownership:** Founded 2015 in Toronto. Modest disclosed funding (about $4.6M total; a multi-million round led by Plymouth Growth). Privately held, founder-led. Not a heavily VC-funded burn machine.
- **HQ / locations:** Toronto, Ontario, Canada.
- **Remote policy:** Toronto or remote. Runs a 4-day work week (Fridays off).

## Products & business model
> Conductor is enterprise SaaS sold to Fortune 500 transformation offices, global consultancies, and private equity value creation teams. It covers the full lifecycle from portfolio planning through benefits realization: plan, prioritize, execute, realize. Solution areas include PE value creation, strategic portfolio management, enterprise transformation, M&A integration, PPM, procurement, continuous improvement, and ESG. Harmony is their enterprise AI assistant (launched 2024, billed as a first-mover for AI in transformation platforms). Single-tenant architecture, SOC-2 compliant. Currently mid-flight on a full platform rewrite ("Conductor Modern") with a customer migration and old-code sunset planned over the next year.

## Tech stack
> Microsoft-centric. CTO is a career .NET developer, and the integration ecosystem named in the JD is Microsoft 365 / SAP / Jira / Power BI. JD says hands-on depth in Azure and/or GCP is essential, plus Kubernetes, IaC, CI/CD, networking and identity, observability. Single-tenant SaaS, SOC-2. Reasonable read: .NET/C# backend on Azure, single-tenant deployments per enterprise customer, now being re-platformed for Conductor Modern.

## Culture & values
> Small, transparent, founder-led. Open-door culture from the CEO down, monthly all-hands, flexible hours, 4-day work week. When they bought the company back from WeWork in 2019 they made employees co-owners, so ownership and long-tenure culture run deep.

## Reputation
> Glassdoor 4.8/5 across ~60 reviews, 98% would recommend, 4.9 for culture and values, 4.6 for work-life balance, 4.5 for career opportunities. Great Place to Work certified in Canada. Strong, consistent signal.

## Recent news
- Full platform rewrite underway ("Conductor Modern"), with customer migration and legacy sunset planned over roughly the next year.
- Launched Harmony AI in the Conductor platform (2024), marketed as the first AI assistant in an enterprise transformation platform.

## Green flags
- AI-first SDLC and agentic product AI are both central to the role. That is Chris's exact thesis.
- Hands-on player-coach VP seat with real ownership, not a manager-of-managers slot.
- Healthy, well-reviewed culture and a 4-day week.
- Established product with real Fortune 500 revenue, not a pre-product gamble.
- Third leg of a three-legged leadership stool (Eng alongside established Product and GTM leaders): a clear, senior mandate.

## Red flags
- Microsoft/.NET + Azure shop. Chris is AWS/GCP. JD allows GCP, but the surrounding ecosystem (Power BI, M365, .NET CTO) leans Microsoft.
- Very small team and modest funding: limited resources, and the VP is expected to be in the code daily. Great for impact, thin for support.
- Mid-migration with a legacy sunset: inherited complexity and migration risk in year one.
- Domain (PPM / EPMO / transformation) is unfamiliar territory; listed as nice-to-have, not required.
- Cross-border remote (Canadian company). Worth confirming they hire US-based remote and how comp/employment is handled.
