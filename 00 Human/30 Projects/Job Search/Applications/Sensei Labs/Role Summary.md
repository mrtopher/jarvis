# Role Summary – Sensei Labs, Vice President, Engineering

## At a glance
- **Company:** Sensei Labs (maker of Conductor, an AI-powered enterprise strategy execution platform)
- **Role:** Vice President, Engineering (reports to CTO)
- **Location / remote:** Toronto or remote. 4-day work week.
- **Seniority:** VP, hands-on player-coach. Owns ~7 (4 engineers, 2 QE, Platform/DevOps). Not a manager of managers.
- **Compensation:** Not listed.

## What the role is
> Own engineering execution for Conductor: team structure, delivery, technical strategy, and architecture. This is a hands-on seat, in the code and the reviews while also accountable for delivery, hiring, and team health. Two big mandates: (1) finish the Conductor Modern rewrite, migrate customers, and sunset the legacy codebase, and (2) rebuild how the team ships using AI-assisted development so a small team ships like a large one. Also owns cloud platform reliability, security posture, and how Harmony AI evolves in the product.

## Key requirements
- Hands-on cloud SaaS architecture and operations at real scale (app layer and infra).
- Azure and/or GCP depth, Kubernetes, IaC, CI/CD, networking/identity, observability, cost discipline.
- Single-tenant vs multi-tenant tradeoffs, data isolation, enterprise-load performance, SOC-2 reality.
- Product instinct in complex B2B; can push back on a spec.
- Player-coach leadership of a 5 to 20 person team; hiring, hard feedback, tuning delivery.
- AI fluency both directions: shipped LLM-backed features AND rebuilt team workflow with AI-assisted SDLC.
- Startup temperament: strategist who also fixes the flaky test.

## Alignment to my goals

It is a VP of Engineering seat that rewards exactly the thing Chris has been building his brand around: AI-assisted SDLC and trustworthy agentic AI in production. Remote-friendly with a 4-day week. ## Recommendation: **APPLY**
> Strong fit on the two mandates that matter most to them (AI-first SDLC and shipping fast with a small team), backed by a genuinely hands-on track record. The technical gaps are real but bridgeable and, per the JD's own wording, not disqualifying.

### Why apply
- The role's centerpiece (a small team that ships like a large one via AI-assisted development) is Chris's exact thesis and lived practice.
- Player-coach VP with real ownership matches how Chris operates now, not a manager-of-managers slot he would have to shrink into.
- Shipped agentic/RAG features with evals and guardrails maps straight onto Harmony AI's roadmap needs.
- Established product, real Fortune 500 revenue, excellent culture, 4-day week. Low-drama, high-impact.

### Why not
- Microsoft/.NET + Azure shop; Chris is AWS/GCP. JD allows GCP, but the surrounding ecosystem leans Microsoft and the CTO is a .NET dev.
- Small team and modest funding mean thin support and daily hands-on load. Fine if that is what he wants; a step change from leading 40.
- Domain (enterprise PPM/transformation) is new.
- Cross-border remote unknowns (US hire? comp band?).

## If applying
- **Angle to lead with:** AI-assisted SDLC that holds quality as code volume climbs (agent skills, eval harnesses, review gates), plus fast zero-to-production delivery under real stakes. Pair it with agentic/RAG production experience for Harmony.
- **Gaps to address:** Frame cloud depth around architecture and operations principles that transfer from AWS to Azure/GCP, and lean on GCP. Signal genuine appetite for a hands-on small-team seat. Do not overclaim Azure or the PPM domain.
