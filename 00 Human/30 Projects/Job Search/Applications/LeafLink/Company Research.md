# Company Research - LeafLink

## Snapshot
- **What they do:** Largest unified B2B cannabis wholesale platform. Marketplace connecting cannabis brands, distributors, and retailers, plus a fintech and logistics layer (payments, financing, order management, insights).
- **Size / stage:** Mid-stage, growth. Headcount reported in the ~120-200 range across sources (post-consolidation). Processes $5B+ in annual wholesale cannabis orders across 30+ North American markets. Reported to serve roughly half of the U.S. wholesale cannabis industry.
- **Funding / ownership:** VC-backed, ~$500M+ raised across rounds. Investors include Founders Fund, Thrive Capital, Nosara Capital, Lerer Hippeau. Raised $100M in early 2023 alongside leadership changes. Acquired competitor Leaf Trade in November 2024.
- **HQ / locations:** New York, NY. Distributed workforce.
- **Remote policy:** This role is fully remote.

## Products & business model
> Two-sided B2B marketplace for wholesale cannabis, monetized through the transaction layer plus adjacent fintech services (LeafLink Financial for seller loans, payments, and logistics/distribution). Business model rides on cannabis wholesale GMV and take rate, with expansion into financial services on top of the marketplace. Revenue is tied to a federally illegal, state-regulated industry, which constrains banking, payments, and capital access.

## Tech stack
> From the JD: Python, Kubernetes, Docker, AWS-managed and self-hosted services (EKS, ECR, R53, SES, ElastiCache, RDS), infrastructure-as-code (Terraform, Ansible), CI/CD (Jenkins, CircleCI, ArgoCD, Spinnaker or similar), event-driven and distributed architectures, service-oriented/API-driven design, containerized and serverless patterns. SOC 2 compliance program in place or being built. Actively pushing agentic AI and LLM-powered code generation internally.

## Culture & values
> Publicly markets an employee-centric culture (Built In NYC Best Places to Work 2021, Fast Company innovator lists). Internal reviews tell a harsher story: understaffed and stretched engineering, low morale in stretches, and leadership churn. New CEO Ashwin Raj (Sept 2025) suggests a reset is underway.

## Reputation
> Glassdoor around 2.9/5 with roughly 24% recommending to a friend. Recurring themes: engineering understaffed and underpaid, burnout and churn, weak performance management, and skepticism about leadership. Positives that recur: strong individual teammates, unlimited PTO, fully remote. Cannabis-sector volatility (industry-wide layoffs 2022-2023) colors the sentiment.

## Recent news
- Sept 2025: Ashwin Raj appointed CEO (marketplace/fintech veteran, prior CEO of ezCater).
- Nov 2024: Acquired Leaf Trade, consolidating wholesale cannabis marketplaces.
- Sept 2024: Expanded LeafLink Financial arm for seller financing/loans.
- Apr 2024: Steffne (Stevie) Palmateer promoted to Chief Technology Officer.
- Early 2023: Raised $100M and reshuffled leadership.
- Dec 2022: Laid off ~80 employees (~44% of staff at the time) amid cannabis-industry headwinds.

## Green flags
- Compensation band ($240-270K) sits inside Chris's target range.
- Fully remote, which fits running the consulting practice in parallel.
- Explicitly wants an agentic-AI / LLM-code-generation champion, which matches Chris's current mode.
- Clear market position: real scale ($5B GMV, 30+ markets, ~50% of U.S. wholesale).
- Senior-most technical seat with real org-building authority and executive/board exposure.
- New CEO signals a mandate for change, which favors an incoming leader who wants to reshape the org.

## Red flags
- Cannabis is federally illegal and capital-constrained; sector-wide volatility and past layoffs.
- Weak Glassdoor sentiment, especially for engineering (understaffed, burnout, churn).
- Leadership turnover history (CEO and CTO changes inside three years).
- Turnaround-flavored role: inheriting technical debt, morale, and staffing gaps, not a greenfield.
- Equity upside is tied to a hard-to-value, regulation-exposed private company.
