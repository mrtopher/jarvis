# Role Summary - LeafLink Head of Engineering

## At a glance
- **Company:** LeafLink (largest B2B cannabis wholesale platform, $5B+ GMV)
- **Role:** Head of Engineering
- **Location / remote:** Fully remote
- **Seniority:** Senior-most technical leader; reports to executive leadership, partners with Product, sits at the leadership table
- **Compensation:** $240,000 - $270,000 base, plus stock options and benefits

## What the role is
> Own and run the entire engineering organization. Set the operating model, hire and develop engineering managers, and make the architecture, tooling, and headcount calls. Build engineering-wide standards for planning and delivery, own architecture, infrastructure, security, technical debt, and the SOC 2 posture, and manage the engineering budget. Partner with Product on the roadmap and champion agentic AI and LLM code generation inside delivery. The seat is at a pivotal size: big enough to need real structure, small enough that one leader still touches everything.

## Key requirements
- 12+ years software engineering, 7+ years leading engineering managers (not just ICs)
- Built or scaled an engineering org from the ground up or through a major scaling inflection
- Set technical vision and a roadmap executives and the board trust
- Communicate engineering strategy, risk, and cost to non-technical executives and board
- Hands-on background with AWS-managed services, IaC (Terraform, Ansible), containers (Docker, Kubernetes)
- Own security/compliance including SOC 2 controls and audit management
- Strong CI/CD and developer-experience point of view
- API management and service-oriented, API-driven architecture at scale
- Manage budget, vendors, and cost efficiency
- Marketplace, e-commerce, or B2B SaaS experience a plus
