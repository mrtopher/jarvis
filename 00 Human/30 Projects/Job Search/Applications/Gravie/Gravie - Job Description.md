# VP, Platform & Architecture at Gravie

**Job Title:** VP, Platform & Architecture

**Company:** Gravie

**Location:** Remote (United States)

**Employment Type:** Full-time

**Compensation:** $270,000–$360,000 annually; offers equity and bonus

---

## About Gravie

Gravie creates health benefits for small and midsize businesses and their employees. The company was founded by industry veterans with experience scaling market-leading healthcare companies. It is well-capitalized by top-tier investors.

Key facts: "Customer satisfaction rates among employees using Gravie health plans consistently rank above 80% – nearly 40 points above the industry average." The culture is "non-hierarchical, merit-driven" with people who "thrive working in a high-performance, fast-paced environment."

---

## Role Summary

The VP of Platform & Architecture leads shared technology foundations enabling scalable products, engineering effectiveness, and AI adoption. This is a hands-on, player-coach role requiring deep engagement in "architecture, platform design, and emerging technology, personally engaging in critical technical decisions."

---

## Key Responsibilities

- Define and execute platform, architecture, and cloud strategy for scalable product development and business growth
- Own a prioritized platform roadmap informed by product strategy, engineering needs, and business priorities
- Lead architecture across shared services and product domains; resolve systemic technical risks and guide cross-team decisions
- Engage personally in architecture, design reviews, technology evaluation, prototyping, and complex problem-solving
- Establish technical standards and practices improving reliability, security, resilience, developer experience, and cloud cost efficiency
- Build Gravie's AI platform: secure model access, agent orchestration, retrieval and knowledge services, evaluation, observability, guardrails, and model lifecycle management
- Lead adoption of agentic development across Engineering, including tooling, standards, training, workflow redesign, and measurement of productivity improvements
- Establish shared quality engineering practices, tooling, and automation
- Lead product support operations: processes, tooling, and cross-functional model for issue identification, triage, escalation, root-cause analysis, and resolution
- Partner with Customer Operations to deploy AI agents and automation improving support effectiveness and customer experience
- Build and develop a high-performing organization while partnering with Product, Engineering, Data, Security, Privacy, Legal, Operations, and business leadership

---

## Required Qualifications

- Significant leadership experience in platform engineering, architecture, infrastructure, and developer experience
- Demonstrated ability to operate as a hands-on technical executive, moving between executive strategy, architecture, system design, technical reviews, prototyping, and detailed engineering problem-solving
- Demonstrated success building shared platforms used by multiple product and engineering teams
- Track record improving engineering productivity, platform reliability, and operational effectiveness
- Experience building technical foundations for AI capabilities embedded in customer-facing products
- Experience with generative AI architecture, agent orchestration, retrieval, evaluation, observability, and production operations
- Experience driving adoption of agentic development practices across an engineering organization
- Experience applying AI agents in customer service, internal operations, or software development
- Experience establishing or leading product support operations, production issue management, or similar cross-functional capabilities
- Ability to operate effectively in a regulated environment and partner with Security, Privacy, Legal, Risk, and Compliance
- Strong organizational leadership, executive communication, financial management, and vendor-management skills

---

## Preferred Qualifications

- Experience in insurance, healthcare, financial services, or another regulated industry
