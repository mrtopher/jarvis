## Background

**Sarb Singh — most likely hiring manager**
- VP of Software Engineering at Gravie since July 2022. Scope explicitly spans Software Engineering, Technical Product Management, and Solutions Architecture.
- ~23+ years in engineering leadership across healthcare, telecom, and cloud/security.
- Prior roles: VP of Software Engineering at Spok (Jan 2018–Jul 2022); Director of Software Engineering, Patient Care Software (Neuromodulation) at Medtronic (2013–2016); Sr. Director of Software Development at Symantec (2004–2013); Senior Consultant at BT/Syntegra (1999–2004).
- Early career: Research Assistant in Artificial Intelligence, Robotics & Computer Vision at the University of Minnesota (1997–1999). Holds a Computer Science degree (advanced degrees in CS/Engineering) from the University of Minnesota.
- Regulated-industry pedigree throughout (medical devices at Medtronic, security at Symantec, healthcare at Gravie) — matches the JD's "operate effectively in a regulated environment" requirement.

**Michael Cameron — Chief Architect (secondary hiring manager / almost certain interviewer)**
- ~12 years at Gravie; came up as software engineer, then held the CTO title, now Chief Architect. Reports to the VP of Software Engineering.
- Owns architecture direction; strong public Clojure/ClojureScript advocate (Gravie's stack is AWS + Clojure + Kinesis, continuous delivery). Personally recruits senior/staff/principal engineers via LinkedIn.
- 25+ years in healthcare product development; University of Minnesota (1996–2009); former Guthrie Theater board member. Long-tenured culture-carrier — his buy-in on architecture philosophy will matter.

**Executive context (who they answer to)**
- **Steve Wolin** — CEO (appointed Feb 2025), former COO of Oscar Health. Focus: expanding ICHRA, network partnerships, national brokerage/channel relationships. Market- and growth-oriented; sets the "why."
- *Note:* Some sources (The Org, cached) still show Abir Sen as CEO. The current, gravie.com-confirmed CEO is Steve Wolin; Sen is Co-Founder/Chairman.

## What they likely care about
- **Shared platforms that unblock product teams** — Singh's remit is scaling engineering delivery; the JD centers on platforms "used by multiple product and engineering teams." Proof of leverage matters more than raw headcount.
- **AI platform done responsibly in a regulated business** — secure model access, agent orchestration, retrieval/knowledge services, evaluation, observability, guardrails, model lifecycle. Health data = HIPAA/PHI; governance is non-negotiable. This is the strongest overlap with Chris.
- **Agentic development adoption across Engineering** — tooling, standards, training, workflow redesign, and *measured* productivity gains. They want someone who has driven this, not just theorized it.
- **Reliability, security, resilience, developer experience, cloud cost efficiency** — classic platform-engineering scorecard.
- **Hands-on player-coach** — the JD stresses personal engagement in architecture, design reviews, prototyping. In a "non-hierarchical, merit-driven" culture, a builder-CTO who still writes/reviews code will resonate. Cameron especially values this.
- **Product support / production issue operations** — triage, escalation, root-cause, plus AI agents for customer support. An under-glamorous but weighted part of the role.
- **Regulated-industry fluency** — partnering with Security, Privacy, Legal, Risk, Compliance.

## Talking points
- Lead with the **AI platform**: how you stood up secure model access, agent orchestration, retrieval/knowledge services, and — critically — **evaluation, observability, and guardrails** so agents run in *governed production* on regulated/sensitive data. This is the JD's centerpiece and Chris's exact wheelhouse.
- Bring a **measured agentic-development adoption story**: rolled out agentic tooling/standards/training across an engineering org and quantified the productivity lift. The JD explicitly asks for "measurement of productivity improvements."
- Frame **platform as leverage for product teams**, not empire-building — reliability, developer experience, and cloud-cost efficiency as shared-service outcomes. Fits Singh's scaling mandate and the non-hierarchical culture.
- Emphasize **hands-on player-coach**: you still do design reviews, prototype, and make critical technical calls personally. Signals fit for the merit-driven culture and reassures Cameron.
- Name the **regulated-environment discipline** directly: partnering with Security/Privacy/Legal/Compliance; data classification; safe rollout. Health benefits = PHI. Mirrors Singh's Medtronic/Symantec DNA.
- Show comfort with **modernization without dogma** — Gravie runs a Clojure/AWS/Kinesis stack. Don't disparage the stack; show you can raise a platform's engineering standards while respecting an opinionated, functional codebase (Cameron's baby).
- If speaking with Cameron: architecture philosophy, developer experience, and quality engineering. If speaking with Singh: org scaling, delivery, roadmap prioritization, and cross-functional partnership.
