# Role Summary – VP, Platform & Architecture (Gravie)

## At a glance

- **Role:** VP, Platform & Architecture
- **Location / remote:** Remote, United States
- **Seniority:** VP, top-of-technical-org (hands-on player-coach)

## What the role is
> A hands-on, player-coach VP who owns Gravie's shared technology foundations: platform, architecture, and cloud strategy. The seat has three centers of gravity. First, platform and architecture leadership across shared services and product domains (standards, reliability, security, resilience, developer experience, cloud cost). Second, and most distinctive, **building Gravie's AI platform** end to end: secure model access, agent orchestration, retrieval and knowledge services, evaluation, observability, guardrails, and model lifecycle, plus driving measured adoption of agentic development across Engineering. Third, standing up shared quality engineering and **product support operations** (triage, escalation, root-cause, resolution) and partnering with Customer Operations to deploy AI agents into support. It operates in a regulated (healthcare/PHI) environment alongside Security, Privacy, Legal, and Compliance.

## Key requirements
- Significant platform-engineering, architecture, infrastructure, and developer-experience leadership
- Hands-on technical executive who moves from strategy to architecture to prototyping to problem-solving
- Built shared platforms used by multiple product and engineering teams
- Track record improving engineering productivity, platform reliability, operational effectiveness
- Technical foundations for AI embedded in customer-facing products
- GenAI architecture: agent orchestration, retrieval, evaluation, observability, production ops, guardrails, model lifecycle
- Driving adoption of agentic development across an engineering org
- Applying AI agents in customer service, internal ops, or software development
- Establishing/leading product support operations or production issue management
- Operating in a regulated environment with Security/Privacy/Legal/Risk/Compliance
- Org leadership, executive communication, financial and vendor management
- **Preferred:** insurance, healthcare, financial services, or another regulated industry
