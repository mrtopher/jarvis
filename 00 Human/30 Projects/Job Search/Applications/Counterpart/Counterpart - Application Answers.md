# Application Answers – Counterpart VP of Engineering

## How do you use AI to improve your work?

> AI does the repeatable work so I can spend my time on the parts that need judgment. In practice that means agents drafting and reviewing code, generating tests, digging through logs, and turning rough notes into first-draft specs. I treat everything they produce like a junior engineer's work. It gets reviewed and evaluated against the same bar before it ships. I build those guardrails into the workflow, so a lean team moves faster without shipping slop.

### Shorter (two-sentence) version
> AI handles the repeatable work, drafting and reviewing code, generating tests, digging through logs, so I spend my time on the calls that need judgment. I hold its output to the same bar as any engineer's, with review and evaluation built into the workflow, so a lean team moves faster without shipping slop.
