# Company Research – Counterpart

## Snapshot
- **What they do:** AI-native insurance company (managing general agent) for small businesses. Sells management and professional liability underwriting, claims, and risk services on an "Agentic Insurance" platform that pairs insurance experts with AI.
- **Size / stage:** ~118 employees across 5 continents (Feb 2026). Series C, growth stage.
- **Funding / ownership:** ~$106M total raised. $50M Series C (April 2026) led by Valor Equity Partners, with Vy Capital returning. Premiums up ~175% year-over-year in 2025.
- **HQ / locations:** Fully distributed, no office. CEO based in Los Angeles.
- **Remote policy:** Fully remote, work from anywhere, team meets in person a couple times per year.

## Products & business model
> Managing general agent for management and professional liability (MPL) insurance aimed at small and mid-sized businesses. Revenue comes from underwriting and placing policies through brokers and carriers. "Agentic Insurance" is their operating system across underwriting, broker services, risk mitigation, and claims. Recent product moves: Affirmative AI Coverage and a Technology E&O insuring agreement, positioning them to insure businesses against AI-era risks (employee lawsuits tied to AI and layoffs). Series C capital is going toward new specialty products, industry-specific programs, expanded claims/risk capabilities, and capitalizing their own insurance carrier to retain risk.

## Tech stack
> Public-facing signals only (marketing site): Gatsby, Tailwind CSS, Netlify, Google Workspace, Oracle. The engineering platform itself is not public, but the JD names the real work: AI and data platforms, developer experience infrastructure, deployment pipelines, and agent systems that "compound every engineer's output." Compliance targets: SOC 2, HIPAA, NYC Cybersecurity regulations. Regulated-industry (insurance) data handling is core.

## Culture & values
> Flat-by-design engineering org. The operating thesis: every person carries real context, ownership, and leverage, and "the system carries the rest" (agents do the repeatable work). High talent density is an explicit goal. Fully remote with high trust and high accountability. They frame compliance as something built into systems, not bolted on. Certified by Great Place To Work.

## Reputation
> Glassdoor: 3.9 / 5 across 18 reviews. 68% would recommend to a friend. Work-life balance 3.7, culture and values 3.8, career opportunities 3.8, comp and benefits 3.5. Reviews are mixed: some praise modern culture, clear vision, and engaging remote work; others cite a tense or unsupportive culture and leadership decisions. Small review sample, so treat as directional not definitive.

## Recent news
- April 2026: Closed $50M Series C led by Valor Equity Partners; total raised ~$106M.
- April 2026: Positioned Agentic Insurance to protect small businesses from a new wave of AI/layoff-driven employee lawsuits.
- November 2025 / March 2025: Expanded Affirmative AI Coverage and launched Technology E&O insuring agreement.
- March 2026: Expanded insurance leadership team amid rising demand.
- 2025: Record growth, premiums up ~175% year-over-year.

## Green flags
- The role is a genuine fit for Chris's thesis: AI won't replace engineers, it makes them stronger. Counterpart wants agents that compound engineer output, not headcount replacement. That is exactly his public POV.
- Fully remote, which matches the requirement to run Dual Logic in parallel.
- Well-funded (fresh $50M Series C) growth-stage company with real revenue traction, not a pre-revenue bet.
- VP Eng owns technical vision, org, and board representation. Broad scope, senior seat.
- Regulated-industry compliance (SOC 2, HIPAA) maps directly to Chris's Pipeline 360 and Industry Dive security/diligence work.

## Red flags
- Compensation not listed. Need to confirm it lands near the ~$250-300K target before deep investment.
- Mixed Glassdoor sentiment mentions leadership tension. Small sample, but worth probing in interviews.
- "Flat-by-design" plus a deep leadership bench already in place (4 Engineering Managers, 2 Principal Engineers, 3 Technical PMs). Clarify what "VP of Engineering" actually owns day-to-day versus the existing EM layer, and whether this is a build role or a coordinate role.
- Insurance domain is new to Chris (he has adjacent regulated-industry experience, not insurance specifically).
