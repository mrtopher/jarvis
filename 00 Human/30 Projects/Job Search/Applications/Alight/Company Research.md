# Company Research – Alight, Inc.

## Snapshot
- **What they do:** Cloud-based benefits administration and employee experience. Health, wealth, and leave solutions for large employers, delivered through the Alight Worklife platform. Serves the world's largest organizations and 30M+ people.
- **Size / stage:** Public company (NYSE: ALIT). Large-cap enterprise scale. Q1 2026 revenue $534M; Health Solutions alone $498M recurring in Q1. Q2 2026 beat expectations on revenue and adjusted EBITDA.
- **Funding / ownership:** Publicly traded. Went public via SPAC (Foley Trasimene) in 2021. Sold its Payroll & Professional Services business in 2024 to focus on the Worklife platform.
- **HQ / locations:** Chicago, IL. Global workforce; growing India footprint.
- **Remote policy:** Flexible/remote-friendly ("Top 100 Company for Remote Jobs" 6 years running). Camera-on culture; occasional travel to a physical office.

## Products & business model
> Recurring-revenue SaaS + services. Alight Worklife is the flagship platform: benefits management, personalization, and data-driven insights across health, wealth, wellbeing, navigation, and absence/leave management. Platform now supports clients on Workday, SAP, Oracle, and Cornerstone OnDemand. Business model is enterprise contracts with SLAs and multi-year recurring revenue tied to member engagement.

## Tech stack
> Not fully public. Enterprise cloud, APIs, microservices, and data platforms per the JD. Heavy integration surface (Workday/SAP/Oracle/Cornerstone). Active AI/GenAI push under the new CTO. The JD explicitly calls out cloud, APIs, microservices, data platforms, and AI/ML.

## Culture & values
> Stated values: purposeful, human, united, growth-minded. External recognition: "Great Place to Work" 7 years, Fortune "Best Companies to Work For," Top 100 remote employer. Reality is more mixed right now given restructuring (see red flags).

## Reputation
> Glassdoor and Blind threads reference repeated rounds of layoffs, stretched teams, low morale, and offshoring to India. External employer-brand awards are strong; internal sentiment during the current restructuring is strained.

## Recent news
- Naveen Baweja appointed CTO effective April 29, 2026 (ex-Disney VP of Technology). Explicit mandate: modernize the benefits platform, expand AI and platform capabilities. Reports to CEO Rohit Verma.
- Restructuring plan announced impacting ~10% of workforce; shift toward digital solutions and outsourcing; benefits/pension plans being revised.
- Q1 2026 revenue $534M, free cash flow $53M. Q2 2026 beat on revenue and adjusted EBITDA.

## Green flags
- Brand-new CTO with an explicit platform-modernization + AI mandate. This role reports to him and is the tip of the spear for that mandate. Timing is ideal for a platform-consolidation operator.
- Clear platform-first, "reduce fragmentation, consolidate, kill tech debt" charter that maps almost exactly to Chris's Pipeline 360 and Industry Dive work.
- Enterprise scale, real recurring revenue, public-company rigor. Salary band ($230K-$275K) is credible for the title.
- Remote-friendly.

## Red flags
- Active restructuring and ~10% layoffs. New senior hires can be exposed if priorities shift; also means a stretched, change-fatigued org to lead.
- Offshoring push to India could mean this role is expected to consolidate/relocate teams (politically heavy).
- 20+ years and "at scale across multiple product lines" bar is high; Chris is at ~20 years and has not run a public-company org of Alight's headcount. Fit is strong on capability, lighter on raw enterprise-headcount scale.
- Camera-on, occasional-travel culture; not fully remote-async.
