# Chief Technology Officer (CTO) — Remote, USA

Company: Super Hire (Recruiting on behalf of the client)

Location: Remote — USA

Employment Type: Full-Time | Executive Leadership

## About the Role:

Super Hire is recruiting on behalf of its client for an experienced Chief Technology Officer (CTO) to lead the organization's technology strategy, engineering operations, innovation, and digital transformation initiatives.

The CTO will work closely with executive leadership to align technology investments with business objectives, build scalable technology capabilities, and lead high-performing technical teams.

## Key Responsibilities:

Develop and execute the organization's overall technology strategy and roadmap.
Lead engineering, software development, infrastructure, data, and technology operations.
Oversee the design, development, deployment, and ongoing improvement of technology products and platforms.
Evaluate emerging technologies and identify opportunities for innovation and operational improvement.
Establish technology standards, architecture principles, development processes, and performance metrics.
Partner with product, operations, sales, marketing, and executive teams to align technology initiatives with business priorities.
Oversee cybersecurity, data protection, system reliability, and technology risk management.
Manage technology budgets, vendors, strategic partners, and resource planning.
Build, mentor, and develop high-performing engineering and technology teams.
Support digital transformation and long-term technology scalability.

## Qualifications & Skills:

Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Information Technology, or a related field; advanced degree is a plus.
Significant leadership experience in technology, engineering, software development, or a related function.
Demonstrated experience developing and executing technology strategies.
Strong understanding of cloud infrastructure, software architecture, cybersecurity, data, and modern development practices.
Experience managing technical teams, budgets, vendors, and cross-functional initiatives.
Strong strategic thinking, problem-solving, communication, and leadership skills.
Experience with cloud platforms such as AWS, Microsoft Azure, or Google Cloud is preferred.
Experience leading technology transformation or scaling technology operations is advantageous.
Must be legally authorized to work in the United States.

## What the Client Offers:

Fully remote executive leadership opportunity.
Competitive executive compensation package.
Performance-based incentives, where applicable.
Benefits package, where applicable.
Opportunity to shape the organization's technology strategy and innovation roadmap.
Collaborative executive leadership environment.
