## Red flags
- **No named client.** A legitimate retained CTO search will usually share the company (or at least the industry, stage, and comp band) once a candidate engages. This posting shares none of it.
- **No compensation.** "Competitive executive compensation package" with "benefits, where applicable" and "incentives, where applicable" is non-committal language, unusual for a real executive engagement.
- **Entirely generic, boilerplate JD.** Every responsibility and qualification is a template CTO line. There is no industry, no product, no team size, no reporting line, no scale marker. Nothing distinguishes this from a copy-paste template.
- **No verifiable recruiter footprint.** "Super Hire" does not surface as an established search firm. Blind postings from unverifiable recruiters are a common pattern for resume-harvesting, pipeline-building, or lead-generation rather than a live, funded search.
- **Low-signal channel.** No source URL, no ATS link, no named contact. This reads like a mass job-board post, not a targeted executive search.

## Assessment
> This is a low-signal blind posting. It is not necessarily fraudulent, but there is no way to verify the client, the compensation, the scale, or even the recruiter. Compared to Chris's other "undisclosed" cards (The Hired Guns, The ReWork Group, SVP Head of Technology), those at least named a real, verifiable search firm and gave scope or comp signals. This one gives neither. Do not invest resume effort until Super Hire discloses the client, the comp band, and the scope in writing.
