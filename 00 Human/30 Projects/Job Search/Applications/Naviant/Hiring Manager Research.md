## Background
> Michael Carr has been President & CEO of Naviant since October 2019, after years in executive leadership at the firm dating to at least 2007. He rose alongside Elizabeth Skolaski (now EVP & COO) and reports up to Founder & Owner Mike Suter. His public framing is growth-and-stewardship: impacting more clients, growing employee careers, contributing to the community. That is a steady, people-first operator, not a hype-driven founder.

## What they likely care about
- A VP who can make the "AI-first agentic automation firm" transition real without breaking a profitable, delivery-driven services business.
- Operational rigor: modern SDLC, DevSecOps, CI/CD, governance, and presales discipline that protect margin and delivery quality.
- Someone who can lead in a consultative, services-led environment, not just a product org.
- Team building and culture: craftsmanship, accountability, mentoring, career pathways. Retention matters at a 160-person firm.
- Evidence-based, standardized approaches over experimentation for its own sake.
