# Company Research – Naviant, Inc.

## Snapshot
- **What they do:** Intelligent automation and business-process consulting firm. Long-standing systems integrator for Hyland OnBase (ECM), intelligent document processing (ABBYY, Brainware), and RPA (Kryon). Pivoting toward an "AI-first agentic automation firm."
- **Size / stage:** ~160 employees. 30+ years in business. Serves 2,500+ mid-size and global organizations.
- **Funding / ownership:** Privately owned, founder-held. No external VC/PE funding on record. Founded 1986 by Mike Suter, who remains Founder & Owner.
- **HQ / locations:** Verona, Wisconsin. Distributed workforce; the role is fully remote (US only).
- **Remote policy:** Fully remote within the US. No visa sponsorship.

## Products & business model
> Services-led, not a product company. Revenue comes from consulting, implementation, and managed support around third-party automation platforms. The "process-first" methodology sells process improvement before technology. Deep Hyland alliance: OnBase Diamond Solutions Integrator, multiple 2024 Hyland Partner of the Year awards (Government vertical, Competency Excellence, SAP Integrations, Premier Partner). Growth partly through acquisition of regional OnBase practices (Reno NV, Syracuse NY, Precision Document Imaging in 2022). The new VP role is the tip of a deliberate move from "implement other people's platforms" toward building agentic-AI and automation IP of their own.

## Tech stack
> Platform-centric: Hyland OnBase / Content Innovation Cloud, ABBYY and Brainware intelligent capture, Kryon RPA, SAP integrations. The JD signals a build-out beyond packaged platforms: agentic AI, custom solution architecture, DevSecOps, CI/CD, "configuration as code," analytics, ECM, and IDP. Cloud-based automation expertise called out explicitly.

## Culture & values
> Human-centered, mission-driven language: "reimagine how work gets done," "craftsmanship, accountability, innovation." Community-minded (charitable giving, volunteer time off, annual in-person celebration week). Stable, long-tenured leadership. Midwest professional-services culture rather than move-fast startup culture.

## Reputation
> Well-regarded in the Hyland/ECM ecosystem, repeatedly recognized as a top Hyland partner. Established Madison-area employer with Greater Madison Chamber ties. Low public controversy. Reviews and comp data are thin (small private firm), so Glassdoor signal is limited.

## Recent news
- 2024: Won four elite Hyland Partner awards (SAP Integrations Competency, Partner of the Year in Government, Premier Partner, Partner of the Year for Competency Excellence).
- 2022: Acquired Precision Document Imaging's OnBase division.
- Ongoing: Public repositioning around "agentic AI" and intelligent automation, which this VP role is built to drive.

## Green flags
- Direct hit on Chris's current identity: an AI consulting technical leader helping an org turn AI strategy into working systems. This is Dual Logic's exact value proposition, at scale.
- Clean mandate. The role owns technology strategy, SDLC maturity, DevSecOps/CI/CD, presales rigor, and team building. That is a greenfield leadership charter, not a caretaker seat.
- Fully remote, stable, profitable, no layoff-driven chaos. A calmer environment than the PE-reset and VC-morale red flags in the rest of the pipeline.
- Established brand and 2,500+ client base give real leverage for someone who wants to build an AI practice with existing distribution.

## Red flags
- Compensation undisclosed. A 160-person bootstrapped Midwest services firm may pay below the $250K+ product-SaaS bar Chris has seen elsewhere. Must clarify range early.
- Services/consulting margins and utilization pressure. This is a billable-delivery world, not a product P&L. The VP owns delivery outcomes and presales, so quota-adjacent pressure is real.
- Platform-heavy legacy (OnBase/ECM/RPA) is not Chris's core lineage. He has to sell the "modern SDLC + agentic AI + cloud architecture" story, not OnBase depth.
- "AI-first" transition is early. Success depends on genuine executive commitment (Carr/Suter) versus marketing repositioning. Probe how real the investment is.
