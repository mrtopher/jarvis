# VP of Technology and Solutions - Naviant

**Company:** Naviant

**Role Title:** VP of Technology and Solutions

**Location:** United States (Fully Remote)

**Employment Type:** Full-time

**Seniority Level:** Executive / Senior Level

**Department:** Delivery

---

## Role Overview

Naviant seeks a strategic VP to lead the company's transition toward an "AI-first agentic automation firm." The position requires balancing visionary technical leadership with operational rigor needed to scale a professional services organization.

## Key Responsibilities

**First 30 Days:**
- Comprehend Naviant's current technology landscape and delivery model
- Establish relationships across executive, sales, and technical teams
- Assess SDLC practices and governance gaps
- Review active implementations for risk assessment

**Within 60 Days:**
- "Define and begin socializing a forward-looking technology strategy"
- Standardize solution architecture and development approaches
- Initiate SDLC and governance improvements
- Introduce DevSecOps and CI/CD practices
- Strengthen presales technical rigor

**90+ Days:**
- Execute technology strategy across AI, automation, ECM, IDP, and analytics
- Establish modern SDLC practices
- Drive structured R&D initiatives
- Build high-performing technical teams
- Position Naviant as an intelligent automation leader

## Required Competencies

**Technology Strategy & Innovation:** Design and govern strategy across agentic AI, RPA, analytics, ECM, and IDP using "standardized, evidence-based approach."

**Engineering Excellence:** Establish modern SDLC with "configuration as code" practices, DevSecOps, and CI/CD across custom and platform solutions.

**Delivery Execution:** Own end-to-end delivery outcomes with presales participation in discovery, estimation, and deal validation.

**Platform Architecture:** Lead in consultative, services-led environments with cloud-based automation expertise.

**Leadership:** Build and mentor high-performing teams while establishing clear career pathways and fostering "a culture of craftsmanship, accountability, innovation."

## About Naviant

Founded in 1986 by Mike Suter, Naviant operates as an intelligent automation and process consulting firm with approximately 160 employees. The company mission emphasizes partnering "to reimagine how work gets done, combining deep industry expertise, intelligent technology, and a human-centered approach."
