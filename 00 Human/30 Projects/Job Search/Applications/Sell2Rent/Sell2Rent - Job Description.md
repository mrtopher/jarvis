# Chief Technology Officer – Remote (Americas time zones)

**Company:** Sell2Rent

**Location:** Remote (requires meaningful overlap with US Eastern and Latin America time zones)

**Role Type:** Full-time

**Seniority:** Executive/C-level

**Compensation:** "Competitive base plus meaningful equity" (range to be discussed early in process)

---

## About the Company

Sell2Rent is a fast-growing residential real estate platform specializing in sale-leaseback transactions. The company helps homeowners unlock home equity without moving while connecting properties with single-family rental investors. Operating in 30+ states with 200+ closed transactions, technology is a key growth driver—including a proprietary operating platform, AI agents for lead qualification and outreach, an investor-matching layer, and a lead engine supporting sales.

---

## Role Overview

This CTO position treats technology as "a growth and revenue engine—not just an engineering function." You'll work closely with Sales and Marketing to leverage AI, automation, data, and product to generate qualified leads, improve conversion, accelerate the sales cycle, and close more transactions.

---

## Key Responsibilities

- **Technology strategy:** Set direction on architecture, build-vs-buy decisions, platform standardization, and where AI creates genuine advantage versus distraction.
- **Engineering organization:** Lead a distributed team of ~10 across engineering and data (based in Latin America). Grow, structure, and set performance standards.
- **Platform scaling:** Over 12 months, ship the roadmap, complete legacy system migration, harden the platform, and remove engineering as a growth bottleneck.
- **AI layer:** Decide how far agent-based qualification, outreach, and operations extend; build durable competitive advantage rather than demos.
- **Resilience and continuity:** Establish security governance, vendor management, monitoring, documentation, and ensure no single person owns critical knowledge.
- **Executive partnership:** Sit on the Executive Committee; partner directly with CEO and sales/marketing/operations leaders to translate business needs into technical execution.

---

## Requirements

- **15+ years in technology** with progressive leadership responsibility and time as CTO, VP of Engineering, or Head of Technology.
- **Scaled a team and system before**—not just managed, but moved something from working-but-fragile to reliable-at-volume while growing the organization.
- **Technical depth with executive focus:** Able to read architecture, challenge design decisions, and evaluate vendors, but your job is not to write code.
- **Real depth in AI-driven architecture, cloud infrastructure (GCP), APIs, integrations, and data.** You understand what LLM-based agents actually break on in production.
- **You find blind spots.** Much of your first six months involves identifying gaps in architecture, tooling, security, and process.
- **Strong people leadership** in remote, distributed, international settings; keeping engineers motivated, growing, and clear on priorities is central to this role.
- **Startup fluency:** Comfortable with ambiguity, limited resources, and deciding before all information arrives.
- **Business-minded:** Connect technical investment to revenue; defend proposals to a CEO who will ask hard questions.

**Helpful but not required:** Real estate, proptech, or fintech experience; running a US company with LatAm engineering; Spanish language skills.

---

## Success Milestones

**90 days:** Know where every system, credential, vendor, and dependency lives (and document it so others do too). Identify the three biggest architectural concerns. Clarify reporting structure, roadmap, and priorities for the team.

**6 months:** Platform migration on credible path; engineering operating cadence established; technology decisions deliberate rather than reactive. Make at least one hire that raises the bar.

**12 months:** Ship the roadmap. Platform handles significantly more volume without headcount growth. AI capability is genuine competitive advantage. No critical knowledge depends on any individual.

---

## About the Role Relative to Product

The CEO owns product direction and will continue to do so. "You own technology, architecture, and execution." You'll have real influence on what gets built and sequencing input—but the product roadmap is not yours to set, and the company does not plan to hand it over. If owning product is your goal for your next role, this is not it.

---

## Hiring Process

1. Intro call with CEO (45 min, mutual fit)
2. Technical and architectural conversation (walk through actual systems built; get your honest assessment)
3. Meet engineering team and two executive peers
4. Working session on a real problem (paid if significant prep required)
5. References and offer

"We move quickly and we'll tell you where you stand at every step."
