# Role Summary – Sell2Rent CTO

## At a glance
- **Company:** Sell2Rent (residential sale-leaseback platform, Miami)
- **Role:** Chief Technology Officer
- **Location / remote:** Remote, US Eastern + LatAm time-zone overlap required
- **Seniority:** C-level, Executive Committee seat
- **Compensation:** "Competitive base plus meaningful equity," range discussed early

## What the role is
> Own technology, architecture, and execution for a small, fast-moving proptech startup where the platform is the revenue engine. Lead a ~10-person distributed engineering and data team in Latin America. Over 12 months: ship the roadmap, finish a legacy migration, harden the platform, and push agentic AI (lead qualification, outreach, operations) from demo to durable advantage. Sit on the Exec Committee and defend technical investment to a CEO who asks hard questions. The CEO keeps product direction; this is not a product-owner seat.

## Key requirements
- 15+ years in tech with time as CTO / VP Eng / Head of Technology
- Scaled a team AND a system from fragile to reliable-at-volume
- Executive-focus technical depth (read and challenge architecture; not a coding seat)
- Real depth in AI-driven architecture, GCP, APIs, integrations, data; knows where LLM agents break in production
- Strong people leadership of remote, distributed, international teams
- Startup fluency and business-minded (connect tech spend to revenue)

## Alignment to my goals
> This is a true C-level CTO seat, a title step up from the VP/Head roles filling the pipeline, and the AI mandate is one of the cleanest thesis matches on the board. It supports the goal of a signed offer. The drags are scale and comp, not fit.

## Recommendation: **APPLY (qualified)**
> Apply, but go in with eyes open on scale and comp. The fit is real and the seat is an upgrade in title; the open questions are money and company size, both answerable early in the process.

### Why apply
- Genuine CTO / C-level title with an Executive Committee seat. A step up from VP/Head roles.
- AI is core to the business (agentic qualification and outreach drive revenue). Directly matches the Dual Logic agentic-AI-in-production work and the "AI slop is real, ship AI that holds up" POV.
- GCP + distributed team + cold-start diligence (90-day "find every system and blind spot") maps cleanly to the Pipeline 360 reset.
- Honest, fast, well-run process. The company knows exactly what it wants.

### Why not
- **Scale.** ~10 eng/data, ~30 total. Right at the recurring pass line. Bigger than Sensei Labs (~7 eng, passed) but smaller than Tiny Health (~12 eng / 41 staff, applied). Sensei was declined on scale alone despite a clean thesis fit; this clears that bar by a little, not a lot.
- **Comp.** "Competitive base plus meaningful equity" at an early, single-round startup usually lands under the $250-300K base target, with upside pushed into equity. Get the range early.
- **No product ownership.** CEO owns product and will not hand it over.
- **Not hands-on.** "Your job is not to write code" cuts against the player-coach thesis, though the JD still wants someone who can read and challenge architecture.

## If applying
- **Angle to lead with:** revenue-tied technology. Position AI as conversion and sales-cycle leverage, agentic systems that hold up in production (not demos), and the executive muscle of defending technical spend to a CEO/board.
- **Gaps to address:** surface GCP depth explicitly (resume leans AWS); acknowledge proptech is new but frame the pattern (regulated, transaction-driven, data-heavy platform) as familiar; be ready to discuss comp expectations honestly given the equity-heavy package.
