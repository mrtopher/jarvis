# Company Research – Sell2Rent

## Snapshot
- **What they do:** Residential real estate platform built around sale-leasebacks. Homeowners sell their house, cash out the equity, and stay on as renters. Single-family rental investors buy the properties as buy-and-hold income assets. Sell2Rent sits in the middle and matches the two sides.
- **Size / stage:** Small and early. ~27-30 employees as of mid-2024, spread across the US and Latin America. Early-stage startup, not a public or PE-scale company.
- **Funding / ownership:** Founded 2018, Miami FL. Private, venture-funded. Public sources show a single funding round with 1 institutional investor; amount not disclosed in Crunchbase/Tracxn.
- **HQ / locations:** Miami, Florida. Engineering and data team based in Latin America. Operates in 30+ states.
- **Remote policy:** Remote, with required overlap across US Eastern and Latin America time zones.

## Products & business model
> Proprietary operating platform for running sale-leaseback transactions end to end. AI agents for lead qualification and outreach, an investor-matching layer, and a lead engine feeding sales. Revenue comes from transaction volume (200+ closed to date), so the platform's job is explicitly to generate qualified leads, lift conversion, and shorten the sales cycle. Technology is framed as a growth and revenue engine, not a cost center.

## Tech stack
> GCP for cloud infrastructure. LLM-based / agentic AI in production for qualification and outreach. APIs and integrations across the operating platform. A legacy system is mid-migration. Data layer supports lead scoring and investor matching. Stack details beyond GCP + AI agents are not public; expect to learn the real picture in the technical interview.

## Culture & values
> Fast-moving startup. Founder-led, with the CEO owning product direction. Distributed and international by design (US + LatAm). The JD is unusually blunt and specific ("we'll tell you where you stand at every step," "if owning product is your goal, this is not it"), which reads as a direct, low-ego, results-first culture.

## Reputation
> No Glassdoor signal of note given the small size. Press coverage (Refresh Miami, Rent Magazine, TodayUS) is positive and frames the company as solving real financial-distress problems for homeowners. Founders carry real real-estate credibility.

## Recent news
- Coverage of growing sale-leaseback demand among homeowners under financial pressure (2025).
- RISE 2026 real estate event tied to the company / founder.
- Ongoing content marketing on leasebacks + investor strategy.

## Green flags
- Real CTO seat with an Executive Committee chair and direct CEO partnership.
- AI is central to the business, not a side project. Agentic qualification/outreach is core to revenue.
- GCP + agentic AI + distributed team maps cleanly to Chris's current Dual Logic work.
- Clear, honest, well-written mandate and hiring process. The company knows what it wants.
- Founders have deep domain experience (15,000+ units managed, $200M+ debt structured).

## Red flags
- **Small scale.** ~10 engineers/data and ~30 total employees. This sits right at Chris's recurring pass line (bigger than Sensei's ~7, smaller than Tiny Health's ~12/41).
- **Comp risk.** "Competitive base plus meaningful equity" at an early startup usually lands below Chris's $250-300K base target; a lot of the upside is equity.
- **No product ownership.** CEO owns product and will not hand it over. The seat is technology, architecture, and execution only.
- **Not a hands-on seat.** "Your job is not to write code." That cuts against Chris's player-coach thesis.
- **Funding opacity.** Single undisclosed round, small institutional backing. Runway and comp ceiling are unknowns to raise early.
- **Real-estate / proptech domain** is new for Chris (helpful-but-not-required, so not disqualifying).
