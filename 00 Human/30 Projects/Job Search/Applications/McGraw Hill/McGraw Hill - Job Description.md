# VP, Software Engineering — McGraw Hill (archival copy)

> Source: https://careers.mheducation.com/jobs/6956
> Fetched: 2026-07-16
> Verbatim archive of the posting as retrieved. Do not edit.

**Company:** McGraw Hill LLC

**Job Title:** VP, Software Engineering

**Location:** Remote, United States

**Employment Type:** Full-Time

**Salary Range:** $250,000 - $310,000 annually (base pay may vary by experience and location)

---

## Overview

McGraw Hill seeks a VP of Software Engineering to lead 100-200 engineers across courseware delivery, data/personalization, and the Sharpen product area. This individual will report directly to the SVP of Product Engineering.

---

## Key Responsibilities

- Define and drive engineering strategy, translating business direction into technical vision
- Lead and develop experienced engineering directors and managers
- Build organizational systems enabling engineer growth and retention
- Recruit senior-level talent and build leadership bench strength
- Design scalable organizational structures anticipating future business needs
- Partner with product, sales, and marketing leadership on strategy and roadmaps
- Manage relationships with platform and infrastructure teams
- Own and manage a $20M+ budget with accountability for spending decisions

---

## Required Qualifications

- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or equivalent experience
- 15+ years professional experience; 8+ years in progressive senior engineering leadership
- Experience managing 150+ professionals with $20M+ budgets
- Strong technical leadership in product-engineering environments
- Track record delivering scaled software products to millions serving $500M+ revenue businesses
- Proven tactical delivery excellence supporting business strategy
- Demonstrated ability building and developing high-performing teams
- Experience leading globally distributed teams (offshore, nearshore, vendors)
- Strong communication skills translating technical concepts for non-technical stakeholders
- Full-stack software background emphasizing usability, accessibility, scalability

---

## Preferred Qualifications

- EdTech industry experience, particularly Higher Education
- Data-driven product background
- Familiarity with adaptive learning or LMS ecosystems
- Platform modernization or migration leadership experience
- Media industry experience
