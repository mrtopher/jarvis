# Role Summary – McGraw Hill VP, Software Engineering

## At a glance
- **Company:** McGraw Hill (global EdTech, ~$2.1B revenue, newly public on NYSE, Platinum Equity-owned)
- **Role:** VP, Software Engineering
- **Location / remote:** Remote, United States
- **Seniority:** VP, reports to SVP of Product Engineering
- **Compensation:** $250,000 - $310,000 base (varies by experience/location)

## What the role is
> A VP owning a 100-200 engineer organization across three product areas: courseware delivery, data/personalization, and the Sharpen study app. The mandate is org leadership at scale: set engineering strategy, develop directors and managers, build retention and growth systems, recruit senior talent, design the org structure, and manage a $20M+ budget. It is a leadership-and-delivery seat on McGraw Hill's digital/AI growth engine, not a hands-on-keyboard role, partnering across product, sales, marketing, and the platform/infrastructure teams.

## Key requirements
- 15+ years experience; 8+ in senior engineering leadership
- Managing 150+ professionals with $20M+ budgets
- Delivering scaled products to millions on $500M+ revenue businesses
- Building/retaining high-performing teams; leadership bench strength
- Leading globally distributed teams (offshore, nearshore, vendors)
- Translating technical concepts for non-technical stakeholders
- Full-stack background: usability, accessibility, scalability
- Preferred: EdTech/Higher Ed, data-driven product, adaptive learning/LMS, platform modernization, media
