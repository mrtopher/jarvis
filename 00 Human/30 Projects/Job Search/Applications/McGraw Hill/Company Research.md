# Company Research – McGraw Hill

## Snapshot
- **What they do:** Global education solutions provider across K-12, higher education, and professional learning. Digital-first learning platforms (Connect, ALEKS, McGraw Hill GO, Sharpen) plus courseware, delivered in 80+ languages.
- **Size / stage:** Large, mature. ~$2.1B revenue (FY2026, ended 2026-03-31), Adjusted EBITDA $744.3M (35.4% margin), returned to profit. 26M+ paid digital users; digital revenue over $1.4B and more than doubled FY21 to FY25.
- **Funding / ownership:** Newly public. IPO'd on the NYSE in 2025 (ticker MH). Owned by Platinum Equity, which bought McGraw Hill from Apollo Global for ~$4.5B; Vector Capital co-invested. PE-backed public company.
- **HQ / locations:** New York City. Offices across North America, Asia, Australia, Europe, Middle East, South America.
- **Remote policy:** This role is Remote, United States.

## Products & business model
> Subscription and courseware revenue across three markets (PreK-12, higher ed, professional). Digital transition is the core story: digital is now the majority of the business and the growth engine. Higher-ed digital platforms include Connect (courseware/assessment), ALEKS (adaptive learning), and Sharpen, the mobile study app (launched Oct 2022; quizzes, flashcards, chapter summaries, AI study tools; covers 150+ textbooks; free + paid tiers; "Sharpen Advantage" is a newer AI-powered personalized offering). The VP role spans courseware delivery, data/personalization, and Sharpen, so it sits directly on the digital growth engine.

## Tech stack
> Not disclosed in the posting. Full-stack product engineering at consumer/education scale (web + mobile, iOS/Android for Sharpen). Emphasis on usability, accessibility, scalability. Data/personalization and adaptive-learning workloads imply a meaningful data platform. Confirm cloud (AWS/GCP/Azure) and languages in early conversations.

## Culture & values
> Glassdoor 4.0/5 overall, 87% would recommend. Work-life balance 4.1, culture & values 4.1. But career opportunities only 3.4 and mixed signals underneath the headline numbers (see Reputation). Mission-driven education framing.

## Reputation
> Glassdoor 4.0/5 across ~1,557 reviews. Recurring complaints: workload creep ("hired for one job, doing three"), long-hours/burnout pressure, and comp that lags the added responsibility. Engineers rate compensation ~3.2/5 (about 12% below the company average). Mentions of "frequent layoffs" and recent cuts on older/struggling products.

## Recent news
- IPO on NYSE in 2025 after Platinum Equity acquisition from Apollo (~$4.5B); Vector Capital co-invest.
- FY2026 revenue ~$2.1B, back to profitability, EBITDA margin 35.4%.
- Sharpen Advantage launched as an AI-powered, personalized study offering.

## Green flags
- Real scale and money. $2.1B revenue and $500M+ businesses inside the org match the JD's "millions of users / $500M+ revenue" bar exactly. Chris would be operating at genuine enterprise scale.
- Comp is disclosed and solid: $250K-$310K base, inside the target band, no guessing.
- Remote, United States. No relocation.
- The role sits on the digital/AI growth engine (data/personalization + Sharpen), not a legacy backwater. AI-forward personalization is squarely Chris's lane.
- Newly public + PE ownership means a clear performance mandate and defined budget authority ($20M+).

## Red flags
- Scale of org exceeds Chris's largest managed org. The JD wants "managing 150+ professionals with $20M+ budgets" and leading 100-200 engineers. Chris has scaled teams (8 to 40+) and run a consulting practice, not a 150+ person org. This is the central honest gap.
- Burnout / overload culture signals and "frequent layoffs" on Glassdoor. PE-owned + newly public can mean aggressive cost and margin pressure (the 35.4% EBITDA margin is defended hard).
- Engineer comp satisfaction is low (3.2/5), which can make talent retention (an explicit responsibility) harder.
- EdTech / higher-ed domain is new for Chris (preferred, not required).
