# Company Research – StartupHakk (Meta DSP), employer undisclosed

## Snapshot
- **What they do:** Two parties here. (1) **StartupHakk** = the recruiter fronting the search. (2) **The actual employer** = an undisclosed, PE-backed digital advertising / ad-tech company with 10+ years in market, processing high-volume campaigns across ad exchanges, SSPs, and DSPs. Its core platform is live; it is now building a proprietary **Meta DSP** module.
- **Size / stage:** Employer: established, PE-backed, growth-oriented. StartupHakk: small.
- **Funding / ownership:** Employer: private-equity-backed (name, sponsor, and stage all undisclosed).
- **HQ / locations:** Not stated for the employer. Remote policy not stated.
- **Remote policy:** Not stated.

## Products & business model
> Employer runs an in-market ad-tech platform for programmatic campaign execution across exchanges/SSPs/DSPs, serving agency and enterprise media buyers. The open role builds a new **Meta DSP** module (a demand-side platform focused on Meta inventory) that integrates into the existing platform, with integrations across Meta, The Trade Desk, Amazon Ads, Google DV360, Beeswax, StackAdapt, Basis, Yahoo, and more. A "Meta-DSP" in the industry sense aggregates/orchestrates buying across multiple DSPs and channels behind one interface — this is core programmatic/RTB infrastructure work, not the advertiser/demand-gen side.

## The recruiter: StartupHakk
> StartupHakk (startuphakk.com) is a small coding-bootcamp and fractional-CTO / dev-consulting shop led by "Spencer" (marketed as 25+ years of development experience). Its public identity is a 12-week fullstack .NET bootcamp (Angular, C#, .NET Core, REST APIs, SQL, Azure) plus fractional-CTO and consulting services. It is **not** a specialized ad-tech or executive-search firm. That matters: a VP-of-Technology ad-tech search run through a bootcamp/consulting shop rather than an ad-tech-native recruiter raises questions about process rigor, the strength of the employer relationship, and how well the recruiter can vet or represent a senior ad-tech candidate.

## Tech stack
> Employer: **intentionally technology-agnostic** for this role. Signals in the JD: REST APIs, high-volume/big-data structures (bid streams, campaign reporting, real-time analytics), large-scale database design, scalability, CI/CD. No specific languages/cloud named for the employer's platform. (StartupHakk's own bootcamp teaches .NET/Azure/Angular — that is the recruiter's stack, not necessarily the employer's.)

## Culture & values
> Employer: fast-paced, growth-oriented, PE-backed, "speed-to-market is the top priority." That is a delivery-pressure culture by its own description.

## Reputation
> Employer unknown (masked), so no Glassdoor / sentiment read is possible. StartupHakk's public footprint is bootcamp marketing (Medium testimonials, Facebook, its own site); no meaningful third-party reputation signal for it as a senior-search recruiter.

## Recent news

- Industry context: "Meta-DSP" builds are an active 2025–2026 ad-tech pattern (e.g., Clearcode and others publish build guides), so the roadmap is plausible and current.

## Green flags
- Established, revenue-generating platform (not a zero-revenue startup); PE backing implies capital behind the build.
- Clear, bounded mandate (one module, team of 5, speed to market) — easy to understand what success looks like.
- Technology-agnostic — rewards engineering judgment over a specific stack, which suits a generalist builder.

## Red flags
- **Employer fully masked** — no way to assess comp, stability, runway, culture, or legitimacy without the recruiter disclosing it.
- **Recruiter is a bootcamp/fractional-CTO shop, not an ad-tech or exec-search specialist** — uncertain how well the search is resourced and represented.
- **Comp not stated.**
- **Domain is specialist programmatic/DSP/RTB ad-tech** — the JD repeatedly asks for direct DSP-infrastructure experience and, as a stated "key differentiator," an existing network of programmatic partners, ad exchanges, and data vendors. Both are hard gates.
- Narrow scope (lead 5 building one module) is below Chris's SVP/VP-over-~40 trajectory; the "VP" title is nominal for a small focused build.
