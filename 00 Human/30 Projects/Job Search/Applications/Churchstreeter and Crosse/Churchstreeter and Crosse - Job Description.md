# Churchstreeter and Crosse - Job Description (archival copy)

> Source: pasted by the user on 2026-07-07. No URL was provided. Verbatim text below.

Why Churchstreeter and Crosse: Churchstreeter and Crosse is redefining healthcare consulting by combining executive operational expertise, workforce strategy, and advanced AI technology into one integrated partnership model. Our executive leaders work alongside healthcare organizations—not as outside consultants, but as trusted strategic partners committed to measurable outcomes.


In this role... You will transform product vision into market-leading software by leading engineering execution, product development, AI innovation, technical architecture, and product delivery. We're seeking a technology executive who has repeatedly built category-defining products, assembled elite engineering teams, accelerated commercialization, established strategic industry partnerships, and helped position organizations for significant enterprise growth, strategic investment, or successful market expansion.


Job Summary

This role would oversee software engineering across application development, AI/ML, cloud infrastructure, DevOps, QA, and platform engineering. This role is responsible for building and scaling high-performing teams, driving technical excellence, and delivering scalable, secure, enterprise-grade healthcare technology products.


This role will translate executive vision into market-ready solutions, leading product development from concept through commercialization while ensuring speed, quality, and innovation. A key focus includes advancing AI capabilities such as intelligent automation, conversational AI, machine learning, LLM integrations, and workforce analytics.


This leader will partner closely with Sales, Marketing, and Customer Success to support go-to-market execution, enterprise client engagements, and product adoption. They will also contribute to product strategy, roadmap development, and evaluation of emerging technologies.


Key Responsibilities

Product Development: Translate executive vision into scalable, enterprise-ready software products by leading product execution from concept through commercialization. Ensure delivery of high-quality software on aggressive timelines while maintaining engineering excellence, and build intuitive, secure, and scalable solutions that are highly differentiated within the healthcare technology market.
Startup Growth & Commercialization: Lead engineering through startup and high-growth phases by developing technology that enables rapid go-to-market execution. Partner with executive leadership to prioritize features that drive competitive differentiation and revenue growth, ensuring alignment with customer needs, market demand, and commercial objectives while supporting product launches, demonstrations, pilot implementations, and enterprise deployments.
Artificial Intelligence & Innovation: Lead engineering efforts focused on advancing AI-powered recruiting and intelligent automation, including conversational AI, agentic workflows, predictive analytics, machine learning solutions, large language model integrations, voice AI technologies, and advanced workforce analytics.
Cross-Functional Leadership: Partner closely with Sales, Marketing, and Customer Success to support enterprise opportunities, technical presentations, and customer demonstrations. Communicate product value and competitive differentiation, ensure strong client adoption and satisfaction, and provide technical leadership during strategic client and executive engagements.
Product Strategy: Contribute to product roadmap development by evaluating emerging technologies and market trends, recommending enhancements to strengthen competitive positioning, and balancing speed-to-market with enterprise-grade quality and scalability.


Qualifications

Bachelor's degree in computer science, Software Engineering, Computer Engineering, or a related technical field. Master's degree is preferred in these areas.
Minimum 12 years of progressive software engineering experience.
Minimum 7 years leading software engineering teams.
Demonstrated success building enterprise SaaS products from concept to commercial launch.
Experience in startup or high-growth technology companies.
Proven experience scaling engineering organizations through periods of rapid growth.
Track record of delivering products that achieved measurable customer adoption and commercial success.


Work Environment

This role typically operates in a hybrid environment depending on organizational needs. Occasional travel may be required for industry events, networking, or candidate/client engagement.
