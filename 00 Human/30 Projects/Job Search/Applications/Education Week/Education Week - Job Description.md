## About the Company
Education Week has served as "the most trusted voice in American K-12 education" for over 40 years, now seeking technology leadership for its AI-enabled future.

## Position Overview
This combined CTO/CIO role offers executive scope with direct CEO access, leading a modern vendor ecosystem rather than managing large internal engineering teams.

## Key Responsibilities
- Develop a multi-year technology and information roadmap balancing editorial, audience growth, and revenue priorities.
- Lead vendor strategy including procurement and performance accountability.
- Establish data governance, cybersecurity, and information policies.
- Guide the small internal team and implementation partners on integrations and automation.
- Represent / communicate technology strategy to the CEO and board.

## Required Qualifications
- 8-10+ years technology leadership experience.
- Track record shaping executive strategy and budget decisions.
- Collaborative work style / comfort in collaborative environments.
- Ability to frame risk and investment tradeoffs in business terms.
- Fluency in modern publishing, subscription, and data / analytics platforms.
- Executive presence across CEO, board, and newsroom contexts.
