# Role Summary - Education Week

## At a glance
- **Company:** Education Week (published by Editorial Projects in Education, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit)
- **Role:** Chief Technology & Information Officer (combined CTO + CIO)
- **Location / remote:** Bethesda, MD (Hybrid)
- **Seniority:** Senior Executive, reports directly to the CEO
- **Compensation:** $180,000 - $220,000 base

## What the role is
> The single technology executive for a mission-driven, digital-first K-12 news operation. It fuses CTO and CIO scope: own the multi-year technology and information roadmap, run the vendor/SaaS ecosystem (procurement, performance, integrations, automation), set data governance, cybersecurity, and information policy, and translate all of it into business terms for the CEO and board. The seat explicitly favors orchestrating a modern vendor ecosystem and a small internal team over managing a large in-house engineering org. The stated reason it exists now is to lead EdWeek into "an AI-enabled future."

## Key requirements
- 8-10+ years of technology leadership experience.
- Track record shaping executive strategy and budget decisions.
- Collaborative work style.
- Ability to frame risk and investment tradeoffs in business terms.
- Fluency in modern publishing, subscription, and data / analytics platforms.
- Executive presence across CEO, board, and newsroom contexts.
