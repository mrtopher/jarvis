## Core responsibilities (nine areas)
1. **Engineering Strategy & Business Operations** — Lead the engineering operating cadence, develop strategic plans, coordinate annual planning, facilitate QBRs, run staff meetings, build executive dashboards/reporting, and drive accountability.
2. **Portfolio, Program & Transformation Management** — Oversee strategic/critical technology initiatives, ensure alignment with priorities, track execution, identify cross-team dependencies, and escalate issues.
3. **Financial Management & Budget Governance** — Own the engineering budget process, manage forecasting, monitor technology investments, establish financial reporting, and drive cost optimization.
4. **Governance, Compliance & Risk Coordination** — Manage risk reporting, support audit activities, facilitate compliance programs, and coordinate AI governance.
5. **Talent Strategy & Workforce Planning** — Lead workforce planning, identify skill gaps, develop talent strategies, oversee recruiting, and monitor talent metrics.
6. **Performance Management & Organizational Effectiveness** — Support calibration sessions, develop performance/improvement frameworks, and drive accountability.
7. **Employee Engagement, Culture & Communications** — Develop the engineering communications strategy, coordinate town halls, and plan offsites/culture initiatives.
8. **Vendor Governance & Executive Relationships** — Oversee strategic vendor relationships, monitor vendor performance, and track vendor risk.
9. **Executive Decision Support** — Serve as strategic advisor to the CTO, develop executive briefings, and prepare business cases.
