# Application Answers – Seeq (Engineering Leader, End-User Facing Squads)

## Why is this job interesting to you?

Two things. First, the mandate fits how I work best: you already have healthy squads with a real track record, and you want someone to take them further without breaking what works. Most of my best results have come from leveling up teams that were already good, not rescuing broken ones.

Second, the problem space is real. Industrial process data is a hard domain most software companies skip: streaming calculation and distributed computing on time-series data at scale. I would rather solve that than build another dashboard. It also helps that you are a hands-on shop that keeps its leaders in the code, which is where I stay, and that you have been remote-first for 13 years, which is how I do my best work.

## Tell us about your leadership style

I lead as a player-coach. I still get into the code, not to take work off my engineers but to stay close enough to be useful and to earn their trust. I set a clear direction, then get out of the way.

I hire good people and let them own their work, mistakes included. When something breaks I want the fix and the lesson, not a name to blame. That is why I build manager-of-managers layers, career ladders, and honest feedback loops. How you treat people is the real measure of leadership, and it pays off. At Industry Dive that approach lifted retention 15 percent while we grew from 8 to 40-plus.

I default to transparency. People do their best work when they know the why, the constraints, and where they stand with me.
