# Company Research – Seeq

## Snapshot
- **What they do:** Advanced analytics SaaS for industrial process data (IPD). Turns sensor/equipment time-series data from pharma, energy, oil & gas, chemicals, mining, and renewables (wind farms) into business insight. Core is a full-featured calculation engine doing complex math and ML on streaming time-series data.
- **Size / stage:** Growth-stage scaleup, founded 2013. Modest headcount for its funding (a few hundred employees), global team.
- **Funding / ownership:** Private, VC/growth-equity backed. Series D of $50M led by Sixth Street Growth (Aug 2024); ~$165-198M total across 10 rounds. Investors: Sixth Street Growth, Insight Partners, Altira Group, Second Avenue Partners, Saudi Aramco Energy Ventures (Aramco Ventures). Nari Ansari (Sixth Street) on the board.
- **HQ / locations:** Seattle, WA (nominal HQ); the company is remote-first (only).
- **Remote policy:** 100% remote since founding, 13+ years. Engineers work anywhere in a compatible time zone. Core hours 8am-12pm Pacific. No US F1 or H-1B sponsorship. Periodic travel expected for team/company face time.

## Products & business model
> B2B enterprise SaaS. Flagship is the Seeq platform (Workbench, Organizer, Data Lab) for engineers and scientists at industrial companies. Subscription/seat-based enterprise sales into process manufacturing. Recent direction: industrial AI insights and a March 2026 partnership with Innovapptive to connect those insights to frontline execution. Kubernetes-based SaaS with clusters in ~a dozen global regions on both AWS and Azure.

## Tech stack
> JVM languages (Java, Kotlin), Python, TypeScript/React, some C#/.NET. Kubernetes-based SaaS across ~12 global regions on AWS and Azure. Hard problems in streaming calculation, storage, and distributed computing. ML executed on streaming time-series data. Agile (Scrumban), CI/CD. Homegrown "Qube Virtual Office" collaboration tool, plus Zoom and Slack.

## Culture & values
> Kind-hearted, collaborative, teaching-oriented engineering culture. Strong internal transparency (leadership priorities, budget plans, account balances, board topics shared with staff). Remote done deliberately well. Values map directly to the JD's leadership bar: humble, empathetic, influence without authority, mentor/coach, not a micro-manager, lead with curiosity.

## Reputation
> Glassdoor 4.6/5 across 74 reviews, 90% would recommend. Work-life balance 4.6, culture & values 4.6, career opportunities 4.0. Named to Inc. Best Workplaces (2022), certified Great Place to Work, Technology Fast 500. Consistent praise for remote execution and quality of people.

## Recent news
- Aug 2024: $50M Series D led by Sixth Street Growth (verify no newer raise before interviews).
- Mar 2026: Strategic partnership with Innovapptive to connect industrial AI insights to frontline execution.
- 2026: Hosting Conneqt North America 2026 user conference.

## Green flags
- Elite remote-first culture with a 13-year track record; matches Chris's remote-first, VA-based search perfectly.
- $290K base + commission is at/above the $250-300K target.
- Strong, distinctive market (industrial process data) with real technical depth: streaming calc, distributed computing, ML on time-series.
- Hands-on-technical-leader mandate over ~half of engineering (end-user facing squads) is a clean VP-of-Engineering shape.
- Healthy funding, marquee investors, and a stated growth trajectory.
- Cloud/K8s multi-cloud (AWS + Azure) and CI/CD/Agile are direct strengths.

## Red flags
- Domain is new: industrial process data / process manufacturing is not Chris's vertical (adjacent, learnable, but real).
- Stack has JVM (Java/Kotlin) and some C#/.NET at the core; Chris's hands-on depth is more web/AWS/Python/JS. "Hands-on technical leader" bar needs an honest framing (leads and reviews, decades of engineering, not a daily Kotlin committer).
- Glassdoor notes: comp/career-development concerns and that firings happen quietly ("you hear by word of mouth or not at all") — probe internal mobility and how performance exits are handled.
- No visa sponsorship (not an issue for Chris; noted for completeness).
- Comp is "base + commission" for an engineering leadership seat — confirm how the commission/variable works for a non-sales eng role.
