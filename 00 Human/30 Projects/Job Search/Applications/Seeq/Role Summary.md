# Role Summary – Seeq (Engineering Leader, End-User Facing Squads)

## At a glance
- **Company:** Seeq — advanced analytics SaaS for industrial process data.
- **Role:** Engineering leader over the end-user-facing squads (~half of engineering); expand engineering capacity as the company grows.
- **Location / remote:** Remote-first (only). Any compatible time zone, core hours 8am-12pm Pacific. Periodic travel. No F1/H-1B sponsorship.
- **Seniority:** Senior engineering leadership (VP/Head-of-Engineering shape), reports into the CTO org.
- **Compensation:** $290K USD base + commission (at/above the $250-300K target).

## What the role is
> A hands-on technical leader to run roughly half of Seeq's engineering (the end-user-facing squads) and take an already-solid, well-run organization "to the next level" while expanding capacity for growth. Not a turnaround. The bar is a credible player-coach who is still current technically, leads through influence rather than authority, coaches and resolves conflict, and keeps a healthy, transparent, remote-first culture intact. The stack is JVM (Java/Kotlin), Python, TypeScript/React, some C#/.NET, on Kubernetes across ~12 regions on AWS and Azure, with a streaming time-series calculation engine at the core. Delivery is Scrumban with CI/CD.

## Key requirements
- Proven track record managing and leveling up technical teams.
- Hands-on technical leader whose skills stay current (contributing meaningfully even as a leader).
- Distributed computing experience and deep cloud-ecosystem knowledge.
- Deep enterprise SDLC: Agile (Scrumban), CI/CD, discovery, design, QA, support.
- Inspiring, humble, empathetic coach; influences without authority; transparent; resolves conflict; not a micro-manager.
- CS degree or equivalent experience; leads with curiosity; willing to travel periodically.
