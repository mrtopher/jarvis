# Company Research – Freely Payments

## Snapshot
- **What they do:** B2B fintech for the wholesale food-distribution industry. Lets distributors accept card payments while cutting processing costs (interchange optimization, intelligent routing, exclusive network rates, cash-back programs). Building an integrated ecosystem: acceptance, automated reconciliation and cash application, merchant analytics, co-branded card programs.
- **Size / stage:** Early stage. Founding-level engineering hire signals a small existing team. Venture-backed, has signed several large distributors with a contracted backlog waiting to launch.
- **Funding / ownership:** Venture-backed. Mastercard is both an investor and strategic partner. Selected for Mastercard's Start Path SMB program; received a 2024 Catalyst Grant from the Arlington Innovation Fund (one of five Arlington startups). [OPEN ITEM: total raised, round, and lead investors not confirmed; likely seed / early stage.]
- **HQ / locations:** Arlington, Virginia (DC metro).
- **Remote policy:** On-site in Arlington, VA for this role. (Local for Chris.)

## Products & business model
> Payment-acceptance platform for wholesale food distributors, a segment that historically avoided cards because of high fees. Freely uses interchange optimization, transaction routing, and network partnerships to cut processing cost by up to 50 percent, and to near zero in some programs. Revenue comes from the payment economics plus a growing SaaS layer: onboarding, billing, automated reconciliation and cash application, merchant analytics/reporting, and co-branded card programs. Distribution via industry buying groups and supplier partnerships (e.g. Frosty Acres, UniPro Foodservice).

## Tech stack
> From the posting: Angular / TypeScript / RxJS / Tailwind on the frontend; Node.js / TypeScript / REST APIs on the backend; Google Cloud Platform (Cloud Run, GKE, Pub/Sub, Cloud SQL, Firestore) for infrastructure; GitHub Actions, Sentry, Linear, Notion for tooling. Cloud-native, event-driven, GCP-first. Chris's GCP, Kubernetes, event-driven, PostgreSQL/Cloud SQL, and CI/CD experience map directly; Node/TypeScript backend depth is the honest stretch.

## Culture & values
> Founder-led, urgency-driven, early-stage builder culture. The posting names a "culture of accountability, urgency, collaboration, and technical excellence." Team is composed of payments, food-distribution, and enterprise-software veterans. Small enough that the VP Eng is a true founding seat with wide ownership and a direct line to the CEO.

## Reputation
> Too early for meaningful Glassdoor signal. Public reputation is positive and momentum-oriented: Mastercard Start Path selection, Arlington Catalyst Grant, and supplier-partnership announcements with national foodservice buying groups. Read culture and stability live in the interviews.

## Recent news

## Green flags
- True founding engineering leadership role: build the org, the architecture, and the culture from the ground up, reporting directly to the CEO.
- Real traction, not a concept: signed major distributors, a contracted backlog, and meaningful payment volume already flowing.
- Mastercard as investor and strategic partner de-risks the network access and lends credibility.
- On-site Arlington, VA is local for Chris (Phone2Action was Arlington), so the on-site requirement is a plus, not a blocker.
- GCP-native, event-driven, cloud-native stack lines up with Chris's platform and infra experience.
- Clear, revenue-tied first-six-months mandate (move the signed backlog into live production), which suits a build-and-ship leader.

## Red flags
- Payments / fintech domain is new for Chris: interchange, reconciliation, cash application, settlement, merchant acquiring, and card-network mechanics are core to the role and are a genuine gap.
- Node.js / TypeScript backend is the primary stack; Chris's hands-on backend depth is PHP, so "advanced Node/TS" is a stretch (softened by "or comparable modern backend technologies" and "experience with every component is not required").
- Early stage: comp is "competitive executive-level salary" with meaningful equity but no number. Cash could sit below the $250-300K target with upside in equity. Confirm the band, funding runway, and round.
- On-site five days is implied. Manageable locally, but confirm the exact on-site expectation and any flexibility.
- Founding seat at a small company: high ownership, thin support, and heavy hands-on load early. Confirm current team size and near-term hiring budget.
