# Role Summary – Freely Payments VP of Engineering

## At a glance
- **Company:** Freely Payments (B2B payments fintech for the food-distribution industry, early stage, Mastercard-backed)
- **Role:** Vice President of Engineering (founding-level, owns the engineering org and the platform)
- **Location / remote:** On-site, Arlington, VA (local for Chris)
- **Seniority:** VP / founding technical leader, reports directly to the CEO
- **Compensation:** Competitive executive-level salary + meaningful early-stage equity (no number stated)

## What the role is
> Build Freely's engineering organization from the ground up and own the architecture and technical direction of the payments platform. The near-term job is to turn signed distributor demand into live production volume by accelerating integrations and hardening onboarding, payment processing, billing, reconciliation, analytics, and reporting. As volume grows toward billions of dollars, build automated reconciliation and cash application so the platform scales without scaling headcount at the same rate. Stay hands-on enough to review architecture and solve hard problems, work directly with the CEO and founders, and build the team, standards, and culture.

## Key requirements
- 10+ years engineering, 5+ years managing/developing teams; proven at building and scaling technical orgs
- Strong hands-on background with credible architectural leadership
- Advanced Node.js / TypeScript, or comparable modern backend
- Cloud-native, distributed, event-driven systems
- Secure, scalable, highly available platforms handling sensitive or high-volume data
- API design, data architecture, integrations, testing, deployment automation, production ops
- SDLC practices, code-quality standards, CI/CD, observability, incident response
- Project/program management; cross-functional partnership; executive communication
- Preferred: fintech / payments / financial-infrastructure domain, high-transaction-volume systems, processor/bank/ERP integrations, venture-backed startup experience, founding an engineering function

### Why apply
- Founding org-builder mandate is exactly what Chris has done: build the team, standards, and culture from zero, then ship.
- Zero-to-production speed under revenue pressure (Pipeline 360, under 90 days) maps to the first-six-months "move the backlog live" priority.

- Node.js / TypeScript is the core backend and not Chris's hands-on depth (PHP is), though "or comparable" and "not every component required" soften it.

- Founding seat at a small company means thin support and a heavy hands-on load early.
