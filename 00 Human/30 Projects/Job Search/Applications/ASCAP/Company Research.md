# Company Research – ASCAP

## Snapshot
- **What they do:** Performing rights organization (PRO). Licenses public performance of musical works and collects/distributes royalties to songwriters, composers, and publishers. Membership association of 1M+ creators; licenses a repertory of 20M+ works.
- **Size / stage:** Large, established non-profit (founded 1914). Not-for-profit basis — returns ~90 cents of every dollar to members as royalties. Stable, mission-driven, not a startup.
- **Funding / ownership:** Member-owned not-for-profit. No VC/PE runway risk.
- **HQ / locations:** New York, NY (250 W. 57th St). Also Nashville, LA, Atlanta, Miami, DC offices.
- **Remote policy (this role):** In office three days per week in NYC + occasional travel. **Not remote.**

## Products & business model
> Core business is licensing (blanket licenses to radio, TV, streaming, venues, businesses) and royalty distribution at massive transaction volume. The engineering org this VP would lead is **Licensing & Finance Engineering** — customer-facing and internal apps for licensing workflows and royalty finance. This is enterprise business-systems engineering (CRM + finance + integration + data at large transaction volume), not consumer media/product engineering.

## Tech stack
> Salesforce (hands-on APEX + declarative), AWS (EC2, S3, AppFlow, Lambda, RDS, SQS), Java, integration middleware (Boomi, Workato, Mulesoft), ERP (Netsuite, Great Plains), Business Objects, databases (SQL Server, Oracle, RDS), .NET, REST + SOAP web services, Jira/Agile. A Salesforce-centric enterprise + finance-integration stack.

## Leadership & recent news
- **New CTO:** John Pershing joined as SVP & Chief Technology Officer, effective Sept 22, 2025 (ex-CTO/Head of Product at What If Media Group). Press framed the hire around harnessing AI. A new CTO usually means roadmap/reorg energy and modernization mandate — relevant upside for an incoming VP.
- **SVP Software Engineering & Architecture:** Bolmar Carrasquilla — the most likely "Head of Engineering" this VP reports to (report line to be confirmed; could be a distinct Head of Engineering under the CTO).
- AI is an active theme under the new CTO — aligns with Chris's AI-native identity if the role weighted that more heavily.

## Culture & reputation
> Glassdoor ~3.0/5 overall (173 reviews), ~3.1/5 in NYC (67 reviews). Work-life balance ~3.5, diversity/inclusion ~3.6, culture/values ~3.0, **career opportunities ~2.3-3.0 (weak)**, **comp/benefits ~2.7 with explicit "underpaid for NYC cost of living" complaints**. ~41% would recommend. Positives: benefits, people, new modern NYC office, some schedule flexibility (though this VP seat is 3 days on-site). Net: stable and mission-driven but with below-market-pay perception and limited advancement signals.

## Green flags
- Stable, mission-driven non-profit — zero runway risk, rare in this pipeline.
- New AI-forward CTO (Pershing) — modernization mandate an incoming VP could ride.
- Real senior scope: ~25+ engineers, budget/headcount ownership, exec + cross-functional exposure.
- Comp $240-260K base — at/near target, disclosed. Strong benefits + profit-share.
- AWS-based (a genuine Chris strength) and AI-awareness required.

## Red flags
- **On-site NYC 3 days/week** — against Chris's remote-first, VA-based search. Relocation or heavy commute required. Near-disqualifying for the stated search unless he'll relocate.
- **Salesforce/APEX + Boomi/Mulesoft + Java + ERP + .NET core** — enterprise CRM/finance-integration engineering, not Chris's consumer-web/JS + AI stack. Multiple *required* quals are genuine gaps.
- **Domain pivot:** licensing + royalty finance systems, not consumer media/product.
- Below-market-pay perception for NYC + weak career-opportunity ratings on Glassdoor.
