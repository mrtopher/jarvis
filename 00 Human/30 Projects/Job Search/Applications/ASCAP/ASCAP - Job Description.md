**Job Title:** VP, Software Engineering
**Company:** ASCAP (American Society of Composers, Authors and Publishers)
**Location:** New York, NY (250 W. 57th Street, New York, NY 10107)
**Employment Type:** Full Time
**Compensation:** $240,000 to $260,000 per year (base salary)
**Remote Policy:** In office three days per week (occasional travel for in-person meetings)

## About the Company
ASCAP is a membership association of more than one million songwriters, composers and music publishers. As the only U.S. performing rights organization operating on a not-for-profit basis, ASCAP licenses a repertory of over 20 million musical works and returns nearly 90 cents of every dollar back to its members as royalties.

## Job Overview
The VP will provide leadership and technology vision for ASCAP's Licensing and Finance Engineering team, reporting directly to the Head of Engineering. Responsibilities encompass managing a team of approximately 25+ engineers working on customer-facing and internal applications using Salesforce, AWS, Java, Boomi, RDBMS, ERP, and data management tools.

## Key Responsibilities
- Provide strategic and technical leadership for the Licensing & Finance Engineering team
- Drive vision, delivery, and technical implementation for roadmap projects
- Recruit and retain top engineering talent in partnership with Talent Acquisition
- Enhance Agile processes for faster time-to-market
- Continuously improve software engineering practices and application security
- Lead and mentor the engineering team through hands-on technical collaboration
- Encourage innovation, cutting-edge technologies, inclusion, and diversity
- Drive accountability for delivery among team members
- Provide continuous career development for the engineering team
- Monitor and update team structure based on business needs
- Handle forecasting, budgeting, and headcount planning
- Maintain relationships with Product Management, stakeholders, Program Management, Customer Success, and Licensing
- Design and continuously improve the engineering delivery process
- Communicate delivery pipeline status to relevant parties

- Experience driving accountability for project deliverables
