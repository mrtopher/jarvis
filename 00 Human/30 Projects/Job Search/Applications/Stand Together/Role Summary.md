# Role Summary – Stand Together Director of Engineering - BTP

## At a glance
- **Company:** Stand Together (Koch-founded philanthropic community, 1,300+ staff) — Be The People platform
- **Role:** Director of Engineering, BTP (btp.org)
- **Location / remote:** Arlington, Virginia (remote/hybrid policy not stated — confirm)
- **Seniority:** Director; top engineering leader on the platform in a "pre-CTO" environment
- **Compensation:** $160,000 - $240,000 base + bonus

## What the role is
> The senior-most engineering leader for Be The People, a new national, consumer-facing civic platform built by agency Work & Co and now moving in-house. The job is to set technical direction (architecture, data model, integrations for btp.org), build and manage the internal engineering team, decide when to build vs use contractors/staff augmentation, lead the knowledge transfer from Work & Co, and own reliability and iteration velocity through the summer launch and beyond (same-week content updates, two-week code cycles). It is a hands-on, foundation-setting, player-coach seat with a path to CTO.

## Key requirements
- 10+ years software engineering; 5+ years leading/managing teams
- Deep infrastructure and back-end experience
- Running production, consumer-facing platforms with continuous availability
- Build-vs-buy judgment and managing a contractor/staff-augmentation mix
- Strong technical judgment across modern web, API, cloud
- Building engineering culture, hiring, growing senior ICs
- Comfort in a pre-CTO environment; durable foundations without over-fixing
- Startup / early-stage / entrepreneurial background
- Track record using AI-enabled tools to accelerate
