# Company Research – Stand Together

## Snapshot
- **What they do:** Philanthropic community founded by Charles Koch (est. 2003) that funds and supports "changemakers" across education, economy, poverty, criminal justice, immigration, free expression, and civic/community issues. Provides partners funding, thought leadership, a peer network, and a "playbook" built on Principle Based Management.
- **Size / stage:** Large and established. 1,300+ employees. Chairman & CEO: Brian Hooks. The BTP platform team itself is early-stage/greenfield inside the larger org.
- **Funding / ownership:** Koch-funded philanthropic network (not a VC/PE company). Related entities include Stand Together Trust, Charles Koch Foundation, Charles Koch Institute.
- **HQ / locations:** Arlington, Virginia. This role is listed as Arlington, VA.
- **Remote policy:** Not stated in the posting. Arlington HQ location implies on-site or hybrid. Confirm early. (Note: this is local/convenient for Chris, who worked DC/Arlington at Industry Dive and Phone2Action.)

## Products & business model
> The relevant product is **Be The People (btp.org / bethepeople.org)**, a 10-year civic campaign launched in 2026 to "reignite the American spirit" and unite Americans across political lines to solve community problems. The digital platform connects community organizations solving local problems with millions of Americans who want to help (volunteering, donation drives, neighbor-to-neighbor action). A National Leadership Council of 50+ cross-sector leaders backs it. Partner initiative "TogetherTuesday" ran with GivingTuesday on 2026-06-23. The platform was built by digital agency **Work & Co** and is now transitioning to an internal engineering team, which is the reason this role exists. It is consumer-facing, national scale, with a summer launch and continuous-availability requirements.

## Tech stack
> Not disclosed. JD signals: modern web, APIs, cloud infrastructure, middleware, back-end and infrastructure-heavy, SRE/continuous availability. Work & Co built the initial platform, so early stack decisions are theirs; the internal team inherits and operates it. Confirm cloud (AWS/GCP/Azure) and framework in early conversations.

## Culture & values
> Principle Based Management (PBM): dignity, openness, bottom-up empowerment, entrepreneurial autonomy. Glassdoor: 3.8/5 work-life balance, praised mission/people/benefits. BUT a recurring, credible counter-signal: reviews describe micromanagement, "layers of leadership override experts," decisions driven by internal politics, and "cult-like" we-believe rhetoric. For a "pre-CTO" director who needs real autonomy to set foundations, that gap between the PBM branding and the reported reality is the thing to probe.

## Reputation
> Glassdoor 3.5/5 across ~148 reviews, 71% would recommend. Positives: benefits (6% 401k immediate vesting, flexible TO, Peloton/Talkspace), mission, high-caliber colleagues. Negatives: micromanagement, indecisiveness, politics-over-expertise, diversity concerns (people of color underrepresented in budget-holding roles). Externally, the Koch network draws political scrutiny (e.g. DeSmog on climate/energy positions).

## Green flags
- Requirements fit is unusually clean (see Role Summary), especially the build-vs-buy / staff-augmentation mandate, which maps directly to Chris's consulting-CTO reality.
- Comp is disclosed: $160K-$240K base + bonus. Real number, no guessing.
- Greenfield, pre-CTO seat: top engineering leader on a brand-new national platform, with room to set durable foundations and a plausible path to CTO.
- Excellent benefits and a genuinely mission-forward, well-resourced parent org (not a runway-risk startup).
- Local to Chris (Arlington, VA), if on-site/hybrid is required.

## Red flags
- **Comp and title are below Chris's recent trajectory.** Director of Engineering (a notch below his CTO/SVP/VP titles) at $160K-$240K base, under the $250K-$300K target. Bonus may narrow the gap, but this reads as a lateral-to-down move on paper.
- **Culture gap:** PBM "empowerment" branding vs Glassdoor reports of micromanagement and experts being overridden. Directly threatens the autonomy a pre-CTO foundation-setter needs.
- **Values / political alignment:** Koch-network philanthropy. BTP is explicitly framed as cross-partisan, but the parent network carries strong political associations. This is a personal-values call only Chris can make.
- Inherited codebase from an agency (Work & Co) plus a "staff augmentation partner" to integrate: real technical-debt and knowledge-transfer risk on a live, summer-launched platform.
