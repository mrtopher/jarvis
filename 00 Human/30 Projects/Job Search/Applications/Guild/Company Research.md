# Company Research – Guild

## Snapshot
- **What they do:** B2B2C workforce-education platform. Guild sits between large employers and a network of learning providers/universities, delivering education-as-a-benefit (tuition assistance, upskilling, coaching, career navigation) plus talent-mobility insights back to the employer. Members are frontline/hourly employees at partner companies; the employer pays.
- **Size / stage:** ~1,200-1,300 employees. Late-stage private (Series F). Past peak growth, now in a cost-discipline / turnaround posture.
- **Funding / ownership:** Raised $730M+ total. Peak valuation $4.4B (Series F, $175M, 2022). Secondary-market activity in 2024 implied ~$1.46B-$1.98B, a 55-66% haircut from peak. Investors: Bessemer, General Catalyst, Oprah Winfrey, others. Certified B Corp; female-founded.
- **HQ / locations:** Denver, CO. Remote allowed in 32 states + DC. **VA is on the list**, so a Virginia-based remote candidate qualifies.
- **Remote policy:** Remote-eligible for this role across the listed 32 states. Comp standardized nationwide (no regional variance).

## Products & business model
> Employer-funded education benefit. Employers (Chipotle, Target, Walgreens, JPMorgan Chase, Hilton, Spectrum, PepsiCo, Tyson, etc.) fund learning; Guild runs the marketplace, advising/coaching, and talent-insight analytics. Revenue comes from employer contracts and the learning-provider network. Classic B2B2C: the buyer is the employer, the user is the employee/member. Typical spend cited at $3K-$6K/year per member across partner programs.

## Tech stack
> From the JD: modern AWS serverless (Lambda, Aurora), GraphQL, Auth0 for identity, CI/CD, shared internal developer platform, AI-native SDLC with agentic workflows and platform guardrails. Data/AI engineering is a named partner org shipping AI-native product experiences. This is a strong, direct match to Chris's AWS + AI-native + platform profile.

## Culture & values
> Publicly: mission-driven (economic mobility for frontline workers), B Corp, DEI-forward, "psychologically safe / sustainable pace" language in the JD. Awards through 2025 (Fortune Change the World 2025, Forbes Cloud 100 2020-2025, Great Place to Work 2022-2025). Internally (see Reputation) the lived experience diverges sharply from the brand.

## Reputation
> **Glassdoor ~2.8/5** (460+ reviews), ~38% recommend, ~39% positive outlook. Recurring themes: repeated layoffs (reportedly 12% then 25% in back-to-back years, plus sporadic cuts), frequent re-orgs, priority thrash, and a "chaotic" engineering culture where teams pivot repeatedly and work gets thrown away. Leadership favorability low (new CEO Bijal Shah ~37% on Glassdoor after founder Rachel Romer's health-driven transition). Some positive reviews cite mission, flexibility, and work-life balance. Trade press (Higher Education Inquirer) has run critical pieces on layoffs and a "promise to precarity" narrative into 2026.

## Recent news
- Founder/CEO Rachel Romer stepped back after a stroke; Bijal Shah became CEO (2024).
- Rohan Chandran joined as Chief Product & Technology Officer (Jan 2024), consolidating product + engineering + platform + data + technology strategy.
- Valuation reset well below the 2022 peak; ongoing cost discipline and layoffs through 2024-2026.

## Green flags
- Comp is transparent and strong: **$300,000-$355,950 base + variable + stock**, top of / above Chris's $250-300K target, standardized nationwide.
- Remote-eligible including VA.
- Tech-stack and mandate match is one of the cleanest in the pipeline: AWS serverless (Lambda/Aurora), GraphQL, AI-native SDLC, internal developer platform, B2B2C growth-product engineering.
- Real mission (economic mobility for frontline workers) and marquee employer logos.
- Genuine VP-level manager-of-managers scope (Directors + EMs across product, platform, and foundational engineering).

## Red flags
- **Turnaround, not a growth story.** Valuation down 55-66% from peak; repeated large layoffs; "is my job ever safe" sentiment in 2026 reviews.
- **Chaotic engineering culture + priority thrash** — the exact conditions that make a VP's "lightweight process, drive to outcomes" mandate hard, and that put a new leader's own seat at risk.
- Low leadership favorability and a documented brand-vs-reality gap (B Corp / Great Place to Work awards vs 2.8 Glassdoor).
- CPTO structure (Chandran owns product + tech) can compress a VP of Engineering's autonomy; confirm the reporting line and real decision authority.
