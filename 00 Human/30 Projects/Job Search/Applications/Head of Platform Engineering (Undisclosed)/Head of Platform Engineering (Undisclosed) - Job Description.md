# Head of Platform Engineering — Job Description (verbatim)

> Source: pasted into `/job-apply` on 2026-07-06. No company name, URL, or contact was included in the posting. This is the archival copy — unedited.

About the job
A rapidly growing technology organization is seeking a Head of Platform Engineering to lead cloud infrastructure, platform engineering, site reliability engineering, and developer enablement initiatives. This role will oversee a high-performing engineering organization responsible for building scalable infrastructure, improving software delivery, and enabling engineering teams through modern platform capabilities.

The ideal candidate combines executive leadership experience with deep technical expertise across cloud-native technologies, infrastructure automation, reliability engineering, and software development. This role will partner closely with senior leadership to define platform strategy, accelerate engineering productivity, and support long-term business growth.

What You'll Tackle:

Lead and scale Platform Engineering, Site Reliability Engineering (SRE), and Infrastructure Engineering functions.
Define and execute long-term platform, cloud, and infrastructure strategies.
Build and evolve internal developer platforms that improve engineering productivity and operational efficiency.
Drive modernization initiatives across cloud-native architectures and distributed systems.
Improve observability, monitoring, reliability, automation, and deployment practices.
Partner with engineering leadership to enhance developer experience and software delivery processes.
Establish engineering standards, governance models, and operational best practices.
Support infrastructure automation, platform self-service capabilities, and Infrastructure-as-Code initiatives.
Collaborate with executive leadership on technology strategy, scalability, and organizational growth.
Mentor engineering leaders and help build scalable engineering structures and processes.
Foster a culture of reliability, innovation, and continuous improvement.

What You Bring:

10+ years of experience in platform engineering, cloud infrastructure, site reliability engineering, or related disciplines.
Proven experience leading and scaling engineering organizations.
Strong hands-on experience with Kubernetes and cloud-native technologies.
Ability to balance technical leadership with hands-on engineering engagement.
Experience building internal developer platforms or platform-as-a-product capabilities.
Experience operating within high-growth and product-focused technology organizations.
Strong software engineering background using languages such as Python, Go, Java, or similar.
Experience with Infrastructure-as-Code and automation frameworks.
Strong expertise in observability, monitoring, reliability, and operational excellence.
Deep understanding of distributed systems, containers, cloud platforms, and infrastructure automation.
Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills across technical and executive audiences.

Nice to Have:

Experience with AI-enabled developer tooling, engineering productivity platforms, or automation initiatives.
Exposure to OpenStack, VMware, or hybrid infrastructure environments.
Experience with policy-as-code, governance automation, or security engineering initiatives.
Experience leading cloud transformation or large-scale modernization programs.
Familiarity with multi-cloud or enterprise-scale infrastructure environments.

What Success Looks Like:

Scalable, reliable, and secure platform infrastructure.
Increased developer productivity and software delivery velocity.
Strong operational performance and platform reliability.
Successful execution of cloud and platform modernization initiatives.
High-performing engineering teams and leadership pipelines.
Technology platforms that support long-term organizational growth and innovation.
