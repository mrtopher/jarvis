## How to unmask the employer (do this before applying)
1. **Ask the recruiter directly:** "Which company is this for, what's the comp band, and is it remote?" A legitimate retained search will answer. A refusal is itself a signal.
2. **Reverse-search a distinctive JD phrase** in quotes on LinkedIn Jobs / Google (e.g. "developer enablement initiatives" + "Site Reliability Engineering (SRE)") to find the original branded posting.
3. **Check the recruiter's LinkedIn** for which client logos they typically place — narrows the field fast.
4. **Match the tech fingerprint:** a growth-stage company hiring a Head of Platform that still runs OpenStack/VMware hybrid is a narrow set. Cross-reference with recent funding news.
