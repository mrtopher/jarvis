## Summary
Dan brought a live prospect: a wholesale construction-materials distributor (tri-state, ~30 orders/day, next-day delivery, no internal technical staff). They run a ~25-year-old distribution ERP called **inSQL** and Dan is standing up **route-optimization software (Optimal Route)** to digitize dispatch and proof-of-delivery. He needs an integration layer between the two systems and wants Dual Logic to scope it. Dan's own fractional developer passed (won't touch a sub-$50k full-stack job), so Dan landed on **n8n** as the lower-cost, more supportable path — which also opens the door to a support retainer with a firm like Dual Logic instead of Dan being on the hook long term. Chris confirmed the core is straightforward (two workflows: orders inSQL→Optimal Route, proof-of-delivery Optimal Route→inSQL, keyed on order number), both systems are REST APIs, and n8n's hosted plan ($20/mo, 2,500 executions) comfortably covers ~1,800 executions/mo. Bigger value comes after go-live once the client sees what's possible. Both agreed to keep it foundational/digitization-first and explicitly steer away from unnecessary AI/agentic scope.

## Decisions
- Integration platform = **n8n** (hosted, not self-hosted — client has no technical staff and won't need community nodes). Lower cost of entry, and n8n developers are easy to hire for future support.
- Scope is **two workflows only** for phase one: orders in, proof-of-delivery out. No pulling Optimal Route's delivery-tracking data back into inSQL (no customer value there).
- Keep it **digitization-first**. Layered automations (AP/AR workflow automation, order-intake automation) come later as separately quoted phases after the foundation is running.
- **No agentic AI** for order handling — Dan explicitly does not want an LLM substituting products. Any AI would be optional dashboards/smart-routing later, out of scope now.
- Pricing model direction: **fixed price** for the initial build (Dan packages one price, eats overages, no time-and-materials), plus an optional **3–6 month support retainer** as a safety net.
