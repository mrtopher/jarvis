# Why AI Hasn't Replaced Software Engineers, and Won't

## Summary
> AI as Normal Technology (Substack) by **Arvind Narayanan and Sayash Kapoor**, June 11, 2026. Thesis: AI has compressed the **execution** layer of software work but has NOT reduced demand for engineers, because **decision-making and accountability stay human**. Much of the "AI replaced my job" narrative is **AI washing** covering financially-driven layoffs. This is near-perfect ammunition for Chris's flagship belief ("AI won't replace software engineers, it will make them stronger") and the AI-slop pillar.

## Key Points
- **AI washing distorts the story.** Layoffs blamed on AI (Block, Snap, Intuit) were financial/restructuring. Across 25,000 NY layoffs, only ONE company checked the "AI-related" box on WARN Act filings.
- **Decide-Execute-Deliver "sandwich" model.** Decide (spec/planning) resists automation; Execute (design/implementation) is what AI compresses; Deliver (testing/verification/accountability) resists automation. Execution was never the bottleneck.
- **Code writing was never the bottleneck.** Devs spend only "9% to 61%" of time coding (Microsoft). Meetings, debugging, decisions dominate - areas AI can't fully automate.
- **Employment still growing**, just ~3 percentage points slower annually post-ChatGPT; doesn't even count entrepreneurship gains.
- **Vibe coding != agentic engineering.** Unsupervised AI code introduces vulnerabilities at **9x** the human-only rate. Pros use agents as supervised tools, not replacements.

## Notable statistics (verbatim)
- 2024 NBER study: "eight-fold increase in lines of code written" but only "30% more releases."
- 59% of hiring managers admit emphasizing AI when explaining layoffs (better optics).
- 21% of executives made large anticipatory headcount cuts; only 2% based on actual AI results.
- 44% of agent-produced code survives into user commits (SWE-chat dataset).
