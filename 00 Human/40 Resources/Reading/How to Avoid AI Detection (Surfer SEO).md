# How to Avoid AI Detection (Surfer SEO)

## Summary
> Surfer SEO guide (author Danni Roseman) on making AI-assisted writing read as human. Frames detection around two signals detectors measure — **perplexity** (word predictability) and **burstiness** (sentence-length variation) — then lists techniques to raise both. Honest core point: don't chase a perfect detector score, make genuinely helpful content; Google penalizes mass-produced low-effort AI, not thoughtfully refined AI-assisted work. Relevant to VOICE.md / the no-ai-slop practice as a mechanistic explanation of *why* the anti-slop edits work.

## Key Points
- **What detectors measure**: perplexity (how predictable the next word is) and burstiness (variation in sentence length/rhythm). Human writing is less predictable and more varied.
- **Structural edits beat synonym swaps**: reorganizing ideas and flow matters far more than surface word-swapping; combine and break sentences to disrupt uniform patterns.
- **Kill AI tell-words**: replace "delve," "leverage," "moreover," etc. with conversational alternatives (mirrors VOICE.md's banned-word list).
- **Vary vocabulary / read aloud**: eliminate formulaic repetition that lowers perplexity.
- **Insert genuine anecdotes + perspective**: first-hand experience is what machines can't fabricate — the strongest human signal.
- **Better prompts → less predictable output**: descriptive, style-specific prompts up front reduce the humanizing work later.
- **Human editor in the loop**: fact-check, flag suspicious language, enforce authentic voice.
- **Context**: YMYL fields (health, finance) need stricter human oversight; helpfulness is the real ranking target regardless of how content is made.
