# What Is AI Governance (Docker)

## Summary
> Docker blog primer defining AI governance as the frameworks, policies, and controls for building, deploying, and overseeing AI systems responsibly — spanning ethics, compliance, risk management, and technical safeguards across the full AI lifecycle. Framed as an operating model for responsible AI, analogous to how engineering teams rely on CI/CD for software delivery. Angle ties governance to Docker's containerization/runtime story (embedding governance into dev workflows). Relevant to the AI consulting work — a clean framing to bring to clients scaling AI agents.

## Key Points
- **Definition:** governance = the rules, roles, and review processes keeping AI aligned with business goals, legal requirements, and ethical standards, from development through monitoring.
- **The gap it closes:** ~60% of orgs already run AI agents in production, but ~40% cite security & compliance as the #1 barrier to scaling further.
- **Three core questions it answers:** who is accountable for AI decisions, what standards systems must meet, and how performance/compliance are monitored over time.
- **Agent-era emphasis:** with agents operating autonomously in production, governance must also cover runtime security, access control, and agent-specific oversight.
- **Strategic takeaway:** embed governance into dev workflows early → scale AI more safely and stay ahead of evolving regulation.
