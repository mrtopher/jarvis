# Forward Deployed Executives - The Next Billion-Dollar Opportunity

## Summary
> Essay by **Rick Manelius** (serial founder, MIT PhD, ~18 yrs in startups + 12 months full-time AI consulting). Thesis: the next big opportunity is the **Forward Deployed Executive (FDX)** — a trusted advisor who *embeds with leadership* to drive AI adoption, the exec-level analog to Palantir's Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE). The bottleneck on AI ROI isn't the technology; it's **human adoption speed and executive fear/uncertainty**, which engineers alone can't fix because they can't influence the boardroom. Directly reinforces Chris's AI-consulting positioning — the "embed with the exec, sell adoption not tools" angle. Pairs with [[The AI-Pilled Operating Company]].

## Key Points
- **The gap:** >95% of enterprise AI pilots fail to produce measurable returns. The blocker is organizational + emotional adoption, not model capability.
- **FDEs aren't enough:** technical engineers can close the build gap but can't move boardroom decisions. CEOs whose orgs build strong AI foundations are ~3x more likely to report financial returns.
- **Psychology is the product:** execs feel genuine anxiety about AI. One told Manelius, *"You're just my security blanket, so I want you here."* They need a trusted confidant, not LinkedIn tips.
- **What an FDX is:** technical depth (architecture, best practices) + management expertise (cost, timelines, teams) + entrepreneurial ROI mindset + AI-first thinking + coaching/adaptability.
- **Takeaway:** to capture AI ROI you have to embed *at the executive level* and close the organizational and emotional gaps, not just ship technology.
