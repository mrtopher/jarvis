# Five Studies Changing How I Think About AI in Software Engineering

## Summary
> Engineering Enablement newsletter (DX) piece by **Brian Houck** (Applied Scientist at DX), July 10, 2026. Thesis: AI is accelerating **upstream code generation** faster than orgs can build systems to safely understand, verify, and deliver that code. **The bottleneck has moved downstream** to reviewing, integrating, understanding, and shipping. Directly reinforces Chris's content pillars: AI strengthens engineers (it does not replace them), and AI slop vs quality. Good source material for [[Dual Logic Platform]] positioning and a `/content` piece for "Joe CEO."

## Key Points
- **Convergent finding across all five papers:** "we're generating code faster than we're generating the systems needed to safely understand, verify, and deliver it." AI and humans are **complements, not substitutes**.
- **1. Copilot & productivity** (Heilman, Kyllo, Murphy-Hill): 16,223 devs, each compared to themselves over 43 weeks. Highest-Copilot-usage weeks tied to **~40% more completed PRs per hour of coding time** vs no-usage weeks. Strongest gains on larger PRs.
- **2. Writing code vs shipping code** (Demirer, Musolff, Yang, NBER): 100,000+ GitHub devs. AI lifts commits **40-180%**, but **shipping (releases) plateaus around +30%**. Elasticity of substitution ~**0.25** (humans and AI stay complements).
- **3. Productivity-experience paradox** (Vella, Blincoe): 6-month study, 95 pro engineers. **84% reported productivity gains**, yet devs reporting worse experience on at least one dimension nearly **doubled (14% -> 27%)**. Flow state most vulnerable; productivity and DevEx decoupled over time.
- **4. AI where it matters** (Choudhuri et al., Microsoft): 860 devs. Want **"bounded delegation"** - AI on tedious assembly (docs, edge-case tests), NOT core logic/architecture. Four guardrails: explicit authority scoping, data provenance clarity, uncertainty signaling, least-privilege security.
- **5. Technical -> cognitive & intent debt** (Storey): AI **reduces technical debt** while quietly accelerating **cognitive debt** (eroded team understanding) and **intent debt** (unclear system goals/rationale). These now constrain safe development more than code quality does.
