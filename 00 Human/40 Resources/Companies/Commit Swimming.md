# Commit Swimming

## Overview
> Swim team management platform being evaluated for the [[WeAquatics]] swim team workstream. Founded 2015; positions as the #2 US swim team management platform (behind Team Unify).

## Company Details
> From the 2026-07-07 vendor demo (verify independently).
- **Scale:** 2M+ users, 3M+ workouts logged, 300+ clubs plus national federations (Sweden, Great Britain, Germany, Australia).
- **Product:** Meet management (Hy-Tek imports, filtered invites, per-meet messaging), Stripe billing with group-specific fees, workout builder + performance tracking, granular staff roles, family parent portal, hosted team websites.
- **Pricing:** Team Premium $2,490/yr (incl. website hosting, up to 3 team sites, 15 users/site; extra users cost more); Team Basic drops hosting. Onboarding ~3 weeks.
- **Gaps:** Paid/limited API (mostly manual CSV export today); no live Meet Mobile results integration; lessons module lacks complex instructor scheduling (improved module planned for summer).
