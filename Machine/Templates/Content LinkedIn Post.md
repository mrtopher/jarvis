## Angle
> One sentence: what is the single idea this post lands?

## Anchor (required - the real, first-person thing this post is built on)
> A client moment (anonymized, with permission), a number Chris actually measured, a mistake and its cost, a 20-year opinion, a decision made this week. A news peg is NOT an anchor. Never invent this - it must be real and sourced.

## Form (rotate - must differ from the last 2 posts)
> One of: first-person story / blunt opinion (no peg) / tactical how-to or artifact / short jab (<50 words) / teardown or contrarian / news reaction (needs a real anchor).

## Hook (first 2 lines)
> Stops the scroll. No throat-clearing. Earns the "see more" click.

## Body
> Short lines. One idea per line. Voice per VOICE.md (no em dashes, no "and"-starts, short and punchy). Vary length across posts - not always ~160 words.

## Close
> A takeaway, a question, OR a hard stop. Do not default to a rhetorical question, and do not reuse the previous post's close type.

## Hashtags
> 3-5 max, relevant.

## Cover image (optional - not every post; vary it)
> Prefer a real photo (whiteboard, redacted screenshot, phone snap) or no image, especially for short jabs / opinions. Only generate an illustration when it fits, and vary the style so consecutive posts are not a matched set. No infographics unless the piece calls for one.
