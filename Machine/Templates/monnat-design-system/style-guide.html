Visual language

Imagery & illustration

No stock photography anywhere on the site. Three modes cover every image slot: flat illustration, abstract gradient tiles, and a moody gradient backdrop.

A. Flat illustration. Front-facing, cropped at the chest, flat solid fills with no shading. A thin coral ring-and-crosshair line drawing is the only decorative flourish.

B. Gradient tiles. 135° diagonal duotone gradients stand in for project photography — see the four swatches in §Color.

C. Mood-gradient backdrop — near-black, purple-leaning radial blend for any section that needs text on a dark, non-photographic ground.

Iconography

Two treatments, kept in separate contexts — never mixed within a single section.

Outline / stroke

Feature list on the dark panel only. stroke-width: 1.4, no fill, color inherits (coral on charcoal).

Solid glyph

Services chips and social roundels. Solid white fill inside a filled coral or charcoal shape.

Use one of the brand gradient pairs (sky, amber, orchid, slate) or a new pair with the same "one warm + one cool anchor" logic.

Moody dark section backdrop

Abstract dark atmospheric background, near-black with a plum/purple undertone (#1C1622 base), soft radial color bloom in muted violet (#4A3960) upper-left and deep purple (#3A2B4D) lower-right, heavy dark vignette (~85-90% opacity), subtle grain, cinematic and minimal, no subject, no text — for use as a text-overlay backdrop.

Icon glyph

Single-color icon glyph, white, centered inside a solid coral #FF5252 rounded-square chip (60x60px, 8px corner radius) OR a solid charcoal #333333 circle (38px), simple geometric line or solid-fill style consistent with a minimal flat icon set, no gradients, no outlines beyond the shape itself.

Brand mark / favicon

Minimal wordmark lockup: bold geometric sans-serif (Montserrat 700) brand name in charcoal #333333, with a single coral #FF5252 period/dot as the only accent mark. No icon, no gradient, no drop shadow.
Guardrails

Do / don't

Do

  • Keep coral as the only saturated accent on any given screen.
  • Use blush #FFF8F8 — not pure white — for alternating section backgrounds.
  • Pair every H2 with a small coral eyebrow label above it.

Don't

  • Introduce photography — illustration and abstract gradient only.
  • Add a second accent color, even a "complementary" one.
  • Use pure grey for body text — keep muted-plum's warm bias.
