# /meeting-notes

Turn a meeting transcript or rough notes into a clean meeting note plus tasks and people updates. Transcripts can be pasted in or pulled from Fireflies AI (via MCP) — pass a meeting name, date, or Fireflies ID as an argument, or leave blank to pick from recent meetings.

Read and execute the workflow file at `Machine/Workflows/meeting-notes.md` — follow every step exactly as documented.

The user's arguments (if any) are: $ARGUMENTS
